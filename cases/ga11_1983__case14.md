## 11-86 Judicial Commission on Case 14

TE Leo Karlberg presented the report of the Commission to Adjudicate the Complaint of RE Truman B. Swain, Jr., et al. against the Presbytery of Eastern Carolina, i.e. that the complainants did not appear and therefore according to BCO 42-10 the case is judged to have been abandoned. The Commission had met several times, these meetings being duly announced. Adopted.

# 35-30 II.H — Voting at Presbytery Reserved Only for Those Giving Financially to the Presbytery

*The CCB found that a proposed overture conditioning presbytery voting on church financial contributions was not in conflict with the constitution, though a minority dissented.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 35th (2007)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 13-1, BCO 14-2  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict

*Source: [ga35_2007 minutes p.74 / PDF p.76](../markdown/ga35_2007.md#ga35-p74)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 13 from Session of Alexandria (VA) Presbyterian Church, Rejected by Potomac Presbytery ("Revise BCO 13-1 and BCO 14-2 to Require Church Giving to Higher Courts in Order to Vote in Higher Courts") was not in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. However, a minority report was submitted which was of the opinion that Overture 13 was, in fact, in conflict with the BCO.

**Key words:** BCO 13-1, BCO 14-2

**Inquiry from:** Session of Alexandria (VA) Presbyterian Church

**In the minutes:** [ga35_2007 minutes p.74 / PDF p.76](../markdown/ga35_2007.md#ga35-p74)

## Verbatim record

Overture 13 from Session of Alexandria (VA) Presbyterian Church, Rejected by Potomac Presbytery: "Revise BCO 13-1 and 14-2 to Require Church Giving to Higher Courts in Order to Vote in Higher Courts" In the opinion of the CCB Overture 13 is not in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. Adopted by the CCB

Minority Report: It is the opinion of this minority of the CCB that Overture 13 is, in fact, in conflict with the present provisions of the current BCO 14-1(4). The present language of BCO 14-1(4) clearly stipulates that the responsibility of a local church to support the whole work of the denomination is to be led by their conscience held captive to the Word of God. The language of the proposed amendment to BCO 14-2 requires a constitutionally mandated support of the denomination's Committees and Agencies. The net effect of the proposed amendment

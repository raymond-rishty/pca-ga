# 29-28 V — Role of a Pastor in a Search Committee for Associate or Assistant Pastor

*The CCB found the overture in conflict with the constitution, as teaching elders are not congregational members and may not serve on the congregation's pulpit committee.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 29th (2001)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 20-2, BCO 20  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga29_2001 minutes p.139 / PDF p.141](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p141)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 3 from Susquehanna Valley Presbytery ("Amend BCO 20-2 to Include Pastor in Search Committee for Assistant or Associate Pastor") was in conflict with the other parts of the Constitution. TEs are not members of the local congregation and therefore cannot be a member of the congregation's pulpit committee. The proposed mandate of the overture that they be so would have interfered with the right of the congregation to determine those who will rule over them, a privilege which undergirds BCO 20.

**Key words:** BCO 20-2, BCO 20

**Inquiry from:** Susquehanna Valley Presbytery

## Verbatim record

Overture 3 from Susquehanna Valley Presbytery: Amend BCO 20-2 to Include Pastor in Search Committee for Assistant or Associate Pastor It is the opinion of the CCB that Overture #3 is in conflict with the other parts of the Constitution. Teaching Elders are not members of the local congregation and therefore cannot be a member of the congregation's pulpit committee. The proposed mandate of the overture that they be so would interfere with the right of the congregation to determine those who will rule over them, a privilege which undergirds BCO 20. Having said that, the Committee does recognize the wisdom of consulting with the senior pastor in the selection of an Assistant or Associate Pastor. Adopted by CCB

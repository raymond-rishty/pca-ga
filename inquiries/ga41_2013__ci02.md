# App. O II.E — Defining the Terms Used in BCO 42

*An overture adding a definitions section to BCO 42 was possibly in conflict with BCO 42-3 if the new definitions excluded grounds of appeal already recognized there.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 41st (2013)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 42-13, BCO 42-3  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga41_2013 minutes p.362 / PDF p.364](../markdown/ga41_2013.md#ga41-p362)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 5 from Suncoast Florida Presbytery ("Amend BCO 42 by Adding 42-13 to Define Terms Used in Chapter 42") may have been in conflict with BCO 42-3 if the proposed BCO 42-13.a and b were taken to exclude possible grounds of appeal listed there.

**Key words:** BCO 42-13, BCO 42-3

**Inquiry from:** Suncoast Florida Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga41_2013 minutes p.362 / PDF p.364](../markdown/ga41_2013.md#ga41-p362)

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 5 from Suncoast Florida Presbytery: "Amend BCO 42 by Adding 42-13 to Define Terms Used in Chapter 42" In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 5 may be in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. The overture may conflict with BCO 42-3 if the proposed 42-13.a and b are taken to exclude possible grounds of appeal listed there. Adopted by the CCB

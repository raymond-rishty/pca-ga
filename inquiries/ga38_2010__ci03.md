# 38-34 II.D — Prohibiting Assistants to Deacons

*CCB found an overture prohibiting commissioned or installed assistants to deacons in conflict with the Constitution on the same grounds as the deaconess overture.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 38th (2010)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 9-7  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga38_2010 minutes p.268 / PDF p.270](../markdown/ga38_2010.md#ga38-p270)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 9 from Eastern Carolina Presbytery ("Revise BCO 9-7 to Prohibit Assistants to the Deacons from Being Commissioned or Installed as Office Bearers") was in conflict with the Constitution on the same grounds as Overture 2.

**Key words:** BCO 9-7

**Inquiry from:** Eastern Carolina Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga38_2010 minutes p.268 / PDF p.270](../markdown/ga38_2010.md#ga38-p270)

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 9 from Eastern Carolina Presbytery: “Revise BCO 9-7 to Prohibit Assistants to the Deacons from Being Commissioned or Installed as Office Bearers"

As the Amendment proposed by Overture 9 is identical to that proposed by Overture 2, in the opinion of the CCB, Overture 9 is contrary to the Constitution on the same grounds. Adopted by the CCB

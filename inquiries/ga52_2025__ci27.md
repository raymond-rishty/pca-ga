# II.AA — Amend BCO 35-9 to Require Recording All Parts of a Trial

*The CCB found an overture requiring that all parts of a trial be recorded under BCO 35-9 to be not in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 35-9  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 7-0-1

*Source: [ga52_2025 lines 14956–14960](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p258)*

---

## Verbatim record

Presbytery. It was timely because it was a BCO 43-1 filing (60 days to the  
original court) and not a BCO 43-3 filing (30 days to the higher court). The  
main error that was alleged is as follows.

> "This act [of Presbytery sustaining the Svendsen Complaint and annulling the Session decision] infringes upon the jurisdiction of VPC Session's duties and charge in maintaining the spiritual governing of the church (BCO 12- 5.e; 11-2; 3-2; 4-3; 39-3)."... "The Session is in fact charged with overseeing the whole service, with all of its elements. That is from the Call to the Benediction (BCO 12.5.e)."

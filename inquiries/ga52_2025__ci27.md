# II.Y — Amend BCO 30-4 to Require a New Case with Process for Elevation of Indefinite Suspension to Excommunication

*The CCB found an overture requiring a new judicial case and process before indefinite suspension may be elevated to excommunication to be in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 30-4, BCO 32-6, BCO 33-2, BCO 33-3, BCO 34-4  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.257 / PDF p.259](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p259)*

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 38 from the Southeast Alabama Presbytery: “Amend BCO 30-4 to Require a New Case with Process for Elevation of Indefinite Suspension to Excommunication"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 38 is in conflict with the Constitution in  
that BCO 30-4 refers to the infliction of an initial censure of excommunication  
after the judgment of a court and is not intended to offer a definition of  
excommunication per se, therefore this overture is incompatible with those

## Minutes transcript

Reference 3 -- from Southern Florida Presbytery re Divestiture: Question: Is the Presbytery required to divest Teaching Elders without call, "for a prolonged period, not to exceed three years," (BCO 13-2) and is the process of divesting a minister of the gospel in BCO 34-10 in conflict with BCO 13-2. Response: BCO 34-10 and BCO 13-2 are not in conflict, i.e., the Presbytery is not obligated to divest every minister who has been without call for three years, but the Presbytery is required to inquire into the matter and then use its discretion. Adopted by CCB

# 29-28 III.6 — Right of Complaint Against a Court's Actions

*The CCB advised that a complainant may not go to a higher court until the lower court has acted on the complaint pursuant to BCO 43-2 and 43-3.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 29th (2001)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 43-2, BCO 43-3, BCO 43-1  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga29_2001 lines 5934–5944](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p136)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from Philadelphia Presbytery by stating that a person may not make a complaint to a higher court until after the lower court has acted on the complaint in accordance with BCO 43-2 and 43-3. The CCB noted the right of individuals under BCO 43-1 to complain against actions taken during a trial so long as the procedures of BCO 43-2 and 43-3 are followed.

**Key words:** BCO 43-2, BCO 43-3, BCO 43-1

**Inquiry from:** Philadelphia Presbytery

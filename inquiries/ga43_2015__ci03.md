# App. O IV.A — Presbytery approval when a TE labors outside the bounds

*The CCB advised Southeast Alabama Presbytery that a teaching elder approved to labor outside its bounds requires the host presbytery's concurrence, though not necessarily before the home presbytery grants its approval.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 43rd (2015)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 13-2  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga43_2015 minutes pp. 372–373 / PDF pp. 374–375](../markdown/ga43_2015.md#ga43-p374)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from Southeast Alabama Presbytery concerning BCO 13-2. It said the teaching elder must receive the concurrence of the presbytery in whose bounds he will labor, but that concurrence need not precede the home presbytery's approval.

**Key words:** BCO 13-2

**Inquiry from:** Southeast Alabama Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga43_2015 minutes pp.372–373 / PDF pp.374–375](../markdown/ga43_2015.md#ga43-p374)

## Verbatim record

**A.** The Presbytery of Southeast Alabama submitted the following nonjudicial reference for the CCB’s consideration:

“Concerning _Book of Church Order_ 13-2, the Presbytery of Southeast
Alabama seeks guidance on the question—When ate is approved to
labor outside the bounds of Presbytery, is it required to have received

<a id="ga43-p373"></a>
<!-- PAGE ga=43 pdf_page=375 printed_page=373 -->

the approval of the other presbytery in whose bounds he would labor,
prior our presbytery granting approval for said TE to move onto the
fields and begin his ministry?”

The CCB responded as follows:

It is the opinion of the CCB that a TE is required to receive approval
of the presbytery in whose bounds he is laboring, but it is not
necessarily required prior to the requesting presbytery granting its
approval for the said TE to move onto the field and begin his
ministry. What is clear, per _BCO_ 13-2, is that the said TE cannot
move onto the field regardless of approval by the presbytery of
which he is member, unless the presbytery in whose bounds he
would labor concurs. _Adopted by the CCB_

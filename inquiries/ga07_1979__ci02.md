## Minutes transcript

3. That answer 1 to the Rev. Laurie Vidal concerning election of a female communing member as a trustee of a church be approved, and that he refer this request to Presbytery for further advice. The Committee answered that a trustee is not ordained and is not an officer of the church, and therefore election of a female communing member on the roll of the church as a trustee does not contravene the BCO 26-6 and 26-7.

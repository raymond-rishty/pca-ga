# 7-41 III, 3 — Eligibility of a female communing member to serve as church trustee

*The CCB advised that electing a female communing member as a church trustee does not violate BCO 26-6 or 26-7, because trustees are not ordained officers.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 7th (1979)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 26-6, BCO 26-7  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga07_1979 minutes p.101 / PDF p.103](../markdown/ga07_1979.md#ga07-p103)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

That answer 1 to the Rev. Laurie Vidal concerning election of a female communing member as a trustee of a church be approved, and that he refer this request to Presbytery for further advice. The Committee answered that a trustee is not ordained and is not an officer of the church, and therefore election of a female communing member on the roll of the church as a trustee does not contravene the BCO 26-6 and 26-7.

**Key words:** BCO 26-6, BCO 26-7

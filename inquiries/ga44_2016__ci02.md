# App. O, section II.G II.G — Requirements for Reporting and Reviewing Minutes of a Mission Church

*The CCB found the overture in conflict with BCO 5-3.a because an evangelist, unlike governing bodies, has no minutes to submit under the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 44th (2016)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 5-3, BCO 5-3.a  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga44_2016 lines 16812–16821](../markdown/ga44_2016.md#ga44-p348)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 38 from Pacific Northwest Presbytery ("Amend BCO 5-3 to Add an Explicit Requirement for Reporting and Reviewing Minutes of a Mission Church Temporary System of Government") was in conflict with the Constitution. Overture 38, as it was written, conflicted with BCO 5-3.a, in that an evangelist would not have minutes to submit as opposed to the governing bodies stipulated in items b and c of BCO 5-3. The BCO does not require an evangelist to submit minutes.

**Key words:** BCO 5-3, BCO 5-3.a

**Inquiry from:** Pacific Northwest Presbytery

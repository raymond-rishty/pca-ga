# II.J — 14-1.11, and 14-1.12 to Allow Non-ordained Persons to serve on Committees and Boards (Version 2)

*A Southern New England Presbytery version of the overture to allow non-ordained persons on GA committees and boards was found in conflict and adopted by the CCB.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 14-1.10, BCO 14-1.11, BCO 14-1.12, RAO 5-2, RAO 4-21.c.6, RAO 4-5  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga47_2019 minutes p.400 / PDF p.402](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p400)*

---

## Verbatim record

J.** Overture 14 from Southern New England Presbytery: “Amend BCO 14-1.10, 14-1.11, and 14-1.12 to Allow Non-ordained Persons to serve on Committees and Boards" (Version 2) In the opinion of the CCB, Overtures 8, 14, 19, 21, and 32 are in conflict with RAO 5-2 as the Overtures establish additional requirements for the Chairman. It should also be noted that RAO 4- 21.c.6 does not explicitly mention non-ordained members. Additionally, RAO 4-5 would need to be changed editorially to properly reference the newly numbered sections in BCO 14-1 upon passage and adoption of the Overtures. Adopted by the CCB

# II.D — Amend BCO 34-1 Regarding Advisors and Original Jurisdiction in Process Against a TE

*The CCB found the proposed amendment to BCO 34-1 regarding advisors and original jurisdiction in TE disciplinary process not in conflict with the Constitution.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 34-1  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 7-0-1

*Source: [ga52_2025 lines 14731–14736](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p253)*

---

## Verbatim record

Consider the extensive and collaborative interactions between the accused and the 15-1 Commission via e-mail regarding the contents and language of the proposed Confession. The 15-1 Commission was appointed by NP in February 2023. Appellant submitted a written response to the allegations in March 2023, in which he wrote, "The specifics of both allegations are true...". Appellant provided background information regarding his and his family's interaction with the person bringing the allegations. In May 2023, the 15-1 Commission drafted a proposed statement of facts and confession of guilt and provided it to the Accused. Appellant and the Chairman of the 15-1 Commission exchanged a series of e-mails regarding the statement and confession. On June 21, 2023, Appellant met with the 15-1 Commission to review the statement, and Appellant objected to parts of the proposed

<!-- PAGE ga=52 pdf_page=878 printed_page=876 -->

statement. The 15-1 Commission agreed to revise the statement and confession in response to Appellant's requests. On June 29, 2023, the 15-1 Commission approved changes to the statement and confession from its June 21 meeting with Appellant. The 15-1 Commission forwarded the changes to Appellant and offered to permit him to submit “anything else you would like...". On July 7, the 15-1 Commission Chairman sent Appellant a “finalized” statement and confession. On July 14, Appellant requested that the 15-1 Commission consider additions to the statement and confession. On July 18, the 15-1 Commission responded, indicating reasons it would not agree to Appellant's latest proposed revisions. On July 20, Appellant requested the 15-1 Commission “take into account” a February 13, 2023 letter from Appellant and statements from two Teaching Elders in NP. The 15-1 Commission agreed to this request. With these additions for the 15-1 Commission's consideration, Appellant e-mailed the 15-1 Commission indicating his intention to sign the statement and confession. On July 26, Appellant signed the statement of facts and confession of guilt. On August 2, the 15-1 Commission approved the statement and confession as signed. On August 8, the 15-1 Commission approved its censure.

# II.R — 14-1.11, and 14-1.12 to Allow Non-ordained Persons to Serve on Committees and Boards (Version 4)

*This overture to allow non-ordained persons to serve on GA committees and boards was found by the CCB to be in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 14-1.10, BCO 14-1.11, BCO 14-1.12, RAO 5-2, RAO 4-21.c.6, RAO 4-5  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga47_2019 minutes p.401 / PDF p.403](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p403)*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overtures 8, 14, 19, 21, and 32 are in conflict with RAO 5-2 as the Overtures establish additional requirements for the Chairman. It should also be noted that RAO 4- 21.c.6 does not explicitly mention non-ordained members. Additionally, RAO 4-5 would need to be changed editorially to properly reference the newly numbered sections in BCO 14-1 upon passage and adoption of the Overtures. Adopted by the CCB

## Minutes transcript

When a person "resigns" from membership in a church or presbytery, is this not
tantamount to renouncing the jurisdiction of the body and is there a constitutional bar to
a court accepting this "resignation?"

131

<a id="ga25-p134"></a>
<!-- PAGE ga=25 pdf_page=134 printed_page=132 -->

Response:
It is the advice of the CCB that there is no constitutional bar that would prevent
a court from accepting the "resignation" and removing the person's name from the roll.

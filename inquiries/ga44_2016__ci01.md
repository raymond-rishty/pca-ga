# App. O II.B — General Assembly Directions Regarding SJC Decisions

*The CCB found an overture allowing the Assembly to direct SJC judicial reasoning was in conflict with the Constitution, because a commission concludes business as the court itself under BCO 15.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 44th (2016)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 15-5.a, RAO 17-1, BCO 15, BCO 15-4  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga44_2016 minutes p.346 / PDF p.348](../markdown/ga44_2016.md#ga44-p346)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 5 from Pittsburgh Presbytery ("Amend BCO 15-5.a and RAO 17-1, Paragraph 4, to Allow the General Assembly to Give Directions to the SJC in Judicial Decisions and Reasoning and Opinions") was in conflict with the Constitution. Following BCO 15, when a commission concludes the business referred to it, it is acting as the court of which it is a commission. BCO 15-4 specifies the business which General Assembly refers to the SJC, which it commissions the SJC to conclude.

**Key words:** BCO 15-5.a, RAO 17-1, BCO 15, BCO 15-4

**Inquiry from:** Pittsburgh Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga44_2016 minutes p.346 / PDF p.348](../markdown/ga44_2016.md#ga44-p346)

the review of its minutes exceptions are taken with respect to that

governed by the judicial procedures of the General Assembly."

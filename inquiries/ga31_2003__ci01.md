# 31-56 VI — Recording Doctrinal Exceptions; Not Setting Doctrinal Precedent

*The CCB found the subscription-related portion of a tri-presbytery overture in conflict with the Constitution, as RAO 16-10.b makes clear that exceptions of substance are reported for more than merely informational purposes.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 31st (2003)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 14-3, RAO 16-3, RAO 14-8, RAO 16-8, RAO 14-10.b, RAO 16-10.b  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga31_2003 lines 3023–3023](../markdown/ga31_2003.md#ga31-p162)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, the second half of Overture 5 from Illiana Presbytery, Eastern Canada Presbytery and Blue Ridge Presbytery ("Amend RAO 14-3 [now 16-3], RAO 14-8 [now 16-8] Regarding Subscription") was in conflict with other parts of the constitution. The discussion of responses in RAO 14-10.b [now 16-10.b] makes it clear that exceptions of substance are reported for more than just informational purposes in that they cannot be ignored by the presbytery to whose records the exception is taken.

**Key words:** RAO 14-3, RAO 16-3, RAO 14-8, RAO 16-8, RAO 14-10.b, RAO 16-10.b

**Inquiry from:** Illiana Presbytery, Eastern Canada Presbytery and Blue Ridge Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga31_2003 p.160](../markdown/ga31_2003.md#ga31-p162)

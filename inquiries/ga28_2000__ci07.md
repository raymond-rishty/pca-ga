*The CCB found no technical constitutional conflict but raised concerns about the nature of ordination and inconsistency between restoration after deposition versus divestiture.*

## Verbatim record

OVERTURE 13 – From Susquehanna Valley Presbytery, “Re-ordination of Former PCA Ministers." Response – It is the opinion of the Committee that Overture 13 as presented is not in technical conflict with any other provisions of the Constitution. However, the Committee would point out that BCO 34-10 does not mandate divestiture in every case ("...it shall be the duty of the Presbytery...to inquire...and, if necessary, to institute judicial proceedings....If it shall appear that his neglect proceeds only from his lack of acceptance to the church, Presbytery may... divest him of his office...."). The Committee is also concerned that the language proposed for the new BCO 21-5 raises questions about the nature and importance of the call to the ministry ("... simply a lack of call...."). Finally, the Committee notes the apparent inconsistency in the handling of one who has been removed from office by discipline (deposition - BCO 36-7) who would be restored as per BCO 37-5 and one who is divested without censure (BCO 34-10) who could be restored only after reexamination and re-ordination. Perhaps a proposed amendment clarifying the relationship between divestiture and deposition would be helpful.

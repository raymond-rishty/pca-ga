# IV. Non-Judicial Reference 1 — Whether one Presbytery may take action toward the involuntary dissociation of another Presbytery from the PCA

*The CCB concluded that no procedure exists in the BCO or RAO by which one presbytery may involuntarily dissociate another from the PCA.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 50th (2023)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 14-6.e, RAO 8-2.B.2  ·  **Disposition:** No such procedure exists; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga50_2023 minutes p.459 / PDF p.462](../markdown/ga50_2023.md#ga50-p462)*

---

## Verbatim record

IV. Non-Judicial References The CCB received a request from Grace Presbytery regarding the possibility of one Presbytery taking action toward the dissociation of another Presbytery. The CCB chairman ruled that this request was a nonjudicial reference. The CCB acceded to the request (RAO 8-2.B.2) by a vote of 5-1-2.

The CCB responds to the first question posed in the non-judicial reference as follows: As BCO 14-6.e, which enumerates the powers of the General Assembly in regard to the Presbyteries, does not empower the General Assembly to involuntarily dissociate a Presbytery from the PCA, the CCB advises Grace Presbytery that no such procedure exists.

The CCB responds to the second and third questions posed in the Non- Judicial Reference by reference to its answer to the first. Adopted 8-0-0

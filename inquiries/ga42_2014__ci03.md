# App. O, section IV.A IV.A — Defining the Term "Labor"

*The CCB advised that "labor" for teaching elders means needful ministry for Gospel dissemination, with each presbytery determining whether such labor is allowable within its bounds.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 42nd (2014)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 13-2, BCO 8-4, BCO 8-7  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga42_2014 lines 16437–16449](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p344)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from Evangel Presbytery regarding BCO 13-2 and the scope of the word "labor" by stating that the BCO speaks of "labor" for TEs as ministry in "needful work" for "disseminating the Gospel for the edification of the Church" (BCO 8-4), and the presbytery determines whether such labor is needful and allowable for a TE in its bounds (BCO 8-7).

**Key words:** BCO 13-2, BCO 8-4, BCO 8-7

**Inquiry from:** Evangel Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga42_2014 p.344](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p344)

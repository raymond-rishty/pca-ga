# II.M — Amend BCO 57-5 to Revise the Membership Vows

*Proposed revision of BCO 57-5 membership vows was found not in conflict with the BCO, though the CCB urged careful attention to Westminster Standards.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 57-5, Westminster Standards  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict with BCO; CCB advises careful attention to theological questions raised in light of the Westminster Standards; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 lines 14794–14801](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p254)*

## Verbatim record

Appellant's contention is that after he agreed to a BCO 38-1 written Confession and the infliction of a censure by JC, he should have been permitted to withdraw his Confession. Again, the Appellant cites Wilbourne for the principle that an accused may make such a withdrawal. But in Wilbourne the issue was whether Wilbourne had the right to withdraw his confession after the SJC had annulled the censure by sustaining his complaint. There is no principle that permits an accused to unilaterally withdraw a mutually agreed written Confession after censure has been announced. To allow Appellant to do so could well change the nature of BCO 38-1; it could permit a provision designed to allow the confession of a person convinced of his guilt to metamorphose into a plea-bargain process in which an accused could withdraw his confession because he disagreed with the censure inflicted.

The Presbytery's judgment and censure are affirmed.

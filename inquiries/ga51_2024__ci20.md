# II.T — Amend BCO 31-2 to Expand Who May Assist in an Investigation

*The CCB found a proposed amendment expanding who may assist in a judicial investigation under BCO 31-2 not in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 51st (2024)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 31-2  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 7-1-0

*Source: [ga51_2024 lines 10277–10282](../markdown/ga51_2024.md#ga51-p228)*

---

## Verbatim record

<!-- PAGE ga=51 pdf_page=587 printed_page=584 -->

- **a.** An “exposition of the Word” by a woman shall not take the place of the ordinary sermon in public worship services.

- **b.** Only qualified men should preach to God's people during public worship services. We do not believe that the principle that “a woman can do whatever an non-ordained man can do" is to be applied to the preaching in public worship services (BCO 4-4, 8-5; WCF 21.5; WLC 156, 158).

# II.L — Amend BCO 7 to Codify the Biblical Standard for Church Officers as Related to Self-Description by Biblical Sins

*The CCB found that amending BCO 7 to prohibit church officers from self-describing by biblical sins (e.g., 'gay Christian') is not in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 50th (2023)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 7  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

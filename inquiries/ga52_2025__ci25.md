# II.Y — Amend BCO 30-4 to Require a New Case with Process for Elevation of Indefinite Suspension to Excommunication

*The CCB found an overture requiring a new judicial case and process before indefinite suspension may be elevated to excommunication to be in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 30-4, BCO 32-6, BCO 33-2, BCO 33-3, BCO 34-4  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

## Verbatim record

04/26/24 Panel adopted a proposed decision, filed it with SJC, and emailed it to the Parties.

### II. STATEMENT OF THE ISSUES

1. As a matter of law (BCO 39-3.4) did the Session have constitutional authority to instruct the minister to close worship with benedictions instead of doxologies?

2. In exercising that authority, was there clear error in the Session's discretion and judgment in deciding to close worship in that manner? (BCO 39.3.3)

### III. JUDGMENT

### 1. Yes

2. No. Therefore, the Complaint is sustained. Presbytery's October 3, 2023 action in sustaining the Svendsen Complaint is annulled. This returns the matter to where it stood after Presbytery sustained the

Martinez Complaint on May 2, 2023, meaning the Session's July 11, 2022 decision regarding benedictions and doxologies has not been annulled.

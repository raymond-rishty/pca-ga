# II.W — Amend RAO 16 to Require Reporting on Session and Diaconate Membership and Duties

*The CCB found an overture amending RAO 16 to mandate statistical reporting on session and diaconate membership and duties to be in conflict with existing BCO provisions.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 16, BCO 9-2  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.257 / PDF p.259](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p259)*

---

## Verbatim record

W.** Overture 34 from the Savannah River Presbytery: “Amend RAO 16 to Require Reporting on Session and Diaconate Membership and Duties"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 34 is in conflict with the Constitution in that if a church does not have Deacons, BCO 9-2 requires that the duties of the office “shall devolve upon the ruling elders" of that church but the amendment as presently written suggests that the duties of the diaconate may devolve upon other persons or entities other than the ruling elders. Adopted

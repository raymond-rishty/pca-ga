# App. O, section II.F II.F — General Assembly Review and Vote on SJC Decisions

*The CCB found an overture allowing the Assembly to vote on SJC decisions was in conflict with the Constitution and also assigned tasks beyond the CCB's own purview under RAO 8.2.b.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 42nd (2014)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 15-5.a, BCO 15-5.b, RAO 8.2.b  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga42_2014 lines 16382–16389](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p343)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 11 from Calvary Presbytery ("Amend BCO 15-5.a and 15-5.b and Direct CCB to Draft Proposed Amendments to RAO and OMSJC") was in conflict with other parts of the Constitution, on the same grounds given for Overture 13. In addition, a portion of the overture assigned tasks to the CCB which go beyond the purview of the CCB (RAO 8.2.b).

**Key words:** BCO 15-5.a, BCO 15-5.b, RAO 8.2.b

**Inquiry from:** Calvary Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga42_2014 p.343](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p343)

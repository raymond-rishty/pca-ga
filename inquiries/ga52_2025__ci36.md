# Supplemental II.1 — Whether the AC Committee of Commissioners may make a substitute motion nominating a Stated Clerk if the AC did not bring a nomination

*The CCB ruled that the Administrative Committee's Committee of Commissioners may not make a substitute nomination for Stated Clerk when the matter does not arise from AC recommendations.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 14-6.d, RAO 14-6.h, RAO 14-6.k  ·  **Disposition:** No — AC CoC may not make a substitute nomination for Stated Clerk if the matter does not arise in AC recommendations; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.262 / PDF p.264](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p264)*

## Verbatim record

1. If the AC didn't make a nomination for Stated Clerk, could the AC CoC make a substitute motion along the lines of, “That Mr. X be nominated for Stated Clerk." I.e., can they make a substitute for a motion that (in our mind) never existed?

2. Further if the AC didn't make a nomination for Stated Clerk, would “additional nominations” from the floor (RAO 3-4) be allowed?

The CCB adopted the following responses to the requests from the Stated Clerk:

# II.E — Amend BCO 31-10 for Non-censure Suspension Option During Investigation

*The CCB found that a proposed overture to add a non-censure suspension option during investigations (BCO 31-10) conflicts with the BCO as it currently stands.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 50th (2023)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 31-10, BCO 13-1, BCO 13-2, BCO 43, BCO 11-4  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga50_2023 minutes p.455 / PDF p.458](../markdown/ga50_2023.md#ga50-p458)*

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 8 from Arizona Presbytery: “Amend BCO 31-10 for Noncensure Suspension Option During Investigation"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 8 is in conflict with BCO 13-1,  
2; 43 where the proposed lines 40-42: “Even in extraordinary  
circumstances, the accused shall have no greater access to information  
from the court or right to speak before the court than his accuser(s)"  
limit the rights of a member of Presbytery.

Additionally, the phrase “in coordination with lower courts” (line 28) is in conflict with original jurisdiction as expressed in BCO 11-4. Adopted 8-0-0

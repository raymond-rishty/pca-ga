# II.X — Amend BCO 12-3 so that a Session May Elect One of Its Members as Moderator in Judicial Cases

*The CCB found an overture allowing a session to elect one of its own members as moderator in judicial cases to be not in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 12-3  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.257 / PDF p.259](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p259)*

---

## Verbatim record

X.** Overture 37 from the Southeast Alabama Presbytery: “Amend BCO 12-3 so that a Session May Elect One of Its Members as Moderator in Judicial Cases"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 37 is not in conflict with the Constitution. Adopted

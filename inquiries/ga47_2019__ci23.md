# III.B — Is a member of a General Assembly permanent committee who has served a full term eligible for consecutive service on a different permanent Committee or Agency Board without taking a sabbatical year?

*The CCB advised that a GA permanent committee member who completed a full term is not eligible for consecutive service on a different committee or agency board without a sabbatical year.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 8-2.a.1, BCO 14-1.12  ·  **Disposition:** No (not eligible); Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga47_2019 minutes p.403 / PDF p.405](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p405)*

B.** Is a member of a General Assembly permanent Committee who has served a full term, eligible for consecutive service on a different permanent Committee or Agency Board without taking a sabbatical year? No. Adopted by the CCB

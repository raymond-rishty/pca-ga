# Supplemental II.2 — Whether additional nominations from the floor (RAO 3-4) would be allowed if the AC did not bring a Stated Clerk nominee

*The CCB ruled that floor nominations are permitted under RAO 3-4 even if the Administrative Committee does not bring a Stated Clerk nominee.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 3-4, RAO 4-9, RAO 8-4.i, RAO 13, BCO 14-1.14  ·  **Disposition:** Yes — floor nominations are allowed; RAO 3-4 second sentence provides GA right to select Stated Clerk regardless of AC nominee; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.262 / PDF p.264](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p264)*

A Committee of Commissioners may not take up new business itself, except a recommendation to commend the committee or staff (RAO 14-6.k.). This precludes the Committee of Commissioners for the Administrative Committee from itself recommending the nomination of a Stated Clerk if the matter of a Stated Clerk does not arise in recommendations of the Administrative Committee. Adopted 8-0-0

2. In the opinion of the CCB, yes. The RAO does not expressly contemplate a set of facts where the Administrative Committee does not bring a nominee for the office of Stated Clerk. RAO 3-4 states: "The Administrative Committee of General Assembly has the responsibility of nominating to the General Assembly the person to fill the office of the Stated Clerk" (emphasis added). Further, RAO 4- 9 states: “The Administrative Committee shall nominate annually a Stated Clerk for election by the General Assembly" (emphasis added). RAO 3-4 and RAO 4-9 require the Administrative Committee to bring a nominee. The second sentence of RAO 3-4 simply assumes the Committee is bringing a nominee and provides an additional method for presentation of nominees.

In the event that the Administrative Committee declines to fulfill its duty under RAO 4-9, the second sentence of RAO 3-4 should be interpreted as acknowledging the General Assembly's right to select a Stated Clerk of its own choosing, regardless of the nominee (or lack of nominee) put forward by the Administrative Committee. A contrary reading would result in the Assembly having no mechanism under the Rules (without suspension thereof) to elect a Stated Clerk when the Administrative Committee does not proffer a nominee. The phrase “additional nominations” has been read in an analogous way regarding floor nominations when the Nominating Committee does not proffer a nominee for any position (RAO 8-4.i.).

Further, absent a recommendation from the Administrative Committee, the election of a Stated Clerk will not come before the Assembly in the ordinary course, and the election may only be taken up as new business under RAO 13.

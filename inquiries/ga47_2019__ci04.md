# II.D — Amend BCO 23-1 on Procedures for Dissolving Various Pastoral Relations

*The CCB found an overture from Central Indiana Presbytery amending BCO 23-1 on procedures for dissolving pastoral relations was not in conflict with the Constitution.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 23-1, BCO 20-3, BCO 20-4  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga47_2019 minutes p.398 / PDF p.400](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p400)*

---

## Verbatim record

D.** Overture 5 from Central Indiana Presbytery: “Amend BCO 23-1 on Procedures for Dissolving Various Pastoral Relations"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 5 is not in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. CCB notes the following constitutional consequences: It removes the explicit requirement for a congregational meeting when the minister resigns. It removes the reference that the meeting is "called and conducted in the same manner as the call of the pastor" (BCO 20-3, 4) with requirements regarding who presides, how votes are counted, the method of voting, etc. Adopted by the CCB

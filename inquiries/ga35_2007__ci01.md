# 35-30 II.B — Modes of Baptism

*A Presbytery of New Jersey overture to alter BCO 56-4.d was in conflict with WCF 28.3, which permits multiple modes of baptism, as the proposed language would allow only sprinkling.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 35th (2007)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 56-4.d, WCF 28.3  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga35_2007 minutes p.71 / PDF p.73](../markdown/ga35_2007.md#ga35-p73)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 2 from the Presbytery of New Jersey ("Delete 'or' and substitute 'the' in BCO 56-4.d") was in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. The proposed language was in conflict with WCF 28.3, which permits more than one mode of baptism, and would in effect have dictated that the only permissible form of baptism is sprinkling and washing with water.

**Key words:** BCO 56-4.d, WCF 28.3

**Inquiry from:** Presbytery of New Jersey

**In the minutes:** [ga35_2007 minutes p.71 / PDF p.73](../markdown/ga35_2007.md#ga35-p73)

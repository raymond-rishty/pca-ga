## Minutes transcript

With respect to a communication from Rev. Donald Dunkerley concerning the oversight of a church without a session but with at least one ruling elder, the Committee would direct attention to Paragraph 12-2 of the Book of Church Order beginning with the word "secondly" and continuing through the end of the paragraph which gives church courts latitude to establish rules as needed agreeable with Scripture and doctrines which are not in violation of the Constitution of the Church.

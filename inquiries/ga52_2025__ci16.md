# II.N — 24-3 and 25-1 to Clarify 'Regular Standing' re Minimum Voting Age

*Proposed amendment clarifying 'regular standing' and minimum voting age in BCO 20-3, 24-3, and 25-1 was found in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 20-3, BCO 24-3, BCO 25-1, BCO 6-4, BCO 25-2, BCO 25-3, Preliminary Principle 6  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.254 / PDF p.256](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p254)*

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 22 from the Pacific Northwest Presbytery: “Amend BCO 20-3, 24-3 and 25-1 to clarify ‘Regular Standing’ re minimum voting age"

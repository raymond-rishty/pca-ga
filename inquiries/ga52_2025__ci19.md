# II.Q — Amend BCO 46-4 to Add a Class of Associate Membership for Honorably Retired TEs

*Proposed amendment to BCO 46-4 creating associate membership for honorably retired TEs was found in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 46-4, BCO 13-1, BCO 13-2, Preliminary Principle 2  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.255 / PDF p.257](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p257)*

---

## Verbatim record

Q.** Overture 25 from the Northwest Georgia Presbytery: “Amend BCO 46-4 to Add a Class of Associate Membership for Honorably Retired TEs"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 25 is in conflict with BCO 13-1 and 13- 2 in that it applies permissive language to a Teaching Elder holding membership in his Presbytery; further, it creates a constitutional conflict by using mandatory language requiring all Honorably Retired Teaching Elders to hold associate membership in a local congregation. To the extent the language of the proposed amendment is deemed to apply to existing Honorably Retired Teaching Elders, it appears to be in conflict with Preliminary Principle 2. Finally, the Overture uses the phrase “communicant fellowship” which is a category undefined by our Constitution. Adopted

# 17-82 III — Certification of presbytery dismissal by committee chairman's letter

*A letter from a presbytery committee chairman is insufficient evidence of dismissal; BCO 46-6 requires a certificate bearing the stated clerk's signature per BCO 10-4.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 17th (1989)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 46-6, BCO 10-4  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga17_1989 lines 4360–4360](../markdown/ga17_1989.md#ga17-p153)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

, 6. That the advice of the Committee on Judicial Business regarding the communication from Review and Control Committee be ratified as amended. The question was: 'Would it be proper to accept as evidence the letter of a chairman of a presbytery committee stating that a dismissal had been granted?' ANSWER: No. BCO 46-6 requires that the presbytery issue a certificate when dismissing a minister, licentiate, or a candidate. According to BCO 10-4, the official certification of an action of a court is that which is issued over the signature of the stated clerk. Adopted.

## Minutes transcript

2. Received a constitutional inquiry asking CCB to give advice on the constitutionality of the Committee of Commissioners recommendation #15. The question posed for CCB was as follows, Is it constitutional for the General Assembly to adopt the following recommendation:

That the General Assembly mandate coverage in the PCA health plans for all active ministers and church lay employees who are current residents of the United States and who are not covered under a spouse's group health plan, and that the Presbyteries be responsible for implementing this mandate.

The CCB answered:

No. It is the opinion of the CCB that in the absence of any evidence from BCO 14, the Westminster Confession of Faith and Catechisms, or Scripture as to the right of the General Assembly to make such a requirement; the General Assembly may not mandate coverage in the PCA health plan. In addition, BCO 14-1.4 states:

"It is the responsibility of every member and member congregation to support the whole work of the denomination as they be led in their conscience held captive to the Word of God."(Emphasis added.)

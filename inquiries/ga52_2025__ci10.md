Nonetheless, we understand Appellant was very concerned that the Commission did not examine the 2020 SC investigation. We, therefore, offer the following observations. Allegations regarding Appellant's conduct were brought to the SC in 2019. The Record indicates that members of SC met with Appellant in 2019 and 2020 regarding those allegations. A four member “Ad Hoc Committee" of SC deliberated concerning a July 2020 document that had been filed with SC regarding Appellant's conduct. The Ad Hoc Committee concluded in a September 10, 2020 report that Appellant had "repented immediately, has greater godly discernment from the experience, continues to be held accountable for his actions, and has responded with a teachable spirit." It recommended to SC that "no further action be taken concerning this report, except to continue to shepherd and support TE Sears and his family." The September 10, 2020 report then reads “MSP: The Shepherding Committee... accepted the recommendation of the Ad Hoc Committee."

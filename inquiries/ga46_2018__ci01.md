# App. O II.B — Granting BCO 59 Full Constitutional Status

*A Grace Presbytery overture to amend and elevate BCO 59 was likewise in conflict, as granting full constitutional status constitutes adding to the BCO and requires the BCO amendment process per BCO 26-2.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 46th (2018)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 59, BCO 26-2  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga46_2018 minutes p.316 / PDF p.318](../markdown/ga46_2018.md#ga46-p318)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 2 from Grace Presbytery ("Amend BCO 59 and Grant Full Constitutional Status") was in conflict with the Constitution. Adopting Overture 2 would have been the same as changing the BCO since giving this chapter full constitutional status is essentially adding to the Constitution of the PCA, and thus requires the same process of approvals as required for any change to the BCO (cf. BCO 26-2).

**Key words:** BCO 59, BCO 26-2

**Inquiry from:** Grace Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga46_2018 minutes p.316 / PDF p.318](../markdown/ga46_2018.md#ga46-p318)

## Verbatim record

Overture 2 from Grace Presbytery: “Amend BCO Chapter 59 and Grant Full Constitutional Authority" In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 2 is in conflict with the Constitution. It is the opinion of the CCB that if Overture 2 was to be adopted, it would be the same as changing the BCO since giving this chapter full constitutional status is essentially adding to the Constitution of the PCA, and thus requires the same process of approvals as required for any change to the BCO (cf. BCO 26-2). It is the opinion of the CCB that a single GA does not have authority to “grant” constitutional status per the provisions of BCO 26-2. Adopted by the CCB

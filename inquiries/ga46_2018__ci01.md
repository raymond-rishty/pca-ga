# App. O II.B — Granting BCO 59 full constitutional status

*The CCB found Grace Presbytery's Overture 2 in conflict because granting BCO 59 full constitutional status would effectively change the BCO without following the amendment process in BCO 26-2.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 46th (2018)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 59, BCO 26-2  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga46_2018 minutes p. 316 / PDF p. 318](../markdown/ga46_2018.md#ga46-p318)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 2 from Grace Presbytery (“Amend BCO Chapter 59 and Grant Full Constitutional Authority”) was in conflict. Giving BCO 59 full constitutional status would effectively change the BCO, requiring the approval process set out in BCO 26-2; one General Assembly cannot grant that status by itself.

**Key words:** BCO 59, BCO 26-2

**Inquiry from:** Grace Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga46_2018 minutes p.316 / PDF p.318](../markdown/ga46_2018.md#ga46-p318)

## Verbatim record

### As referred / posed

2. That **Overture 2** from Grace Presbytery (“Amend _BCO_ Chapter 59

and Grant Full Constitutional Authority,” p. 671) be answered by
**reference** to the action taken in Overture 24. _Adopted_

### CCB advice

**B.** Overture 2 from Grace Presbytery: “Amend _BCO_ Chapter 59 and

Grant Full Constitutional Authority”
In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 2 **is in conflict** with the
Constitution. It is the opinion of the CCB that if Overture 2 was to be
adopted, it would be the same as changing the _BCO_ since giving this
chapter full constitutional status is essentially adding to the
Constitution of the PCA _,_ and thus requires the same process of
approvals as required for any change to the _BCO_ (cf. _BCO_ 26-2) _._ It is
the opinion of the CCB that a single GA does not have authority to
“grant” constitutional status per the provisions of _BCO_ 26-2.
_Adopted by the CCB_

# 34-34 II.C — Referring All BCO Changes to CCB and Bills & Overtures

*The CCB found James River Presbytery's overture mandating all BCO changes go to Bills and Overtures in conflict with RAO 13-1, which permits BCO amendments of a particular nature to be referred to other committees.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 34th (2006)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 10-5, RAO 11-5, RAO 13-1, BCO 14-1.15, BCO 14-1  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga34_2006 minutes p.80 / PDF p.82](../markdown/ga34_2006.md#ga34-p82)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

- In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 14 from James River Presbytery ("Amend RAO 10-5 [now 11-5] to Refer All BCO Changes to CCB and Bills & Overtures") was in conflict with RAO 13-1* in that the business assigned to the Bills & Overture Committee was defined as "of general nature." RAO 13-1 permits BCO amendments of a particular nature to be referred to other Committees of Commissioners. Furthermore, this amendment failed to recognize that proposals to amend the BCO can come before the Assembly in other reports as allowed by BCO 14-1.15.
- In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 14 from James River Presbytery ("Amend RAO 10-5 [now 11-5] to Refer All BCO Changes to CCB and Bills & Overtures") was in conflict with RAO 13-1* in that the business assigned to the Bills & Overture Committee was defined as "of general nature." RAO 13-1 permits BCO amendments of a particular nature to be referred to other Committees of Commissioners. Furthermore, this amendment failed to recognize that proposals to amend the BCO can come before the Assembly in other reports as allowed by BCO 14-1(15).

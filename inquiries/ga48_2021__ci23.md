# II.U — Amend BCO 21 and 24 to Clarify Moral Requirements for Church Office

*The CCB found that proposed amendments to BCO 21 and 24 clarifying moral qualifications for church office were not in conflict with the BCO, and the overture was adopted.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 48th (2021)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 21, BCO 24, BCO 13-6, BCO 21-4  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted

*Source: [ga48_2021 minutes p.464 / PDF p.477](../markdown/ga48_2021.md#ga48-p464)*

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 30 from Lowcountry Presbytery: “Amend BCO 21 & 24 to Clarify Moral Requirements for Church Office" In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 30 is not in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. CCB notes that if Overture 30 is adopted, it would require amendment to BCO 13-6 to update the re-lettering of BCO 21-4. Adopted

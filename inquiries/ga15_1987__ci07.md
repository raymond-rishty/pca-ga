## Minutes transcript

9. That the advice of the Permanent Committee regarding Constitutional Inquiry (hereafter designated "CI") #3 be ratified. Constitutional Inquiry #3. From All Saints Reformed Presbyterian Church, Richmond, VA, regarding a church treasurer. "Is it in conflict with BCO 9-4 for the deacons to delegate the responsibilities of treasurer to a capable member of the congregation who is not a deacon?"

### ANSWER

"No." Adopted

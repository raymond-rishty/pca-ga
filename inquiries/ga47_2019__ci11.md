# II.K — Amend RAO 11-5 to Prevent Confusion and Possible Contradictory Actions on Overtures by Multiple Committees of Commissioners

*An overture to amend RAO 11-5 to prevent multiple committees of commissioners from taking contradictory actions on overtures was found to create internal conflict and was adopted by the CCB.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 11-5  ·  **Disposition:** creates internal conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga47_2019 minutes p.400 / PDF p.402](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p402)*

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 15 from West Hudson Presbytery: “Amend RAO 11-5 to Prevent Confusion and Possible Contradictory Actions on Overtures by Multiple Committees of Commissioners"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overtures 15 and 18 create an internal conflict in RAO 11-5. The Overtures do not specify how to refer overtures that require consideration by more than one Committee or Agency. Adopted by the CCB

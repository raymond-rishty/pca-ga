## Minutes transcript

Reference 2 -- from Eastern Carolina Presbytery re Voting Requirements: Question: Does a Session or the Moderator of a Congregational meeting have the authority to require a greater vote than that required by the Book of Church Order for the call of a pastor or for the dissolution or a pastoral relationship of for the election of a church officer? That is, may the provision of BCO 20-4 and 24-4, "a majority of all the voters present shall be required to elect," be made more restrictive? Response: It is not within the purview of the Session or Moderator to require a greater than majority vote for the election of a pastor or church officer. The BCO nowhere gives such powers to Sessions or Moderators. Allowing them to claim such power would open the door to the possible thwarting of the right of the members of the Congregation to determine those who will rule over them, a right which undergirds BCO 20 and 24. Adopted by CCB

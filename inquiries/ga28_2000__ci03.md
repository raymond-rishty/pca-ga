## Minutes transcript

Reference 3 – (Communication 4) Presbytery of the Ascension re: RAO 4-2; 4-3; 4-4 and 13-2: Question – Which of the entities listed in RAO 4-2, 4-3 and 4-4 are covered by the "ineligibility provision" of RAO 13-2, and why? Response – It is the advice of the Committee that the term "permanent committee" is a technical term referring only to the four committees mentioned in RAO 4-2 (cf. BCO 14-1.12). Therefore, only those serving on those four committees (or their sub-committees and staffs) are barred from serving on committees of commissioners (cf. RAO 4-10.a). Service on any other committee, commission or agency of the General Assembly would not, under our current rules, bar one from serving on a committee of commissioners unless he would be serving on a committee of commissioners that oversees the committee or agency on which he serves (see Roberts Rules sec. 44). The response was adopted by proper motion, 8-0-0.

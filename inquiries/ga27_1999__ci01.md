# 27-43 II.1 — Authority to Sell Church Property

*CCB advised that deacons, subject to session approval and congregational consent, hold authority to sell church property such as donated stock.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 27th (1999)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 9-2, BCO 12-5, BCO 25-6, BCO 25-7, BCO 25-8, BCO 25-10  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga27_1999 lines 6703–6713](../markdown/ga27_1999.md#ga27-p144)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from First Presbyterian Church of Montgomery, AL, regarding who has the authority to sell stock given to the church, by stating that the deacons have authority, subject to the approval of the session and consent of the congregation, to sell church property. The authority of sessions, trustees, and corporation officers to sell church property was also addressed (BCO 9-2, 12-5; 25-6, 25-7, 25-8, 25-10).

**Key words:** BCO 9-2, BCO 12-5, BCO 25-6, BCO 25-7, BCO 25-8, BCO 25-10

**Inquiry from:** First Presbyterian Church of Montgomery, AL

## Minutes transcript

The CCB received a request for advice from the Stated Clerk in a communication dated May 31, 2024: [W]hether future RAO changes originating from any source should be required to have CCB review and Overtures Committee approval before being voted upon by the General Assembly.

It was moved and carried to adopt the following advice to the Stated Clerk: Rules of Assembly Operations 8-2.b.3 requires that the Committee on Constitutional Business "[p]rovide advice concerning the effect of any

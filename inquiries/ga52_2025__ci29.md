# III.A — Whether future RAO changes from any source must have CCB review and Overtures Committee approval before GA vote

*The CCB ruled that all RAO amendments require CCB review per RAO 8-2.b.3, but no provision requires Overtures Committee approval for RAO amendments originating from CRPR or the SJC.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 8-2.b.3, RAO 15-1  ·  **Disposition:** All RAO amendments require CCB review per RAO 8-2.b.3; no provision requires Overtures Committee approval for RAO amendments from CRPR or SJC; Adopted 8-0-0

## Verbatim record

In this SJC Decision, the authority issue is addressed in Issue 1. But because we find that the Session had the authority to render its decision, a second issue needs to be addressed regarding the exercise of judgment and discretion. This Case involves both a matter of law (i.e., differing constitutional interpretations) and a matter of discretion and judgment.

Part of the argumentation in the first Complaint against the Session's benediction decision, sustained by Presbytery at its January 2023 meeting in Santa Barbara, included quotes from BCO 50, 51, and 52 regarding the minister's discretion and responsibilities in worship. However, those chapters are not part of our Constitution. The second Complaint against the same Session decision, which was sustained by Presbytery at its October 2023 meeting in Calabasas, argued primarily that the Session's decision bound the conscience of the minister. The issue of conscience is addressed in the Reasoning below on Issue 2.

Regarding Issue 1 and Session authority, the Parties disagreed primarily on the interpretation of BCO 12-5.e and on how extensive a Session's authority is regarding worship services. Complainant contends a Session has more authority than what Presbytery contends. The disputed section is below, with enumeration added for clarity.

BCO 12-5. The church Session is charged with maintaining the spiritual government of the church, for which purpose it has power:

- **e.** To exercise, in accordance with the Directory for Worship,

1. authority over the time and place of the preaching of the Word

2. and the administration of the Sacraments,

3. over all other religious services,

4. over the music in the services, and

5. over the uses to which the church building and associated properties may be put;

6. to take the oversight of the singing in the public worship of God;

# 29-28 III.1 — Withdrawal from the PCA

*The Constitution is silent on whether a whole presbytery may withdraw from the GA, though individual congregations may withdraw as a group with each congregation's consent.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 29th (2001)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 25-11  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga29_2001 lines 5844–5864](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p133)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from Westminster Presbytery by advising the Assembly that the constitution is silent on the question of whether a presbytery as a whole may withdraw from the General Assembly (though the 2nd General Assembly received Westminster Presbytery with a provision recognizing its right to withdraw, cf. 2001, p. 143, 29-28, Item 1). The CCB referenced BCO 25-11 regarding procedures to be followed if individual churches withdraw from a presbytery in a group, and stated the requirement that, if a group of churches should choose to leave the PCA in order to form a new affiliation, or to continue an affiliation that they perceive as antecedent to the PCA, then they can peaceably withdraw as a group subject to the consent of each congregation in the group.

**Key words:** BCO 25-11

**Inquiry from:** Westminster Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga29_2001 p.133](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p133)

## Verbatim record

Should Westminster Presbytery, contrary to this Assembly's brotherly advice and sincere hope, persevere in the course set out in its action to withdraw from the Presbyterian Church in America, it is the judgment of this Assembly that at the effective date of the withdrawal, all the congregations and Teaching Elders of Westminster Presbytery will no longer be members of the Presbyterian Church in America. Should Teaching Elders or congregations find that they are unable in good conscience to continue as members of Westminster Presbytery as seceded, and by appropriate act renounce its jurisdiction, they may seek admission to the Presbyterian Church in America.

Finally, we appeal to Westminster Presbytery, if Presbytery remains determined to continue in their announced course, that, for the sake of those who may find their insurance coverage threatened by Presbytery's withdrawal, to meet and change the effective date of the action to withdraw, until the conclusion of the 30th General Assembly, so that those who desire to remain members of the PCA may immediately be admitted as a presbytery of the PCA. Adopted as amended

OVERTURE 38 From Westminster Presbytery (to MNA) "Erect New Presbytery"

Westminster Presbytery hereby notifies the General Assembly of its intention to withdraw from the Presbyterian Church in America, effective at the January 2002 Stated Meeting of Presbytery; and the General Assembly be overtured to take the following action:

That the General Assembly, under the powers granted to it under the PCA Constitution to erect new presbyteries and to unite and divide presbyteries per their request, hereby established a new presbytery, in accordance with the following criteria:

Those churches and ministers which have certified to the Stated Clerk of Westminster Presbytery by the January 2002 Stated Meeting of said Presbytery that they wish to remain in Westminster Presbytery, shall be constituted as Westminster Presbytery; while those churches and ministers which have not certified to the Stated Clerk of Westminster Presbytery by the January 2002 Stated Meeting of said Presbytery that they wish to remain in Westminster Presbytery, shall be constituted as a new Presbytery, effective that date, with the same boundaries as the present Westminster Presbytery.

Adopted by Westminster Presbytery on May 15, 2001. Attested by: /s/ D. Steven Meyerhoff, Stated Clerk

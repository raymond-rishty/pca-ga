# II.R — 21-4, and 24-1 to Require Background Checks for Church Office

*The CCB found a proposed overture requiring background checks for church office to be in conflict with the BCO and WCF Preliminary Principles.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 51st (2024)  ·  **Provisions:** WCF 23.3, BCO Preliminary Principle II, BCO 13-6  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga51_2024 minutes p.228 / PDF p.231](../markdown/ga51_2024.md#ga51-p231)*

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 23 from Missouri Presbytery: “Amend BCO 13-6, 21-4, and 24- 1 to Require Background Checks for Church Office"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 23 is in conflict with (1) WCF 23.3 because, insofar as it adds a requirement for Presbytery to “cause... to be performed" something that the civil magistrate might withhold or abolish, it cedes to the civil magistrate an effective authority he may not assume, namely, the right to deny admittance to the body of Christ and (2) BCO Preliminary Principle II in that it deprives members of the church of the right to determine who shall be admitted to its communion.

CCB also notes that should this Overture and Overture 2 be adopted, there should be clarity regarding how the proposed amendments interact to amend BCO 13-6. Adopted 8-0-0

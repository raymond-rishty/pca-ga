# 34-34 IV.D — The Session Approving Severance Packages for Ministers

*CCB advised that sessions may approve severance packages for ministers without congregational approval, as these are budgetary matters under BCO 12-5b.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 34th (2006)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 12-5b  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga34_2006 lines 4126–4134](../markdown/ga34_2006.md#ga34-p82)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from Westminster Presbytery on who may approve severance packages for ministers, by stating that sessions can approve severance packages without congregational approval because these are budgetary matters (BCO 12-5b).

**Key words:** BCO 12-5b

**Inquiry from:** Westminster Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga34_2006 p.82](../markdown/ga34_2006.md#ga34-p82)

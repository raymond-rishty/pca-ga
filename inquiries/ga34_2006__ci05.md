## Minutes transcript

Non-judicial Reference 1 from Westminster Presbytery: “...whether severance packages for ministers must be approved by the congregation or may they be approved by the session alone?" It is the opinion of the CCB that sessions can approve severance packages because these are budgetary matters. Approval of budgetary matters are the responsibility of the Session (BCO 12-5b). There is no constitutional mandate that severance packages be approved by the congregation. Adopted by the CCB

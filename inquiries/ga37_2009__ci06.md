## Minutes transcript

4. Regarding the non-judicial reference from Central Carolina Presbytery concerning the procedure adopted by one of its member churches to apply BCO 24-5: The Constitution of the PCA does not permit a congregation to require that candidates for church office receive greater than a majority of the vote to be elected. "In every case a majority of all the voters present shall be required to elect." (BCO 24-4). If the majority are not dissuaded by the minority, and "insist on their right to choose their officers, the election shall stand" (BCO 24-5). Adopted by the CCB

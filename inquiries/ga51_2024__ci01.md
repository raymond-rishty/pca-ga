# II.A — Amend BCO 35-1 and 35-8 Regarding Witness Eligibility

*The CCB found an overture to amend witness eligibility provisions in BCO 35-1 and 35-8 to be constitutionally vague.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 51st (2024)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 35-1, BCO 35-8  ·  **Disposition:** constitutionally vague; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga51_2024 minutes p.223 / PDF p.226](../markdown/ga51_2024.md#ga51-p223)*

---

## Verbatim record

A.** Overture 1 from Piedmont Triad Presbytery: “Amend BCO 35-1 and 35-8 Regarding Witness Eligibility"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 1 is constitutionally vague in that it provides the mechanism of objecting to witnesses for incompetency, even though the amended language does not use the word incompetency, without providing courts any apparent rule to guide their ruling on the objection. Adopted

# II.G — Amend BCO 32-19 to Clarify Use of Professional Counsel in Cases of Process

*The CCB found that a proposed amendment to BCO 32-19 clarifying use of professional counsel in disciplinary proceedings conflicts with the existing BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 50th (2023)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 32-19, BCO 8-9, BCO 13-1  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga50_2023 minutes p.456 / PDF p.459](../markdown/ga50_2023.md#ga50-p459)*

---

## Verbatim record

G.** Overture 10 from Northern New England Presbytery: "Amend BCO 32-19 to Clarify Use of Professional Counsel in Cases of Process"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 10 is in conflict with BCO 8-9,  
13-1 in that it removes the right of full participation from some  
members of the Court.

Additionally, we note the vagueness of the phrase "corporate resources” and the ambiguous phrase “admitted to the bar” not sufficiently clear for a party to know whether he may utilize a representative that is licensed to practice law in another jurisdiction or whose license to practice law is inactive. Adopted

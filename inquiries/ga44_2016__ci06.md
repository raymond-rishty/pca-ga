## Minutes transcript

The Korean Southwest Presbytery submitted a second non-judicial reference for the CCB’s consideration regarding regarding ordination credentials of a former presbytery member.

The CCB responded as follows: It is the opinion of the CCB that records indicate that Reverend Jung Hyun Oh was properly ordained and at the time of his transfer was a member in good standing of KSWP. Adopted. 8-0-0.

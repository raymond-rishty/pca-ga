# II.M — Amend BCO 7 Regarding Men Who Hold Office

*The CCB found that an overture amending BCO 7 regarding the requirement that church officers be men is not in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 50th (2023)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 7  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga50_2023 minutes p.457 / PDF p.460](../markdown/ga50_2023.md#ga50-p460)*

## Verbatim record

M.** Overture 17 from the Session of Meadowview Reformed Presbyterian Church, Lexington, NC: “Amend BCO 7 Regarding Men Who Hold Office"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 17 is not in conflict with the Constitution. Adopted

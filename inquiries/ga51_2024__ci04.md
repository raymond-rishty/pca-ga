# II.C — Grant Constitutional Status to BCO 53 re Preaching

*The CCB found no constitutional conflict in an overture to grant constitutional status to BCO 53 governing the ministry of preaching.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 51st (2024)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 53, BCO 26-2  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga51_2024 minutes p.224 / PDF p.227](../markdown/ga51_2024.md#ga51-p227)*

---

## Verbatim record

C.** Overture 3 from Pee Dee Presbytery: “Grant Constitutional Status to BCO 53 re Preaching"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 3 is not in conflict with the Constitution.

CCB also notes that in order for this proposal to gain constitutional status, it must be submitted to the constitutional amendment process as outlined in BCO 26-2. Adopted

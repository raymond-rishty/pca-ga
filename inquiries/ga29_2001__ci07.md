## Minutes transcript

Reference 7 from New River Presbytery re Exceptions to the Minutes of New River Presbytery The Chair ruled that New River Presbytery is asking the CCB to serve as a channel to give advice to the CRPR. Response: The reference from New River Presbytery asks CCB to advise the CRPR. It is not within our purview to provide advise to committees who have not solicited it (see duties in RAO 7-2). Adopted by CCB

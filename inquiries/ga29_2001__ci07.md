# 29-28 III.7 — CCB Providing Unsolicited Advice to Committees

*The CCB advised New River Presbytery that it lacks authority to give unsolicited advice to other committees, though the CRPR may make any recommendation within its RAO 16 purview to the GA.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 29th (2001)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 14, RAO 16  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga29_2001 lines 5949–5955](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p137)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from New River Presbytery asking the CCB to serve as a channel to give advice to the CRPR, by replying that it is not within the purview of the CCB to provide advice to committees who have not solicited it. The CCB answered a related constitutional inquiry (2001, p. 143, 29-28, Item 2) asking that it advise the CRPR about an exception in presbytery minutes, by stating that the CRPR may make to the GA any recommendation it wishes which are within the purview of RAO 14 [now RAO 16], and that it is then up to the GA to decide how to handle such recommendations.

**Key words:** RAO 14, RAO 16

**Inquiry from:** New River Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga29_2001 p.137](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p137)

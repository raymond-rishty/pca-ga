# 19-48 — Meaning of 'stay of proceedings' under BCO 40-5

*Although 'stay of further proceedings' technically means holding a matter in abeyance, the CCB found that the 17th and 18th General Assemblies clearly intended the stay to finally terminate the matter.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 19th (1991)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 40-5  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga19_1991 lines 5289–5332](../markdown/ga19_1991.md#ga19-p100)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

1991. p. 100. Reasoning in a case: 'The crux of this issue relates to the language used by the 17th General Assembly that "all further proceedings in this case be stayed cf BCO 40-5."' Acting under BCO 40-5, the 17th General Assembly cited Mississippi Valley Presbytery to appear and appointed a Commission for disposition of the matter. The 17th General Assembly adopted the Commission's recommendation to stay all further proceedings. The committee opined that the 17th General Assembly clearly intended to dispose of the matter finally and conclusively. The words in BCO 40-5 — 'may stay all further proceedings' — technically mean to hold in abeyance further proceedings until such time as the court decides to lift 'the stay' and reopen the matter. However, it was the clear intention of the 17th and 18th General Assemblies to end, conclude and terminate the matter.

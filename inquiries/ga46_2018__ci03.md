# App. O, section II.F II.F — Referring the Recommendations of Ad Interim Committees to the Overtures Committee

*The CCB found Calvary Presbytery's overture requiring ad interim committee recommendations to go through the Overtures Committee in conflict with BCO 14-1.15 and productive of procedural ambiguity on the GA floor.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 46th (2018)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 14-1.15  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga46_2018 lines 15648–15659](../markdown/ga46_2018.md#ga46-p317)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 6 from Calvary Presbytery ("Revise RAO 9 to Require that Recommendations from Ad Interim Committees be referred to OC") was in conflict with BCO 14-1.15 and created ambiguity within the RAO which could create a conflict on the floor of GA when opposing recommendations could come from two different committees with no RAO procedures in place to resolve such conflicts.

**Key words:** BCO 14-1.15

**Inquiry from:** Calvary Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga46_2018 p.317](../markdown/ga46_2018.md#ga46-p317)

# 33-29 II — A Presbytery Including as a Member a Church Not Within Its Bounds

*The CCB advised that a presbytery does not have constitutional authority under BCO 13-1 to include as a member a church located outside its bounds.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 33rd (2005)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 13-1  ·  **Disposition:** advice given — not constitutional

*Source: [ga33_2005 lines 7796–7810](../markdown/ga33_2005.md#ga33-p156)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from Potomac Presbytery on whether a presbytery has the constitutional right to include as a member a church not within its bounds, by stating that a presbytery does not have the constitutional right to include as a member a church not within its bounds (BCO 13-1).

**Key words:** BCO 13-1

**Inquiry from:** Potomac Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga33_2005 p.155](../markdown/ga33_2005.md#ga33-p156)

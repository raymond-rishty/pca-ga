# II.P — Amend BCO 17 by Adding a Clause Which Prohibits Ordination for Men Who Self-Identify as 'Gay Christians,' 'Same-sex Attracted Christians,' 'Homosexual Christians,' or Like Terms

*The CCB found this overture to amend BCO 17 prohibiting ordination of men using gay-identity labels to be in conflict with existing BCO provisions, though it was adopted.*

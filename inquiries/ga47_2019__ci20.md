# II.T — 43-2, and 43-3 as well as OMSJC 18.10.b to Clarify Filing Requirements and Allow Electronic Filings

*Proposed amendments to BCO 42-4, 43-2, 43-3, and OMSJC to clarify filing requirements and allow electronic filings were found not in conflict with the Constitution.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 42-4, BCO 43-2, BCO 43-3, OMSJC 18.10.b  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga47_2019 minutes p.402 / PDF p.404](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p402)*

---

## Verbatim record

T.** Overture 34 from Philadelphia Presbytery: “Amend BCO 42-4, 43-2, and 43-3 as well as OMSJC 18.10.b to Clarify Filing Requirements and Allow Electronic Filings"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 34 is not in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. While both Overtures 9 and 34 are not in conflict with the Constitution, they are in conflict with each other and only one can be adopted.

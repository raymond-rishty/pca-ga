# II.T — Amend BCO 31-10 to Require Vote of the Entire Court to Suspend an Officer's Official Functions during Process

*Proposed amendment requiring the entire court to vote before suspending an officer's functions during BCO 31-10 process was found not in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 31-10  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.256 / PDF p.258](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p256)*

---

## Verbatim record

T.** Overture 31 from the Houston Metro Presbytery: “Amend BCO 31-10 to Require Vote of the Entire Court to Suspend an Officer's Official Functions during Process"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 31 is not in conflict with the Constitution.

CCB notes that the word “administratively” is not otherwise constitutionally defined and may therefore create an ambiguity. Adopted

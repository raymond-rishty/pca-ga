# II.V — Amend BCO 57-2 re the Examination of Young Persons for Admission to the Sealing Ordinances

*Proposed amendment to BCO 57-2 regarding examination of young persons for admission to the sealing ordinances was found not in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 57-2  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 lines 14906–14911](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p257)*

## Verbatim record

| --- | --- |
| 01/28/23 | Pacific Presbytery stated meeting in Santa Barbara, at which it sustained the Shaw/Riedinger Complaint and annulled the decision of the VPC Session. REs Martinez and Jesse Cook were VPC commissioners to this meeting. |
| 03/12/23 | REs Martinez and Cook filed a BCO 43-1 Complaint with Presbytery against its 1/28/23 action in Santa Barbara. |
| 05/02/23 | Presbytery stated meeting in Las Vegas, at which it sustained the Martinez/Cook Complaint, thus rescinding/reversing its prior decision of 01/28/23. The original Session decision was reinstated. |
| 07/01/23 | TE Svendsen, VPC Sr. Pastor, filed a timely BCO 43-1 Complaint with Presbytery against its 5/02/23 action that sustained Martinez/Cook Complaint in Las Vegas. |
| 10/03/23 | Presbytery stated meeting in Calabasas, CA (45 minutes NW of Los Angeles). Presbytery sustained the Svendsen Complaint, thus returning to, and reaffirming, its decision eight months prior on 01/28/23 in Santa Barbara wherein it annulled the Session decision.[^fn-ga52-p886-n16] No rationale was adopted. |

[^fn-ga52-p886-n16]: VPC REs Shaw, Riedinger, and Mateko also signed the Svendsen Complaint, but because they were not VPC commissioners to the May Presbytery meeting, they did not have standing to file a BCO 43-1 Complaint.

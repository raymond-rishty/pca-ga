# App. O II.B — Specifying that Only Males May Be Ordained

*The overture was in conflict because designating male-only ordination an 'essential component' of ecclesiology improperly added it to the 'fundamental to our system of doctrine' standard.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 45th (2017)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 24-11, BCO 21-4.e, BCO 21-4.f  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga45_2017 minutes p.325 / PDF p.327](../markdown/ga45_2017.md#ga45-p325)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 4 from Northwest Georgia Presbytery ("Add BCO 24-11 to Specify that Males Only May be Ordained as Elders or Deacons") was in conflict with the Constitution, because the phrase "an essential component to our ecclesiology" should not be added as a component that is "fundamental to our system of doctrine" as referenced in BCO 21-4.e and BCO 21-4.f.

**Key words:** BCO 24-11, BCO 21-4.e, BCO 21-4.f

**Inquiry from:** Northwest Georgia Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga45_2017 minutes p.325 / PDF p.327](../markdown/ga45_2017.md#ga45-p325)

## Verbatim record

Overture 4 from Northwest Georgia Presbytery: “Add BCO 24-11 to Specify that Males Only May be Ordained as Elders or Deacons" In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 4 is in conflict with the Constitution. It is the opinion of the CCB that the phrase "an essential component to our ecclesiology" should not be added as a component that is "fundamental to our system of doctrine" as referenced in BCO 21-4.e and 21-4.f. The BCO is not the document where our doctrinal standards are defined. Adopted by the CCB

# 32-36 II.1 — Constitutional meaning of “papists” in WCF 24-3

*The CCB declined to define “papists” abstractly, leaving the question to presbyteries, judicial process, or an in thesi statement.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 32nd (2004)  ·  **Provisions:** WCF 24-3  ·  **Disposition:** declined to determine abstractly

*Source: [ga32_2004 p. 133](../markdown/ga32_2004.md#ga32-p135)*

---

## Digest headnote

The Committee on Review of Presbytery Records asked for the constitutional definition of “papists” in WCF 24-3, including whether the term includes Roman Catholics who can credibly profess faith in Christ alone as Savior. The CCB declined to decide an abstract doctrinal question and said it should be resolved by an in thesi statement, judicial process, or presbyteries working through the issue, subject to review.

**Key words:** WCF 24-3

**Inquiry from:** Committee on Review of Presbytery Records

**In the minutes:** [ga32_2004 p.133](../markdown/ga32_2004.md#ga32-p135)

---

## Verbatim record

### As referred / posed

Constitutional Inquiry 1 from the Committee on Review of Presbytery Records. What is the constitutional definition of “papists” in WCF 24-3? Does this term include Roman Catholics who can give a credible profession of faith in Christ alone as their Savior?

### CCB advice

It would be unwise, improper, and unconstitutional for the Committee on Constitutional Business to determine abstractly the meaning of a point of doctrine such as this. The understanding of this term should be determined by means such as an in thesi statement, by judicial process, or, most commonly, by presbyteries working through the issue, subject to proper review. Adopted by CCB. (TE Hoop recused himself from this vote.)

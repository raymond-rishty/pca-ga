# II.N — Amend BCO 16 by Adding Paragraph 16-4 on Qualifications for Ordination

*An overture from Northwest Georgia Presbytery proposing a new BCO 16-4 on ordination qualifications was found not in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 49th (2022)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 16-4  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga49_2022 minutes p.423 / PDF p.426](../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p423)*

---

## Verbatim record

N.** Overture 20 from Northwest Georgia Presbytery: “Amend BCO 16 by Adding Paragraph 16-4 on Qualifications for Ordination"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overtures 20 is not in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. CCB notes that Overtures 12, 23, and 29 contain similar language to amend BCO 16-4. Adopted by the CCB

# App. O, section II.A II.A — Granting BCO 59 Full Constitutional Status

*A Calvary Presbytery overture to elevate BCO 59 to full constitutional status was in conflict because doing so is equivalent to amending the BCO and requires the same multi-stage approval process under BCO 26-2.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 45th (2017)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 59, BCO 26-2  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga45_2017 lines 15489–15503](../markdown/ga45_2017.md#ga45-p324)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 2 from Calvary Presbytery ("Grant BCO 59, 'Solemnization of Marriage,' Full Constitutional Status") was in conflict with the Constitution. Adoption of Overture 2 would be the same as changing the BCO since giving this chapter full constitutional status is essentially adding to the Constitution of the PCA, and thus requires the same process of approvals as required for any change to the BCO (cf. BCO 26-2).

**Key words:** BCO 59, BCO 26-2

**Inquiry from:** Calvary Presbytery

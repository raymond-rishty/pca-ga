# App. O II.H — Referring Overtures Regarding Committees and Agencies and Ad Interim Committees to Overtures Committee

*The CCB found Western Carolina Presbytery's overture to also route agency and ad interim committee overtures to the Overtures Committee in conflict with the RAO, creating irresolvable procedural ambiguity at the GA.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 45th (2017)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 11-5  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga45_2017 minutes p.326 / PDF p.328](../markdown/ga45_2017.md#ga45-p328)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 10 from Western Carolina Presbytery ("Revise RAO 11-5 to Direct Clerk to Refer Overtures Regarding Committees and Agencies and Ad Interim Committees to Overtures Committee Also") created ambiguity within the RAO and could create a conflict on the floor of GA when opposing recommendations could come from two different committees with no RAO procedures in place to resolve such conflicts.

**Key words:** RAO 11-5

**Inquiry from:** Western Carolina Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga45_2017 minutes p.326 / PDF p.328](../markdown/ga45_2017.md#ga45-p328)

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 10 from Western Carolina Presbytery: "Revise RAO 11-5 to Direct Clerk to Refer Overtures Regarding Committees and Agencies and Ad Interim Committees to Overtures Committee Also"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 10 creates ambiguity within the Rules of Assembly Operations. The proposed overture, if approved, could potentially create a conflict on the floor of the GA where opposing recommendations could come from two different committees with no RAO procedures in place on how to resolve such conflict. Adopted by the CCB

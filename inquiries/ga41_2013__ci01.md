# App. O II.D — Defining Supporting Reasons for a Complaint or Appeal

*The CCB found Suncoast Florida Presbytery's overture adding a definition of supporting reasons for complaints or appeals was not in conflict if BCO 32-18 is read as addressing cases in process.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 41st (2013)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 32-21, BCO 32-18  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict

*Source: [ga41_2013 minutes p.362 / PDF p.364](../markdown/ga41_2013.md#ga41-p364)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 4 from Suncoast Florida Presbytery ("Amend BCO 32 by Adding Section 32-21 Defining Supporting Reasons for a Complaint or Appeal") may not have been in conflict with other parts of the Constitution if BCO 32-18 is understood as dealing with cases in process.

**Key words:** BCO 32-21, BCO 32-18

**Inquiry from:** Suncoast Florida Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga41_2013 minutes p.362 / PDF p.364](../markdown/ga41_2013.md#ga41-p364)

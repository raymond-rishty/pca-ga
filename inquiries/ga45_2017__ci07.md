# App. O II.N — Procedure for Forming Ad Interim Committees

*The CCB found the overture in conflict with RAO 9-4, as the phrase 'exclusively submitted' directly contradicted existing provisions permitting other means of forming ad interim committees.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 45th (2017)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 9-4, RAO 9-5  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga45_2017 minutes p.327 / PDF p.329](../markdown/ga45_2017.md#ga45-p329)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 18 from James River Presbytery ("Amend RAO IX So That Ad Interim Committees May Only Be Formed in Response to Presbytery Overtures") was in conflict with the RAO 9-4. The phrase "exclusively submitted" (line # 22) was in direct conflict with RAO 9-4 [now 9-5].

**Key words:** RAO 9-4, RAO 9-5

**Inquiry from:** James River Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga45_2017 minutes p.327 / PDF p.329](../markdown/ga45_2017.md#ga45-p329)

## Verbatim record

Overture 18 from James River Presbytery: “Amend RAO IX So That Ad Interim Committees May Only Be Formed in Response to Presbytery Overtures"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 18 is in conflict with the Rules of Assembly Operations 9-4. The phrase “exclusively submitted” (line # 22) is in direct conflict with RAO 9-4. Adopted by the CCB

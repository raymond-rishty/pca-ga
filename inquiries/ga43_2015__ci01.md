# App. O, section II.D II.D — Requiring Church Officers to Testify

*An overture to compel church officer testimony was possibly in conflict with BCO 35-3 and 35-4 regarding the two-witness requirement and exclusion of witnesses during others' testimony.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 43rd (2015)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 35-1, BCO 35-3, BCO 35-4  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga43_2015 lines 18073–18082](../markdown/ga43_2015.md#ga43-p372)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 7 from the Session of New Hope PCA, Fairfax, VA ("Amend BCO 35-1 to Require Church Officers to Testify") may have been in conflict with the Constitution. BCO 35-3 leaves open the question of whether a person's testimony would count as one of the two witnesses required, and BCO 35-4 leaves open the question of whether the TE, as a witness, could be asked to not be present at the testimony of other witnesses.

**Key words:** BCO 35-1, BCO 35-3, BCO 35-4

**Inquiry from:** Session of New Hope PCA, Fairfax, VA

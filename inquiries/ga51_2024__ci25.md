# II.X — Amend BCO 53 by Addition To Ensure Only Men Preach

*The CCB found the proposed addition to BCO 53 to ensure only men preach in conflict with existing BCO provisions, adopted by a divided vote.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 51st (2024)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 53  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted 3-2-3

*Source: [ga51_2024 minutes p.229 / PDF p.232](../markdown/ga51_2024.md#ga51-p232)*

---

## Verbatim record

X.** Overture 29 from the Session of Bryce Avenue Presbyterian Church, White Rock, New Mexico: "Amend BCO 53 by Addition To Ensure Only Men Preach"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 29 is in conflict with the Constitution because it establishes a rule that applies to jurisdictions beyond the bounds of the PCA. Adopted

## Minutes transcript

On December 6, 2024, the Highlands Presbytery submitted the following questions to the General Assembly for advice only (BCO 41-3) regarding the Presbytery's compliance with SJC Case No. 2023-16:

1. Does the SJC have the remit from the General Assembly to address a question not raised by a complainant? (BCO 39-3.1)

2. Does the SJC have the remit from the General Assembly to rule in any other way than to either: (a) uphold the complaint, (b) deny the complaint, or (c) refer the case back to the Presbytery? (BCO 39-3.1, 43-10; OMSJC 14.4.c)

3. Does the SJC's ruling constitute an injunction contrary to the 50th General Assembly's approval of Highlands Presbytery's minutes? (M50GA, pp.564–565)

4. If the SJC gives a lower court an unlawful injunction (cf., BCO 40- 2), what options are available to the lower court in its response?

259

The CCB declined to accede to the reference and provided the following comment:

The decisions of the SJC are final decisions of the GA (BCO 15-5). The CCB does not render opinions on previous SJC decisions.

Adopted

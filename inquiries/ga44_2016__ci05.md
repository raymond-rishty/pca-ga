# App. O, section IV.B IV.B — Scope of Presbytery Stated Clerk to Disseminate Information

*CCB advised that a stated clerk may provide information consistent with presbytery records, especially when requested by a former TE in connection with ecclesiastical litigation.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 44th (2016)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 10-4  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga44_2016 lines 16876–16903](../markdown/ga44_2016.md#ga44-p349)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from Korean Southwest Presbytery regarding the scope of duty of the Presbytery stated clerk concerning the dissemination of information and offering of opinion. The CCB stated its opinion that, following BCO 10-4, it is in accordance with the duties of the clerk to provide information consistent with the records kept, and especially so, when asked by a former Presbytery TE to provide the information when it is related to ecclesiastical litigation.

**Key words:** BCO 10-4

# III.A — Is a member of the General Assembly's Standing Judicial Commission (SJC) eligible for concurrent service on the Board of a General Assembly Agency?

*The CCB advised that a member of the GA's Standing Judicial Commission is eligible to serve concurrently on the board of a General Assembly agency.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 8-2.a.1, BCO 15-4, BCO 14-1.12, BCO 15-1, RAO 4, RAO 4-3, RAO 4-5, RAO 4-6, RAO 4-10, RAO 4-11, RAO 4-12, RAO 4-13, RAO 4-14, RAO 4-15, RAO 4-16, RAO 4-19, RAO 4-20, RAO 4-21  ·  **Disposition:** Yes (eligible); Adopted by the CCB

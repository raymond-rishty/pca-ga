# II.B — Encourage CTS toward Growth and Release from Assembly Oversight

*The CCB found an overture encouraging CTS toward greater growth and release from Assembly oversight created a conflict with the RAO and Corporate By-Laws.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO, Corporate By-Laws  ·  **Disposition:** creates conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga47_2019 minutes p.398 / PDF p.400](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p400)*

---

## Verbatim record

B.** Overture 2 from Northwest Georgia Presbytery: "Encourage CTS toward Growth and Release from Assembly Oversight" In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 2 creates conflict with the Constitution in that the passage of this Overture requires unspecified subsequent independent actions to amend the RAO and Corporate By-Laws. Although the Overture does state what is required in the RAO and Corporate By-Laws that needs to be passed, this Overture does not contain any proposed contingent amendments for those sections. Adopted by the CCB

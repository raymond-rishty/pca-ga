# II.K — Amend BCO 36-4 to Add Language for the Administration of Definite Suspension from Office

*Proposed amendment to BCO 36-4 adding definite suspension from office language was found not in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 36-4  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

## Verbatim record

But, in fact, the Record shows that SC lacked a quorum at the September 10, 2020 meeting. SC therefore took no action on September 10, 2020. Furthermore, the Record gives no indication that SC subsequently

<a id="ga52-p880"></a>
<!-- PAGE ga=52 pdf_page=880 printed_page=878 -->

notified NP of the document that had been brought against Appellant. Nor is there indication in the Record that SC informed NP of its deliberations on September 10, 2020. The 15-1 Commission cannot be found to be in error for having failed to consider a report 1) that did not carry the recommendation of SC and, more importantly, 2) that SC never carried to NP. The 15-1 Commission could not deliberate or take action upon a report of which it was officially unaware.

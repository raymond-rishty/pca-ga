# II.E — Amend BCO 34-1 Establishing Threshold for Original Jurisdiction Requests

*CCB found in conflict a Calvary Presbytery overture setting a percentage threshold for original jurisdiction requests, touching BCO 34-1 and 11-4.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 49th (2022)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 34-1, BCO 11-4, BCO 31-2  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga49_2022 minutes p.420 / PDF p.423](../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p423)*

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 9 from Calvary Presbytery: “Amend BCO 34-1 Establishing Threshold for Original Jurisdiction Requests"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 9 is in conflict with the principle  
that every court has the right to resolve questions of doctrine, etc., in  
BCO 11-4 and 31-2, as this proposed motion would potentially allow  
other courts to assume original jurisdiction before the appropriate  
lower court has exercised its right.  
Adopted by the CCB

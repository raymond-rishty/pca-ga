# II.I — Amend BCO 35-1 and 35-7 to Allow All Persons as Witnesses in Cases of Process

*The CCB found that amending BCO 35-1 and 35-7 to permit all persons (without restriction) to serve as witnesses in cases of process is not in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 50th (2023)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 35-1, BCO 35-7  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 7-0-1

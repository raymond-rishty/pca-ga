# 36-30 II.F — Erecting a Committee or Commission Prior to Instituting Process

*The overture was in conflict with BCO 31-2 and 32-2, which make clear that a judicial case does not exist until process is actually instituted.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 36th (2008)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 15-3, BCO 31-2, BCO 32-2  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga36_2008 minutes p.71 / PDF p.71](../markdown/ga36_2008.md#ga36-p71)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 7 from Missouri Presbytery ("Amend BCO 15-3 Regarding Judicial Investigations") was in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. The proposed language conflicted with existing BCO 31-2 and BCO 32-2, which make it clear that a judicial case does not exist until process is actually instituted.

**Key words:** BCO 15-3, BCO 31-2, BCO 32-2

**Inquiry from:** Missouri Presbytery

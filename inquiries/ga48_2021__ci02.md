# II.AA — Amend BCO 32-13 and 35-5 to Allow Victim Protection Provisions

*The CCB found that amending BCO 32-13 and 35-5 to allow victim protection measures in discipline proceedings was not in conflict with the BCO, and the overture was adopted.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 48th (2021)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 32-13, BCO 35-5  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted

*Source: [ga48_2021 minutes p.465 / PDF p.478](../markdown/ga48_2021.md#ga48-p478)*

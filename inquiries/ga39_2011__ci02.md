## Minutes transcript

Minutes of the SJC officers meeting on May 13, 2010: in the fifth paragraph, reference is made to “rebuking” a Presbytery Clerk under the provisions of BCO 43-6; elsewhere in the paragraph, the action is referred to by the words “admonish” and “admonition," which elsewhere in the Constitution (BCO 30-1 and 2, 36-3) is identified as a judicial censure. While “admonition” and related words are often used in PCA circles in their less technical sense, use of those words in a context of action against an officer of the Church could be confusing.

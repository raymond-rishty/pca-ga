# App. O II.I — Timing of Higher Court Review of Complaints in Judicial Cases

*A Pacific Northwest overture to clarify timing of higher-court complaint review was in conflict because it would bar filing any complaint against a court while a judicial case is in process.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 44th (2016)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 43-1  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga44_2016 minutes p.348 / PDF p.350](../markdown/ga44_2016.md#ga44-p348)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 40 from Pacific Northwest Presbytery ("Amend BCO 43-1 to Clarify the Timing of Higher Court Review of Complaints in Judicial Cases") was in conflict with the Constitution. As written, Overture 40 would preclude the filing of complaints against any action of that court while any judicial case is in process.

**Key words:** BCO 43-1

**Inquiry from:** Pacific Northwest Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga44_2016 minutes p.348 / PDF p.350](../markdown/ga44_2016.md#ga44-p348)

## Verbatim record

Overture 40 from Pacific Northwest Presbytery: "Amend BCO 43-1 to Clarify the Timing of Higher Court Review of Complaints in Judicial Cases" In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 38 is in conflict with the Constitution. As currently written, Overture 40 would preclude the filing of complaints against any action of that court while any judicial case is in process. Adopted. 8-0-0.

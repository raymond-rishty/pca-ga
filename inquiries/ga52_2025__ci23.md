# II.U — Amend BCO 36 to Provide for Elevation of a Censure without an Entirely New Trial

*Proposed amendment to BCO 36 providing for censure elevation without a wholly new trial was found to be ambiguous under the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 36, BCO 32-10, BCO 32-13, BCO 32-15, BCO 32-16, BCO 32-17, BCO 32-18, BCO 35, BCO 37-7  ·  **Disposition:** ambiguous; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.256 / PDF p.258](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p258)*

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 32 from the Houston Metro Presbytery: “Amend BCO 36 to Provide for Elevation of a Censure without an Entirely New Trial"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 32 is ambiguous in that it is not clear whether the "hearing" in the added paragraph is governed by other provisions in the Rules of Discipline, such as BCO 32-10, -13, -15, -16, -17, -18; and BCO 35, and whether the accused has the right to Appeal an elevated suspension.

CCB notes that the phrase “original trial court” is potentially ambiguous in  
that it is not clear if the phrase refers to the exact commission or group of  
presbyters who heard the evidence at the original trial; whether the phrase

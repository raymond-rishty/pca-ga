## Minutes transcript

The CCB met on June 13, 2018, at the 46th General Assembly to respond to a request for advice from the Overtures Committee on the question of the referral of overtures. The Question: Does the Overtures Committee have exclusive property over overtures requesting amendment to the Constitution? Yes. Adopted by the CCB Rationale: The phrase “other than” in RAO 11-5 excludes “an overture proposing amendment to the Constitution" from the set of Overtures to be referred to permanent Committees and Agencies. The phrase “other than" is used throughout the BCO and RAO as a term of exclusion.

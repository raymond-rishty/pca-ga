## Minutes transcript

Non-Judicial Reference from Evangel Presbytery Evangel Presbytery made a non-judicial reference, inquiring as to the scope of the word “labor" in the context of BCO 13-2. The CCB responds to the non-judicial reference of Evangel Presbytery in the following manner: The BCO speaks of "labor" for teaching elders as ministry in "needful work" for "disseminating the Gospel for the edification of the Church" (BCO 8-4). The Presbytery determines whether such labor is needful and allowable for a teaching elder in its bounds (BCO 8-7). Adopted 8-0-0

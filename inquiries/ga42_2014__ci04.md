# App. O, section IV.B IV.B — Views of Male-Only Eldership and Candidates for Ordination or Transfer

*No constitutional procedure exists to record a candidate's views on the BCO's requirements, nor is a candidate required to list his differences with its provisions.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 42nd (2014)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 21-4.c  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga42_2014 lines 16451–16472](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p344)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from Philadelphia Presbytery regarding BCO 21-4.c and views of male-only eldership held by candidates for ordination or transfer, by stating that there is no constitutional procedure for recording a candidate's views regarding the requirements of the BCO; nor is a candidate required to provide a list of his differences with its provisions.

**Key words:** BCO 21-4.c

**Inquiry from:** Philadelphia Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga42_2014 p.344](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p344)

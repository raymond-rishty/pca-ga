# 38-34 II.A — Prohibiting Deaconesses

*CCB found an overture prohibiting deaconesses in conflict with the Constitution because 'deaconess' need not denote an ordained office equivalent to deacon.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 38th (2010)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 9-7  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga38_2010 lines 12664–12675](../markdown/ga38_2010.md#ga38-p267)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 2 from Central Carolina Presbytery ("Amend BCO 9-7 to Prohibit Deaconesses") was in conflict with the Constitution as it relied upon the following unwarranted assumptions about the Constitution: (1) that the term "deaconess" necessarily denotes an office equivalent to that of deacon, whereas in Scripture, to which the Constitution is subject, the term diakonos is most commonly used to refer to a person being a servant and not an office bearer; and (2) that it restricts the use of a term ("commissioned") not defined in the Constitution and uses the term as equivalent to the actions of ordination and installation.

**Key words:** BCO 9-7

**Inquiry from:** Central Carolina Presbytery

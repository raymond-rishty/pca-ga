# 17-82 III, 20 — Re-examination and divestiture of officers in rotational systems

*The CCB advised that the BCO provides no procedure for re-examining rotated-off officers' qualifications, but divestiture may only occur under BCO 24-6, 38-2, or 34-10.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 17th (1989)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 24-6, BCO 24-1, BCO 38-2, BCO 34-10  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

Constitutional Inquiry #4: From the Town North Presbyterian Church, Richardson, Texas, requesting advice concerning previous answers to their requests for advice (which have been ratified by the GA) concerning the election of officers. Their revised questions were: 1. Is a man to be re-examined for qualification for office each time he is re-nominated in a rotational system? 2. Which view is correct, in the case where an officer who has rotated off active service, but has not been disciplined or removed from office, a) must he ipso facto be considered as qualified and, thereby, be presented to the congregation as a qualified nominee if nominated; or b) may he, on examination by the Session, be considered to be unqualified because the Session believes there are circumstances that currently disqualify a nominee but which do not constitute grounds for discipline and/or deposition? 3. In the case where an officer who has rotated off active service, can he be divested of his office other than under BCO 38-2 or BCO 34-10. ANSWER: The Committee advises the Town North Presbyterian Church that the answers to their questions are: 1. & 2. There is no provision in the BCO to answer these questions. 3. An elder or deacon not presently serving on a Session or Diaconate cannot be divested except in accordance with 24-6, 38-2, or 34-10. Notation: The answer to question 3 above is an amplification of paragraph II of the 16th General Assembly's answer to Constitutional Inquiry #6 (M16GA, p. 178, 30). BCO 38-2 does not apply to an inactive officer unless the officer initiates the procedure of 38-2. Adopted as amended

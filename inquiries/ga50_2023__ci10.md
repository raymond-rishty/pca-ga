# II.J — Amend BCO 32-19 Regarding Use of Professional Counsel in Cases of Process

*The CCB found that a Northern California Presbytery overture amending BCO 32-19 regarding professional counsel in cases of process conflicts with the existing BCO.*

## APPENDIX O

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 14 is in conflict with BCO 8-9,  
13-1 in that it removes the right of full participation from some  
members of the Court.  
Additionally, we note the vagueness of the phrase "corporate  
resources" and an apparent internal inconsistency in the language in  
sections B.i and B.ii with section A of the proposed changes.  
Adopted 8-0-0

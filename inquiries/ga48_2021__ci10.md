# II.H — Revise RAO 11-2 to Disallow Electronic Communications Regarding Voting at the General Assembly

*An overture to revise RAO 11-2 disallowing electronic communications about GA voting was found not in conflict, though not germane to the proposed RAO chapter, and adopted.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 48th (2021)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 11-2  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict but not germane to the RAO chapter proposed; Adopted

*Source: [ga48_2021 minutes p.462 / PDF p.475](../markdown/ga48_2021.md#ga48-p475)*

---

## Verbatim record

H.** Overture 15 from the Session of New Covenant PCA, Abingdon, Maryland: “Revise RAO 11-2 to Disallow Electronic Communications Regarding Voting at the General Assembly" In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 15 is not in conflict but not germane to the RAO chapter for which it is proposed. 1.Adopted

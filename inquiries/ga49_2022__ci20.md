# II.T — Amend BCO 15-1 and 15-3 to Clarify Role of Presbytery Commission

*Potomac Presbytery's overture to amend BCO 15-1 and 15-3 clarifying a presbytery commission's role was found not in conflict with the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 49th (2022)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 15-1, BCO 15-3  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

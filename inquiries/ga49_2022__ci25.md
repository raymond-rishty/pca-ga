# II.Y — Amend RAO 10-6 to Authorize a Virtual Meeting Protocol

*CCB found the Administrative Committee's proposed RAO 10-6 amendment establishing a virtual meeting protocol to be in conflict with the Constitution.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 49th (2022)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 10-6, RAO 1-1  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga49_2022 minutes p.426 / PDF p.429](../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p426)*

---

## Verbatim record

Y.** Proposed RAO Amendment from the Administrative Committee: “Amend RAO 10-6 to Authorize a Virtual Meeting Protocol”

In the opinion of the CCB, the proposed amendment to RAO 10-6 is in conflict with the RAO'$ 1-1 in that RAO 1-1 requires the Lord's Supper to celebrated at any GA meeting. The CCB advises the AC that an amendment to RAO 1-1 adding “(except when a Condensed version of the meeting is being held as stipulated in RAO 10-6)" would resolve this conflict. Adopted by the CCB

# III.B — Whether overtures received on or by April 25 should be officially handled for this year's GA

*The CCB ruled that overtures received on or by April 25 are timely under RAO 11-6's 60-day deadline and must be officially handled at the current General Assembly.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 11-6  ·  **Disposition:** Overtures received on or by April 25 are timely per RAO 11-6 (60-day deadline); Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.259 / PDF p.261](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p261)*

---

## Verbatim record

The CCB received a request for advice from the Stated Clerk in a communication dated April 25, 2025, and in person at the meeting: Should overtures received on or by April 25 be officially handled for this year's General Assembly by the Stated Clerk's office?

It was moved and carried to adopt the following advice to the Stated Clerk: Overtures received on or by April 25 should be deemed timely for this year's General Assembly, having arrived within the 60-day deadline according to RAO 11-6. Adopted

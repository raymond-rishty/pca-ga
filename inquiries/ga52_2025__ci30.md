# III.B — Whether overtures received on or by April 25 should be officially handled for this year's GA

*The CCB ruled that overtures received on or by April 25 are timely under RAO 11-6's 60-day deadline and must be officially handled at the current General Assembly.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 11-6  ·  **Disposition:** Overtures received on or by April 25 are timely per RAO 11-6 (60-day deadline); Adopted 8-0-0

7. to ensure that the Word of God is preached only by such men as are sufficiently qualified (BCO 4-4, 53-2, 1 Timothy 2:11- 12);

8. to assemble the people for worship when there is no minister;

Considering BCO 12.5.e, and particularly the final clause, the SJC finds that the VPC Session had the constitutional authority to make the decision it made, i.e., to have worship services closed with benedictions and not with doxologies.

To cite a constitutional example, BCO 58-1 explicitly grants a Session the authority to determine how often a congregation observes the Lord's Supper. If a Session has authority over that extremely significant matter, then it's reasonable to conclude a Session has authority over whether worship will close with a benediction.[^fn-ga52-p890-n17]

Issue 2 questions whether this Session's constitutionally permitted exercise of discretion and judgment was a clear error of discretion and judgment. (BCO 39.3.3) We note first, regarding worship, both a Session and Presbytery have authority and duties under BCO 11-2.

[^fn-ga52-p890-n17]: BCO 58-1. "The Communion, or Supper of the Lord, is to be observed frequently; the stated times to be determined by the Session of each congregation, as it may judge most for edification."

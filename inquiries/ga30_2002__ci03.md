# 30-29 III — Procedure for Dealing with Alleged Conflicts Between Scripture and the Constitution of the PCA

*The CCB found Eastern Canada Presbytery's overture in conflict with the Constitution because it would allow a General Assembly to bypass constitutional processes and permit modification of commission reports.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 30th (2002)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 26-1, BCO 26-2, BCO 15-1, BCO 15-5  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga30_2002 lines 4658–4685](../markdown/ga30_2002.md#ga30-p102)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 27 from Eastern Canada Presbytery ("Add to BCO 26-1 Procedure for Dealing with Alleged Conflicts Between Scripture and the Constitution of the PCA") was in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. Specific areas of conflict included, but were not limited to the following: 1) the Overture would have allowed a General Assembly to avoid the provisions of the BCO without appropriate constitutional process and would have resulted in serious constitutional uncertainty (BCO 26-2), and 2) the Overture was in conflict with BCO 15.1 and BCO 15.5, in that it allowed for modification of a commission report.

**Key words:** BCO 26-1, BCO 26-2, BCO 15-1, BCO 15-5

**Inquiry from:** Eastern Canada Presbytery

### As referred / posed

The purpose of MNA Multicultural Ministries is to provide leadership and coordinate the resources necessary for PCA churches and presbyteries to start and develop healthy, multiplying, and reformed multicultural and crosscultural churches, which are part of church multiplication movements. The term multicultural is used by GA/MNA in two ways. (1) To describe the work of ethnic-specific church planting through which congregations are formed that are largely homogeneous (in most cases, the main homogeneous factor is that the group of people reached have a primary language other than English) and non-white; (2) To describe specific churches who include leaders and members from a variety of ethnic backgrounds, worshipping and serving God together.

Multicultural Advances in 2001

1. Ethnic and multicultural church planters continue to be assessed, placed, and coached in church planting. Most of GA/MNA's assessment centers held during 2001 saw more than 30% of the candidates having a multicultural focus.

2. There are more than 20 PCA churches and groups working with Hispanics around the PCA in various ministries; English as a Second Language is one of the primary service programs.

3. There are three organized Hispanic congregations in the PCA (Miami, Houston, McAllen TX).

4. There are currently twelve PCA Hispanic church plants in the United States. (Laredo, TX – 3; Miami, FL – 2; Alexandria, VA – 1; Austin, TX – 1; Escondido, CA – 1; El Paso, TX – 2; Birmingham, AL - 2).

### D. MNA CHAPLAIN MINISTRIES

“Those who know their God shall prove themselves strong, shall stand firm and do mighty things." Daniel 11:32

Core Values and Beliefs: GA/MNA Chaplain Ministries, in concert with the doctrinal teachings of the PCA, and in obedience to the Great Commission of our Lord, provides the organization, and thus the means, by which Teaching Elders are commissioned and sent to minister to personnel and family members within our government institutions (Military, Veteran Affairs, Law Enforcement Agencies, State and Federal Prisons). The Chaplaincy provides the PCA an opportunity and effective manner for patterning ministry after the Scriptural and apostolic model of taking the Gospel into the marketplace (Acts 17:17). Recently, in the January 2002 publication of Tabletalk, the devotional for the day of the 14th provided insight on Romans 1:14-15, "I am a debtor both to Greeks and to the barbarians, both to the wise and to the unwise." The editor writes, "Paul is telling the Romans that he is sent to all the world's peoples, cultured and uncultured, educated and non-educated, sophisticated and simple alike. The Gospel is for all of them, for 'God shows personal favoritism to no man' (Gal. 2:6). This emissary of the Gospel feels compelled to go to all classes of people. Clearly he sees his message as the Good News for all. The world is his field." This thought, along with many 577

Mission of MNA Chaplain Ministries: The Coordinator and Assistant Coordinator's mission, on behalf of the PCA, through GA/MNA is:

- **(1)** To recruit, ecclesiastically endorse and send Teaching Elders who are ordained to serve as Evangelists as they minister to personnel in our government institutions;

- **(2)** To oversee, encourage, supervise and train chaplains;

- **(3)** To pastor all PCA Chaplains and their family members;

- **(4)** To continually enhance the sense of "connectionalism" between chaplains and their respective presbyteries and thus, the denomination; and

- **(5)** To raise an annual budget of $175,000 for the purpose of supporting the mission of GA/MNA Chaplain Ministries.

Scriptural Purpose for the Chaplaincy:

- **(1)** Evangelism;

- **(2)** Pastoring, discipling and encouraging Christians;

- **(3)** To communicate a Christian world view/message to all people who are employed by our government institutions that will enable and motivate the hearer, thus the institution to "give reverence for the Holy Scriptures as God's Word, and to be received as the final instruction, source and norm for faith and life."

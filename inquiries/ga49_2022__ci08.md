# II.H — Change the Composition of the SJC by Amending BCO 15-4 and RAO 17-1; 3 Amend OMSJC 5.1 and 6.1

*CCB found no constitutional conflict in a Westminster Presbytery overture to change the membership composition of the Standing Judicial Commission.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 49th (2022)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 15-4, RAO 17-1  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga49_2022 minutes p.421 / PDF p.424](../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p421)*

---

## Verbatim record

H.** Overture 14 from Westminster Presbytery: “Change the Composition of the SJC by Amending BCO 15-4 and RAO 17-1; 3 Amend OMSJC 5.1 and 6.1”

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 14 is not in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. However, CCB notes that the final adoption of the amendment to BCO 15-4 proposed by the overture would need to occur before the adoption of the amendments to the RAO and OMSJC in order to avoid a conflict with the existing provisions of BCO 15-4. Additionally, the adoption of the changes proposed by the overture may warrant additional changes to the OMSJC with regard to numbers of members required for certain actions. Adopted by the CCB

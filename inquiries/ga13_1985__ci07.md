<h3 class="record-transcript-heading">Question and answer</h3>

7. Contitutional Inquiry 7: From the Session of Evangelical Presbyterian Church of Levittown, PA. See Recommendation 25. "Does the associate pastor, when he is not moderating Session, have a vote in that court?"

### ANSWER:

"The BCO 4-3, 12-1, 22-2 lists the member of Session which includes Pastors, Associate Pastors, and Ruling Elders. Every member of the Session, including the Moderator, is entitled to vote by virtue of his membership in the Session."

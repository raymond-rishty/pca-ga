<h3 class="record-transcript-heading">Question and answer</h3>

5. Constitutional Inquiry 15: From the Presbytery of the Western Carolinas. See Recommendation 45. (That) we request the Judicial Business Committee of the General Assembly to interpret BCO 15-2 as to whether or not commisions are limited to the five matters included in the first paragraph and to give an interpretation of the whole paragraph, including the meaning of "Among the matters...".

### ANSWER:

The language "Among the matters that may be properly executed..." provides a typical but not exhaustive list. Those items specifically listed may be assigned to commissions. Other matters may be assigned, with the understanding that the assignment to a commission will be subject to review and control.

# II.I — Amend BCO 7 by Addition to Disqualify Same-sex Attracted Men from Ordination

*An overture from Westminster Presbytery to amend BCO 7 and related provisions disqualifying same-sex attracted men from ordination was found in conflict and adopted.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 48th (2021)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 7, BCO 12-5.b, BCO 13-9.a, BCO 21-4, BCO 24-1, BCO 26-2  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted

*Source: [ga48_2021 minutes p.462 / PDF p.475](../markdown/ga48_2021.md#ga48-p475)*

---

## Verbatim record

I.** Overture 16 from Westminster Presbytery: "Amend BCO 7 by Addition to Disqualify Same-sex Attracted Men from Ordination" In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 23 is in conflict with BCO 12-5.b, 13-9.a, 21-4, and 24-1, which state it is the right and responsibility of the lower courts to determine if the candidate is qualified. 2. Adopted

# II.G — Amend BCO 22 to Affirm the 'Consent of the Governed' Regarding Assistant Pastors

*The CCB found the proposed amendment affirming congregational consent for assistant pastors ambiguous, as it did not address how current assistant pastors would be treated upon adoption.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 22  ·  **Disposition:** ambiguous (does not address treatment of current Assistant Pastors upon adoption); Adopted 7-0-1

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.253 / PDF p.255](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p255)*

---

## Verbatim record

G.** Overture 10 from the Central Florida Presbytery: “Amend BCO 22 to Affirm the Consent of the Governed’ Regarding Assistant Pastors"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 10 is ambiguous in that it does not address how to treat men who currently serve as Assistant Pastors when the amendment is adopted.

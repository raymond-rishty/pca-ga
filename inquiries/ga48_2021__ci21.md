# II.S — Amend BCO 31-2 to Describe Sequence of Discipline Investigation

*The CCB found proposed amendments to BCO 31-2 prescribing an investigation sequence to be in conflict at multiple points with existing BCO provisions, though the overture was adopted.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 48th (2021)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 31-2, BCO 32-19  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict at multiple points; Adopted

*Source: [ga48_2021 minutes p.464 / PDF p.477](../markdown/ga48_2021.md#ga48-p464)*

---

## Verbatim record

S.** Overture 28 from Philadelphia Metro West Presbytery: "Amend BCO 31-2 to Describe Sequence of Discipline Investigation" In the opinion of CCB, Overture 28 is in conflict at multiple points with the Constitution. These include but are not limited to a removal of the “great discretion” provided to courts in BCO 31-2; conflict with representation at the discretion of the accused (BCO 32-19); conflict with obtaining response from the accused that is not required; and conflation of later elements of the Rules of Discipline. Adopted

# 36-30 IV — Who May Be Licensed to Preach the Gospel

*The CCB stated that a man not a ruling or teaching elder and not pursuing eldership may nonetheless be licensed to preach per the plain wording of BCO 19-1.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 36th (2008)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 19-1  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga36_2008 lines 3528–3582](../markdown/ga36_2008.md#ga36-p72)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference about licensure by stating that a man who is neither a ruling elder nor a teaching elder nor a candidate for ministry and who is not pursuing a call to the office of eldership may be licensed to preach the gospel in accord with the clear wording of BCO 19-1. A Memorandum from Morton H. Smith is reproduced as an appendix with this reference.

**Key words:** BCO 19-1

**In the minutes:** [ga36_2008 p.72](../markdown/ga36_2008.md#ga36-p72)

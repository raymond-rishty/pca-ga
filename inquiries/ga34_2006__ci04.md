# 34-34 IV.C — Language in Which Session Records Can Be Written

*CCB advised that a presbytery may permit session records kept or translated into English when submitted, provided they meet BCO standards.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 34th (2006)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 12-7, BCO 13-9b, BCO 40-2  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga34_2006 lines 4113–4121](../markdown/ga34_2006.md#ga34-p82)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from the Presbytery of Southern Florida on whether sessions records must be originally recorded in English or can be translated into English when submitted to Presbytery, by stating that presbytery may adopt either of these procedures provided that the records are consistent with the standards in the BCO (BCO 12-7, 13-9b, 40-2).

**Key words:** BCO 12-7, BCO 13-9b, BCO 40-2

**Inquiry from:** Presbytery of Southern Florida

**In the minutes:** [ga34_2006 p.82](../markdown/ga34_2006.md#ga34-p82)

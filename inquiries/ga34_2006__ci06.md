# 34-34 IV.E — Views of the Reprobate in the WCF

*The CCB declined to answer a doctrinal question from Westminster Presbytery, stating that hypothetical issues of doctrine must be settled by adjudication.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 34th (2006)  ·  **Disposition:** advice given — hypothetical issues must be settled by adjudication

*Source: [ga34_2006 minutes p.82 / PDF p.84](../markdown/ga34_2006.md#ga34-p84)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

The CCB answered a non-judicial reference from Westminster Presbytery, on whether it is in accord with the Westminster Standards to hold and teach the view that the reprobate is in covenant with God, by stating that hypothetical issues of doctrine must be settled by adjudication or determination by the appropriate court.

**Inquiry from:** Westminster Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga34_2006 minutes p.82 / PDF p.84](../markdown/ga34_2006.md#ga34-p84)

---

## Verbatim record

Non-judicial Reference 2 from Westminster Presbytery: "Is it in accord with the Westminster Standards to hold and teach the view that the reprobate, who is a member of the visible church, is in covenant (of grace) with God?" It is the opinion of the CCB that hypothetical issues of doctrine are beyond our authority to answer and must be settled either by adjudication or determination by the appropriate court. Adopted by the CCB

# II.H — 43-2, and 43-3 regarding Method and Deadlines of Filing Cases

*An overture amending BCO 42-4, 43-2, and 43-3 to update methods and deadlines for filing judicial cases was found not in conflict and adopted by the CCB.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 42-4, BCO 43-2, BCO 43-3  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga47_2019 minutes p.399 / PDF p.401](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p401)*

---

## Verbatim record

H.** Overture 9 from Platte Valley Presbytery: “Amend BCO 42-4, 43-2, and 43-3 regarding Method and Deadlines of Filing Cases"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 9 is not in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. While both Overtures 9 and 34 are not in conflict with the Constitution, they are in conflict with each other and only one can be adopted.

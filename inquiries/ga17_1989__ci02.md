# 17-82 III, 8 — BCO 10-3 procedures and presbytery meetings

*The CCB said BCO 10-3 is ambiguous about moderator absence, so each presbytery should interpret it as it sees best. It also said BCO 10-3 does not require a sermon at presbytery meetings and BCO 13-4 requires meetings at an appointed place, ruling out telephone meetings.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 17th (1989)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 10-3, BCO 13-4  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga17_1989 pp. 151–152](../markdown/ga17_1989.md#ga17-p153)*

---

## Digest headnote

The 16th General Assembly assigned the Committee on Judicial Business questions from the Review and Control Committee about how BCO 10-3 applies to presbytery meetings: moderator absence, whether a sermon is required at each meeting, and whether it must be preached by the moderator. The committee also considered whether a presbytery may meet by conference telephone call.

**Key words:** BCO 10-3, BCO 13-4

**Inquiry from:** Review and Control Committee

**In the minutes:** [ga17_1989 p.151](../markdown/ga17_1989.md#ga17-p153)

---

## Verbatim record

### As referred / posed

8. That the advice of the Committee on Judicial Business regarding Assignment #8 be ratified as amended; and that the Committee on Judicial Business be instructed to draft an amendment to BCO 10-3 to clarify the matter related to “Question a.” under ADVICE. The 16th General Assembly assigned to this Committee a request from the Review and Control Committee on the operation of BCO 10-3 and the conduct of Presbytery meetings. The specific questions asked were: “a. Do the last two sentences of BCO 10-3, paragraph 1, apply to presbyteries? In particular:

- **a)** Do they establish a procedure which the presbytery must follow when the moderator is absent?

- **b)** Do they require that a sermon be preached at each meeting (stated or called) of presbytery?

- **c)** If a sermon is required, must the sermon be preached by the Moderator?” “b. Is it proper for a meeting of presbytery to be conducted by conference telephone call?”

### CCB advice

Concerning “Question a.”: As BCO 10-3 is ambiguous with regard to this question, each Presbytery should interpret the section as it sees best.

Concerning “Question b.”: No. BCO 13-4 requires that the Presbytery meet at the “place appointed.” Adopted

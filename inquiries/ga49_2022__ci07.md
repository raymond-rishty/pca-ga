# II.G — Amend BCO 16 by Adding a New Paragraph BCO 16-4 with Wording from the Report of the Ad Interim Committee on Human Sexuality

*CCB found no constitutional conflict in an overture incorporating the Ad Interim Committee on Human Sexuality's language as a new paragraph BCO 16-4.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 49th (2022)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 16-4  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga49_2022 minutes p.421 / PDF p.424](../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p424)*

## Verbatim record

G.** Overture 12 from Hills and Plains Presbytery: “Amend BCO 16 by Adding a New Paragraph BCO 16-4 with Wording from the Report of the Ad Interim Committee on Human Sexuality" In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 12 is not in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. CCB notes that Overtures 23, 20, and 29 contain similar language to amend BCO 16-4. Adopted by the CCB

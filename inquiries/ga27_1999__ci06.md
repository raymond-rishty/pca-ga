# 27-43 III — Distinction between Suspension from Office and from Sacraments

*A proposed overture adding two criteria beyond BCO 37-3 for removing indefinite suspension was in conflict with the Constitution.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 27th (1999)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 37-3  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga27_1999 minutes p.146 / PDF p.148](../markdown/ga27_1999.md#ga27-p146)*

---

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 12 from Pittsburgh Presbytery ("Clarify Distinction between Suspension from Office and from Sacraments") was in conflict with the Constitution (BCO 37-3) in that it added to the criterion for the removal of indefinite suspension two additional criteria.

**Key words:** BCO 37-3

**Inquiry from:** Pittsburgh Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga27_1999 minutes p.146 / PDF p.148](../markdown/ga27_1999.md#ga27-p146)

Response:
It is the opinion of the Committee that the overture is in conflict with the
Constitution (BCO 37-3) in that it adds to the criterion for the removal of indefinite
suspension two additional criteria -- 1, "has not yet made such restitution as is
appropriate", and 2, "for whom the court believes a probationary period is warranted";
conflict would be eliminated if these criteria were added to 37-3, distinguishing
carefully between removal of indefinite suspension from office and from the
sacraments.

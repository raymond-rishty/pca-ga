# Supplemental III.1 — Whether Committees of Commissioners may nominate a substitute program coordinator

*CCB ruled that a Committee of Commissioners may only approve or disapprove nominees for program coordinator, and has no authority to nominate substitutes.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 49th (2022)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 4-9, RAO 20  ·  **Disposition:** CoC may only approve or disapprove, not nominate substitute; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga49_2022 minutes p.429 / PDF p.432](../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p432)*

---

## Verbatim record

It is the opinion of the CCB that Committees of Commissioners (CoC) may only approve or disapprove a Permanent Committee's recommendation for coordinator, but may not nominate a substitute coordinator (RAO 4-9). CCB also notes that the Assembly is free to suspend its rules according to RAO 20 to receive a nomination for a program coordinator from a CoC. Adopted by the CCB

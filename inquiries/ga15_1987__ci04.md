# 15-83 III, 10 — Emeritus officers voting in higher courts and local bodies

*The CCB affirmed that an Elder Emeritus may not vote in the local session but may vote as a delegate to Presbytery if appointed to serve there.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 15th (1987)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 24-9  ·  **Disposition:** advice given

*Source: [ga15_1987 lines 10350–10393](../markdown/ga15_1987.md#ga15-p170)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

Constitutional Inquiry #4. From Town North Presbyterian Church, Richardson, TX, regarding the status of emeritus officers. 'Attached are two letters between David Clelland and Morton Smith on the interpretation of the BCO (sic) 24-9. We request you confirm the following points of their interpretation or instruct us accordingly. 1. BCO 24-9 prohibits the Elder or Deacon Emeritus from voting in his local Session or Diaconate. 2. BCO 24-9 permits him to perform certain duties on a voluntary basis if requested by the Session or a higher court. If such service is requested and agreeable then the Emeritus officer serves with the power to vote in the higher court or board which he has been requested to serve. (This in no way restores any right to vote in the local Session or Diaconate.) For example, an Elder Emeritus can serve as a voting delegate to Presbytery.' ANSWER: '1. Affirmed. 2. Yes.' Previous actions of the Assembly on this matter noted, including the grandfather clause resolution adopted at M12GA.

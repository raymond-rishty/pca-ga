# II.P — Change the Prohibition Against 'Interlocutory Appeal' by Complaint in BCO 43-1

*The CCB found the proposed overture to change BCO 43-1's prohibition against interlocutory appeals by complaint to be internally inconsistent and in conflict with existing BCO provisions.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 51st (2024)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 43-1, BCO 43-2  ·  **Disposition:** internally inconsistent and in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga51_2024 minutes p.227 / PDF p.230](../markdown/ga51_2024.md#ga51-p230)*

---

## Verbatim record

P.** Overture 21 from Central Indiana Presbytery: "Change the Prohibition Against Interlocutory Appeal’ by Complaint in BCO 43-1”

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 21 is internally inconsistent insofar as it prohibits a complaint after process has commenced while simultaneously describing a procedure for adjudication of a complaint after process has commenced. Further, Overture 21 is in conflict with the existing language of BCO 43-2 insofar as it creates conflicting requirements regarding the timeliness for consideration of the complaint. Adopted

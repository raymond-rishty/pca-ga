# VII — SJC proposed amendment to RAO 17-3

*The CCB found the SJC's proposed amendment to RAO 17-3 to be not in conflict with the BCO, and the Assembly adopted it unanimously.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 17-3  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

## Verbatim record

And it matters little whether this Complainant or his Session believed the Scriptures require a benediction to be pronounced at the close of worship. For example, I could vote in favor of a motion even though I disagree with the reasons given by the maker. The action proposed could still make sense. While a closing benediction is not a Scriptural requirement, that Session's authority to choose that option still prevails, unless it's demonstrated to violate Scripture or is a clear error of judgment—neither of which was demonstrable. BCO 58- 7 ends this way: "[after the Lord's Supper]... Now let a psalm or hymn be sung, and the congregation dismissed, with the following or some other Gospel benediction." (emphasis added)

<a id="ga52-p895"></a>

There are numerous ways a worship service could "end"—some wise; some not so. A Session might decide that every worship service will close with:

1. whatever the minister chooses
2. whatever benediction the minister chooses

# Supplemental II.B — Advice whether RPR's proposed amendment to RAO 16-4c is in conflict with the Constitution

*CCB advised that the Review of Presbytery Records Committee's proposed change to RAO 16-4c is not in conflict with the Constitution.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 49th (2022)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 16-4c  ·  **Disposition:** not in conflict; Adopted by the CCB

*Source: [ga49_2022 minutes p.428 / PDF p.431](../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p428)*

## Verbatim record

Advice was requested concerning a change to RAO 16-4c proposed by RPR.

In the opinion of the CCB, the proposed change to RAO 16-4c is not in conflict with other portions of the constitution. Adopted by the CCB

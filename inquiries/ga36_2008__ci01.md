# 36-30 II.B — Affirmation of Apostles' Creed for Membership

*A Southeast Alabama overture requiring Apostles' Creed affirmation for membership was in conflict because 'profession of faith' in the BCO denotes commitment to Christ, not subscription to a doctrinal statement.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 36th (2008)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 57-5  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga36_2008 minutes p.68 / PDF p.68](../markdown/ga36_2008.md#ga36-p68)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 2 from Southeast Alabama Presbytery ("Amend BCO 57-5 to Require Affirmation of the Apostles' Creed for Membership") was in conflict with other parts of the Constitution in that the term "profession of faith" as used throughout the BCO refers to commitment to Christ as Savior rather than subscription to a system of doctrine.

**Key words:** BCO 57-5

**Inquiry from:** Southeast Alabama Presbytery

**In the minutes:** [ga36_2008 minutes p.68 / PDF p.68](../markdown/ga36_2008.md#ga36-p68)

## Verbatim record

Overture 2 from Southeast Alabama Presbytery: “Amend BCO 57-5 to Require Affirmation of the Apostles' Creed for Membership"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 2 is in conflict with other parts of the Constitution in that the term "profession of faith" as used throughout the BCO refers to commitment to Christ as Savior rather than subscription to a system of doctrine. For example, BCO 2-1 refers to the Visible Church as consisting of "those who make profession of their faith in the Lord Jesus Christ," and BCO 6-2 refers to communing members as “those who have made a profession of faith in Christ." Adopted by the CCB

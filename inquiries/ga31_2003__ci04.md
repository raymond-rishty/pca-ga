# 31-56 IV — Exceptions to Westminster Standards or BCO Recorded in Presbytery Minutes

*The CCB answered no: neither the PCA constitution nor the RAO then required a presbytery to record a minister's exceptions to the Westminster Standards or BCO in its minutes.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Constitutional inquiry  ·  **Assembly:** 31st (2003)  ·  **Provisions:** RAO 14-3e.5, RAO 16-3.e.5  ·  **Disposition:** advice given — no requirement

*Source: [ga31_2003 minutes p.159 / PDF p.161](../markdown/ga31_2003.md#ga31-p161)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

- The CCB answered a constitutional inquiry from the 30th General Assembly, which asked "Does either the Constitution of the PCA or the RAO presently require a presbytery to record a minister's exceptions to the WCF, WLC, WSC or BCO in its minutes?" In the opinion of the CCB, the answer was "no." See 2003, p. 180, 31-57, III.4.a, for action to amend RAO 14-3e.5 [now 16-3.e.5].

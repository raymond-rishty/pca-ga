## Minutes transcript

Constitutional Inquiries Inquiry 1 – Referral by 30th General Assembly [M30GA, 30-64, p. 342] The Assembly referred the following to the Committee: Question: Does either the Constitution of the PCA or the "RAO" presently require a presbytery to record a minister's exceptions to the WCF, WLC, WSC or BCO in its minutes? Response: No Adopted by CCB

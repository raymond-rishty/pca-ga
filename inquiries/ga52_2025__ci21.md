# II.U — Amend BCO 36 to Provide for Elevation of a Censure without an Entirely New Trial

*Proposed amendment to BCO 36 providing for censure elevation without a wholly new trial was found to be ambiguous under the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 36, BCO 32-10, BCO 32-13, BCO 32-15, BCO 32-16, BCO 32-17, BCO 32-18, BCO 35, BCO 37-7  ·  **Disposition:** ambiguous; Adopted 8-0-0

## Verbatim record

### CASE SUMMARY

By a vote of 5-3, a Session adopted a policy that worship services should close with a benediction and not a doxology. Complainant RE Martinez voted in favor. The long-time Senior Pastor did not agree with that decision, and two Session members filed a complaint with the Session, which was denied. Then, over the course of several meetings, Presbytery alternately sustained, denied, and sustained complaints related to this Session decision, with the final Presbytery decision being that the Session erred in its decision.

The SJC concludes that the Session had the authority to make that decision (BCO 39-3.4), and that the liturgical decision was not a clear error of judgment or discretion (BCO 39-3.3), and therefore the Complaint is sustained, and Presbytery's action is annulled in whole.

### I. SUMMARY OF THE FACTS

07/11/22 Stated meeting of the Session of Valley Presbyterian Church (hereafter "VPC") at which by vote of 5-3, the Session, approved a motion "to have God's blessings as formal benedictions to conclude the service and not doxologies."

08/09/22 Session members RE Riedinger and Shaw and filed a timely BCO 43-1 Complaint with the Session regarding that decision.[^fn-ga52-p885-n15]

09/08/22 Session denied the Reidinger/Shaw Complaint.

10/05/22 REs Reidinger and Shaw took that Complaint to Presbytery.

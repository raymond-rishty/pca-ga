# II.S — 20-1, 21-1, 21-12, and 23-1 re the Calling and Dissolution of TE Relationships for Needful Work

*Proposed amendments to BCO 8-4, 20-1, 21-1, 21-12, and 23-1 regarding TE relationships for needful work were found in conflict with and ambiguous under the BCO.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 8-4, BCO 20-1, BCO 21-1, BCO 21-12, BCO 23-1, BCO 13-2, BCO 13-5  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict (and ambiguous); Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga52_2025 minutes p.256 / PDF p.258](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p258)*

---

## Verbatim record

S.** Overture 30 from the Houston Metro Presbytery: “Amend BCO 8-4, 20-1, 21-1, 21-12, and 23-1 re the Calling and Dissolution of TE Relationships for Needful Work"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 30 is in conflict with our Constitution in that it purports to give Presbyteries authority over the installation of Teaching Elders to calls outside of churches of the Presbytery and the PCA as well as the dissolution of such calls.

Additionally, in the opinion of the CCB, Overture 30 is ambiguous regarding the relation between a “needful work" (BCO 8-4) and “a work not under the jurisdiction of his Presbytery" (BCO 13-2; cf. BCO 13-5), which is often called an “out of bounds call." It is unclear whether a “needful work" encompasses all “out of bounds calls," or whether there is still a category of “out of bounds calls" that are different from “needful works." Adopted

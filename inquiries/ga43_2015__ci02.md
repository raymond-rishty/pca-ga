# App. O II.E — Mandating That Those Without Call Report Annually to Presbytery

*An overture mandating annual presbytery reporting for officers without call was in conflict with the Constitution because the new BCO 34-10 language conflicted with BCO 42-2.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 43rd (2015)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 13-2, BCO 34-10, BCO 24-7, BCO 24-9, BCO 42-2  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict

*Source: [ga43_2015 minutes p.372 / PDF p.374](../markdown/ga43_2015.md#ga43-p372)*

## Digest headnote
*Editorial summary from the PCA Digest, Part II (Interpretations of the Constitution) — this is the Digest's wording, not the verbatim minutes. The authoritative text is the verbatim record below / linked above.*

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 8 from Tidewater Presbytery ("Amend BCO 13-2, 34-10, 24-7 and 24-9 regarding Ministers, Ruling Elders, and Deacons without Call") was, as written, in conflict with the Constitution. The insertion of the new language for BCO 34-10 left a conflict with BCO 42-2.

## Verbatim record

Overture 8 from Tidewater Presbytery: “Amend BCO 13-2, 34-10, 24-7 and 24-9 regarding Ministers, Ruling Elders, and Deacons without Call"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 8 is, as written, in conflict with the Constitution. The insertion of the new language for BCO 34-10 now leaves a conflict with BCO 42-2. The current version of BCO 34-10 includes the phrase, "he may appeal, as if he had been tried after the usual forms." BCO 42-2 limits appeal to those who have submitted to trial and without the current qualifying phrase in BCO 34-10, the proposed overture stands in conflict with the Constitution. Adopted by the CCB

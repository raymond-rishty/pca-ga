## Minutes transcript

10. That Reference 3 (Refer p. 50) which reads as follows: The Session of Vineville Presbyterian Church at its stated November 1981, meeting requested that the General Assembly's Judicial Committee provide an interpretation of 24-3 and 24-4 of the Book of Church Order with respect to what constitutes a voter (one who casts a vote for one or more candidates or one who casts a blank ballot). be answered with the following advice: A person eligible to vote is defined in BCO 24-3 as a communing member in good and regular standing who is present at the congregational meeting called to elect officers. A majority vote of the voters present is required to elect. Grounds: This recommendation is a ratification of the answer already given by the Permanent Sub- Committee on Judical Business. Adopted

<h3 class="record-transcript-heading">Question and answer</h3>

12. Constitutional Inquiry (appended to Case 4). From the Great Lakes Presbytery. See Recommendation 20.

"The Appellant.... Is he divested from office or is that action of Presbytery suspended until the General Assembly's Commission has acted?"

### ANSWER

"Yes the judgment is suspended, unless the court for sufficient reasons, which are duly recorded,
by separate action puts the censure into effect.

Grounds: See BCO 30-1, 30-5, 34-10, and 42-6."

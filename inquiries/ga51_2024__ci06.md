# II.E — Amend BCO Sections to Require Background Checks for Church Office

*Overture to amend BCO 13-6 requiring background checks for church officers was found in conflict with existing BCO provisions but adopted 6-1-1.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 51st (2024)  ·  **Provisions:** WCF 23.3, BCO Preliminary Principle II, BCO 13-6  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted 6-1-1

*Source: [ga51_2024 minutes p.224 / PDF p.227](../markdown/ga51_2024.md#ga51-p224)*

---

## Verbatim record

Overture 6 from the Presbytery of Susquehanna Valley: "Amend BCO Sections to Require Background Checks for Church Office"

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 6, particularly the language “shall obtain and review," is in conflict with (1) WCF 23.3 because, insofar as it adds a requirement for Presbytery to obtain something that the civil magistrate might withhold or abolish, it cedes to the civil magistrate an effective authority he may not assume, namely, the right to deny admittance to the body of Christ and (2) BCO Preliminary Principle II in that it deprives members of the church of the right to determine who shall be admitted to its communion.

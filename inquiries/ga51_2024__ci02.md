# II.AA — Amend BCO 23 to Address Dissolution of Call for those employed by a Committee or Agency

*The CCB found the proposed amendment to BCO 23 addressing dissolution of call for committee- or agency-employed ministers in conflict with BCO 11-4 and BCO 23.*

**Body:** Committee on Constitutional Business (CCB)  ·  **Type:** Overture/amendment advice  ·  **Assembly:** 51st (2024)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 11-4, BCO 23  ·  **Disposition:** in conflict; Adopted 8-0-0

*Source: [ga51_2024 minutes p.229 / PDF p.232](../markdown/ga51_2024.md#ga51-p229)*

---

## Verbatim record

AA. Overture 32 from the Presbytery of Eastern Pennsylvania: "Amend BCO 23 to Address Dissolution of Call for those employed by a Committee or Agency"

229

<a id="ga51-p233"></a>
<!-- PAGE ga=51 pdf_page=233 printed_page=230 -->

In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 32 is in conflict with BCO 11-4 because  
the proposed amendment requires a presbytery to cite a committee or agency  
of a higher court.  
Adopted 8-0-0

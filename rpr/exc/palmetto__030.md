# Palmetto Presbytery — BCO 13-9.b

*No record of all the church Session records being…*

**Presbytery:** Palmetto  ·  **First raised:** 48th (2021)  ·  **Final disposition:** satisfactory (closed)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 13-9.b

**Lifecycle:** raised (48th) → satisfactory (49th)

---

## Raised — 48th General Assembly (2021)
*[ga48_2021 p.598](../../markdown/ga48_2021.md#ga48-p598)*

**Exception: Nov 12, 2020** ( _BCO_ 13-9.b) – No record of all the
church Session records being reviewed.

## Response found satisfactory — 49th General Assembly (2022)
*[ga49_2022 p.604](../../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p604)*

**Exception: Nov 12, 2020** ( _BCO_ 13-9.b) – No record of all the church
Session records being reviewed.

**Response:** Palmetto Presbytery has requested all the churches to
submit their session records for Presbytery examination, but some
churches have not responded in a timely way, and others not at all.
Presbytery admits the failure and will give greater effort in motivating
churches to submit their records annually.

---

## Raised — 46th General Assembly (2018)
*[ga46_2018 p.371](../../markdown/ga46_2018.md#ga46-p371)*

**Exception: General** ( _BCO_ 13-9b) – No record of review of session
records.

## Response found satisfactory — 47th General Assembly (2019)
*[ga47_2019 p.455](../../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p455)*

**Exception: General** ( _BCO_ 13-9b) – No record of review of session
records.

**Response:** The Presbytery agrees with the exception. In 2018 all
session records but two were reviewed. A workshop was held in
March of 2018 to help sessions with these records. An experienced
stated clerk in Presbytery has volunteered to help any local church
with their record keeping.

# Southeast Alabama Presbytery — BCO 23-1)

*There is no record of a congregational meeting of Woodland Presbyterian Church…*

**Presbytery:** Southeast Alabama  ·  **First raised:** 25th (1997)  ·  **Final disposition:** satisfactory (closed)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 23-1)

**Lifecycle:** raised (25th) → satisfactory (26th)

---

## Raised — 25th General Assembly (1997)
*[ga25_1997 p.217](../../markdown/ga25_1997.md#ga25-p217)*

Exception: There is no record of a congregational meeting of Woodland Presbyterian Church giving their agreement with the dissolution of the pastoral relation (BCO 23-1).

## Response found satisfactory — 26th General Assembly (1998)
*[ga26_1998 p.251](../../markdown/ga26_1998.md#ga26-p251)*

Exception: There is no record of a congregational meeting of Woodland
presbyterian church giving their agreement with the dissolution of the
pastoral relation (BCO 23-1).

Response: Presbytery will be careful to record that the congregation has
concurred in the Pastor’s request for the dissolution of the pastoral
relationship

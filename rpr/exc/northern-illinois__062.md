# Northern Illinois Presbytery — BCO 8-7

*No record that the Presbytery is assured that an out-of-bounds TE will have…*

**Presbytery:** Northern Illinois  ·  **First raised:** 51st (2024)  ·  **Final disposition:** satisfactory (closed)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 8-7

**Lifecycle:** raised (51st) → satisfactory (52nd)

---

## Raised — 51st General Assembly (2024)
*[ga51_2024 p.604](../../markdown/ga51_2024.md#ga51-p604)*

**2024-4: Jan 14, 2023** ( _BCO_ 8-7) — No record that the Presbytery is
assured that an out-of-bounds TE will have full freedom to maintain

and teach the doctrine of our Church.

## Response found satisfactory — 52nd General Assembly (2025)
*[ga52_2025 p.647](../../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p647)*

**2024-04: Jan 14, 2023** ( _BCO_ 8-7) — No record that the
Presbytery is assured that an out-of-bounds TE will have full
freedom to maintain and teach the doctrine of our Church.

**Response:** Presbytery agrees with the exception and
corrects its record. Presbytery had, in fact, received

correspondence from Interim Pastors Ministries that TE

[Name Omitted] would have full freedom to maintain and
teach the doctrine of our Church. That correspondence should
have been recorded; the record will be corrected to reflect
IPM’s correspondence.

or dismissal of members after dissolution of a church.

**Response:** On 8/22/16, I contacted [name omitted] ([church name
omitted]’s pastor) to learn what actions we did take with [church name
omitted]’s members. He could recall where some of the members
transferred to but could not recall what specific actions the presbytery
took to transfer and dismiss members of [church name omitted] after
its dissolution.

Presbytery agrees with the exception, corrects its actions and
promises to be more careful in the future regarding the transfer or
dismissal of members after dissolution of a church.

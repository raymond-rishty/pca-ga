# Western Canada Presbytery — BCO 13-9, BCO 3-1

*presbytery may not appoint a session fora church without congregational…*

**Presbytery:** Western Canada  ·  **First raised:** 38th (2010)  ·  **Final disposition:** satisfactory (closed)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 13-9, BCO 3-1

**Lifecycle:** raised (38th) → satisfactory (39th)

---

## Raised — 38th General Assembly (2010)
*[ga38_2010 p.119](../../markdown/ga38_2010.md#ga38-p119)*

**Exception: March 6-7, 2009:** _BCO_ 13-9, _BCO_ 3-1 and Preface II.6 –
presbytery may not appoint a session fora church without
congregational approval prior to action.

## Response found satisfactory — 39th General Assembly (2011)
*[ga39_2011 p.490](../../markdown/ga39_2011.md#ga39-p490)*

**Exception: March 6-7, 2009:** _BCO_ 13-9, _BCO_ 3-1 and Preface II.6 –
presbytery may not appoint a session fora church without
congregational approval prior to action.
_**Response:**_ _Presbytery agrees with the exception and promises to be_
_more careful in the future in provision of temporary governance in the_
_event of the resignation of all the REs from a Session._

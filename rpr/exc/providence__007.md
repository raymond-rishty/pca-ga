# Providence Presbytery — BCO 5-9.2

*No record that organizing commission examined ruling elders before their…*

**Presbytery:** Providence  ·  **First raised:** 39th (2011)  ·  **Final disposition:** satisfactory (closed)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 5-9.2

**Lifecycle:** raised (39th) → unsatisfactory (40th) → raised (41st) → satisfactory (41st) → unsatisfactory (42nd) → satisfactory (43rd)

---

## Raised — 39th General Assembly (2011)
*[ga39_2011 p.479](../../markdown/ga39_2011.md#ga39-p479)*

**Exception: November 9, 2010** ( _BCO_ 5-9.2) – No record that
organizing commission examined ruling elders before their election

## Response found unsatisfactory — 40th General Assembly (2012)
*[ga40_2012 p.469](../../markdown/ga40_2012.md#ga40-p469)*

**Exception: November 9, 2010** ( _BCO_ 5-9.2) – No record that
organizing commission examined ruling elders before their election.

## Raised — 41st General Assembly (2013)
*[ga41_2013 p.471](../../markdown/ga41_2013.md#ga41-p471)*

**Exception: November 9, 2010** ( _BCO_ 5-9.2) – No record that
organizing commission examined ruling elders before their election.

## Response found satisfactory — 41st General Assembly (2013)
*[ga41_2013 p.472](../../markdown/ga41_2013.md#ga41-p472)*

**Exception: November 9, 2010** ( _BCO_ 5-9.2) – No record that
organizing commission examined ruling elders before their election.

**Response:** The ruling elders were examined but it was not recorded.
In the future we will be more careful to note this.

## Response found unsatisfactory — 42nd General Assembly (2014)
*[ga42_2014 p.448](../../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p448)*

**Exception: November 9, 2010** ( _BCO_ 5-9.2) – No record that
organizing commission examined ruling elders before their election.

## Response found satisfactory — 43rd General Assembly (2015)
*[ga43_2015 p.467](../../markdown/ga43_2015.md#ga43-p467)*

**Exception: November 9, 2010** ( _BCO_ 5-9.2) – No record that
organizing commission examined ruling elders before their election.

**Response:** This was an unintentional oversight on our part. The
organizing commission did examine the ruling elders in accordance

with _BCO_ 5-9, but it was not properly recorded. We apologize for
this oversight and endeavor to be more careful in the future.

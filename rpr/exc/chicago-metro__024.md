# Chicago Metro Presbytery — BCO 13-6, BCO 21-4

*Incomplete ordination exam of minister transferring from another…*

**Presbytery:** Chicago Metro  ·  **First raised:** 42nd (2014)  ·  **Final disposition:** satisfactory (closed)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 13-6, BCO 21-4

**Lifecycle:** satisfactory (42nd)

---

## Response found satisfactory — 42nd General Assembly (2014)
*[ga42_2014 p.402](../../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p402)*

**Exception:** **July 20, 2011** ( _BCO_ 13-6 and _BCO_ 21-4) – Incomplete
ordination exam of minister transferring from another denomination

**Response:** Regarding the minister described having not responded in
the affirmative to the eight ordination vows in _BCO_ 21-4: after
having met with the minister in question, the chairman of the
Shepherding Committee and a member of the Candidates and
Credentials committee, the questions in _BCO_ 21-4 were asked of the
minister and he responded in the affirmative.
This was a failure of notation by the clerk, the presbytery asks
that the exam and transfer be sustained and this response be found
satisfactory.

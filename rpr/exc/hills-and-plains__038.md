# Hills and Plains Presbytery — BCO 36-1

*Minutes record the expiration of one-year definite suspension from office, yet…*

**Lifecycle:** raised (48th) → satisfactory (49th)

## Raised — 48th General Assembly (2021)
*[ga48_2021 p.560](../../markdown/ga48_2021.md#ga48-p560)*

**Exception: May 5, 2020** ( _BCO_ 36-1; 13-11) – Minutes record the
expiration of one-year definite suspension from office, yet there is
no indication in the previous year’s minutes of any trial or case
without process.

## Response found satisfactory — 49th General Assembly (2022)
*[ga49_2022 p.530](../../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p530)*

expiration of one-year definite suspension from office, yet there is no
indication in the previous year’s minutes of any trial or case without

process.

indicated. Far stricter record keeping has been instituted since this
time.

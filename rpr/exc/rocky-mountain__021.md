# Rocky Mountain Presbytery — BCO 38-2, BCO 46-8

*January 27, 2011 ( BCO 38-2 and BCO 46-8) Two TEs demitted the office but…*

**Lifecycle:** raised (40th) → satisfactory (41st)

## Raised — 40th General Assembly (2012)

**Exception:** **January 27, 2011** ( _BCO_ 38-2 and _BCO_ 46-8) Two TEs
demitted the office but neither was assigned membership in the local
church.

promises to do better in the future.

## Response found satisfactory — 37th General Assembly (2009)
*[ga37_2009 p.84](../../markdown/ga37_2009.md#ga37-p84)*

**Exception: January 16, 2007** : _BCO_ 13-11. Inaccurate citation of
_BCO_ 5-3.2 to justify a “loaner” session.

**Response:** Minutes cite _BCO_ 5.3.2 Naperville Presbyterian Church to
serve as a loaner session was inaccurate and should have been noted

as “temporary governing body.” Christ church was formerly a church
plant of NPC; during the time of reorganization NPC was established
by Chicago Metro presbytery for having oversight ( _BCO_ 5.3.2).
Please accept this response to the exception noted.

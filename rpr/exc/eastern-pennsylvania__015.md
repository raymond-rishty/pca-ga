# Eastern Pennsylvania Presbytery — BCO 23-2

*A TE cannot simultaneously be honorably retired and an Associate Pastor ( BCO…*

**Presbytery:** Eastern Pennsylvania  ·  **First raised:** 48th (2021)  ·  **Final disposition:** satisfactory (closed)  ·  **Provisions:** BCO 23-2

**Lifecycle:** raised (48th) → satisfactory (49th)

---

## Raised — 48th General Assembly (2021)
*[ga48_2021 p.552](../../markdown/ga48_2021.md#ga48-p552)*

**Exception: Apr 9, 2019** ( _BCO_ 23-2) – A TE cannot simultaneously
be honorably retired and an Associate Pastor ( _BCO_ 23-2).

## Response found satisfactory — 49th General Assembly (2022)
*[ga49_2022 p.518](../../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p518)*

**Exception: Apr 9, 2019** ( _BCO_ 23-2) – A TE cannot simultaneously
be honorably retired and an Associate Pastor ( _BCO_ 23-2).

**Response:** In light of the TE in question serving as Associate Pastor,
the Presbytery has rescinded its action of April 9, 2019 to approve his
status as “honorably retired.

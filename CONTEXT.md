# PCA General Assembly Judicial Cases

The canonical language for describing judicial matters decided or handled by
the PCA General Assembly, its Standing Judicial Commission, and its earlier
judicial bodies.

## Language

**Matter type**: The procedural vehicle by which a matter comes before the
General Assembly or its judicial body, such as a complaint, appeal, judicial
reference, original-jurisdiction request, BCO 40-5 matter, or other review and
control matter.
_Avoid_: Proceeding type, case type, or subject matter when referring to the
procedural vehicle.

**Final disposition**: One or more source-supported terminal rulings or actions
in a matter. Multiple dispositions may coexist, as when an appeal is sustained
and remanded or a complaint is found judicially out of order and dismissed.
_Avoid_: Outcome when it would collapse distinct rulings or remedies into one
label.

**Review basis**: The source-stated basis governing how the higher court reviews
a particular issue. A matter may have more than one review basis when different
issues are governed by different provisions.
_Avoid_: Review standard when used as a second, redundant metadata field.

**Editorial summary**: A concise, source-grounded headnote stating the dispute,
the court's action, and the decisive reason or limitation. It does not replace
the official decision text.
_Avoid_: Case text or holding when referring to editorial prose.

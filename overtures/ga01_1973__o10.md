# GA1 O10 — Keep Presbytery Size Small Enough for Full Representation and Fellowship

**Assembly:** 1st (1973)  ·  **Source:** Covenant Presbytery  ·  **Disposition:** Adopted (final)

*Source: [ga01_1973 p. 19](../markdown/ga01_1973.md#ga01-p19)*

---

## Submitted overture

"That the General Assembly seek to keep, both now and in the future, the size of our presbyteries —both geographically and numerically —small enough so that every church and minister may be represented at all meetings, and so that general fellowship among members may be fully realized.”

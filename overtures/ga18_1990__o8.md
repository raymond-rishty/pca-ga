# GA18 O8 — Keep the Title ‘Coordinator’ and Specify Its Usage

**Assembly:** 18th (1990)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 8 From New River Presbytery (to AC)

*Source: [ga18_1990 PDF p. 106](../markdown/ga18_1990.md#ga18-p106)*

---

## Submitted overture

That the Presbytery advise the General Assembly of its concern regarding the amendment of BCO 14-1-14 deleting the term 'coordinator' and request that the General Assembly proceed to amend it's constitution in some manner to retain and specify its usage.

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Administrative Committee recommends answering Overture 8 by reference to GA17 action</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
8. That Overture 8 to &quot;Retain the Term Coordinator&quot;...be answered by reference to 17-70, III, 24, p. 124 (17 thGA Minutes) which did not remove the term &#x27;coordinator&#x27; but did allow that &quot;In communications and relationships with non- PCA organizations a Coordinator may use the title &#x27;Executive Director&#x27;. This title does not convey any additional rights or privileges to the office of Coordinator, but only provides for the use of a term readily understood by organizations outside the PCA.&quot;
</blockquote>

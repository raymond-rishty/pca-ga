# GA47 O39 — Re-commend Previous PCA Statements and Study Reports on Homosexuality

**Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Source:** Southern New England Presbytery (to OC) “Recommend the Previous Statements and Study Reports of the PCA in Regard to Homosexuality”  ·  **Disposition:** Out of order

*Source: [ga47_2019 p. 693](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p693)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Fifth General Assembly of the PCA in 1977 affirmed "the Biblical position for our denomination which states that: (1) The act of homosexuality is a sin according to God's Word; (2) Churches should actively seek to lead the homosexual person to confession and repentance that he might find justification and sanctification in Jesus Christ, according to 1 Corinthians 6:11; and, (3) In light of the Biblical view of its sinfulness, a practicing homosexual continuing in this sin would not be a fit candidate for ordination or membership in the Presbyterian Church in America." (Minutes of the 5th General Assembly of the PCA, 1977, pp. 67-68); and

Whereas, the PCA General Assembly has reaffirmed its position on the issue of homosexuality on subsequent occasions, including 1996, 1999, and 2018; and

Whereas, the 158th General Synod of the RPCES adopted a study report on homosexuality and commended their report, entitled "Pastoral Care for the Repentant Homosexual," to their sessions and congregations as an aid for their ministry to those struggling with homosexuality" (158th General Synod Minutes, 4 July 1980, pp. 43-50; Documents of Synod, pp. 301-8 http://www.pcahistory.org/findingaids/rpces/docsynod/301.html); and

Whereas, the 9th General Assembly of the PCA in 1981 adopted the Ad Interim Committee report on Joining and Receiving, which included the statement: "In receiving these denominations, the Presbyterian Church in America recognizes the history of the respective denominations as part of her total history and receives their historical documents as valuable and significant material which will be used in the perfecting of the church" [Minutes of the 9th General Assembly of the PCA, p. 305].

Therefore, be it resolved, that the Southern New England Presbytery overture the 47th General Assembly of the PCA to re-commend the previous statements and study reports of the PCA, including our study report adopted by the 158th General Synod of the RPCES on homosexuality entitled “Pastoral Care for the Repentant Homosexual," with the deletion of Sections III and IV, to our sessions and congregations as an aid for their ministry to those struggling with homosexuality." [158th General Synod Minutes, 4 July 1980, pp. 43-50; Documents of Synod, pp. 301-308. http://www.pcahistory.org/ findingaids/ rpces/docsynod/301.html]. Adopted by Southern New England Presbytery at its stated meeting, April 27, 2019 Attested by /s/ TE Ron Heald, stated clerk 691

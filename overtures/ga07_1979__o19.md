# GA7 O19 — Declare Total Abstinence from Alcoholic Beverages the Approved PCA Position

**Assembly:** 7th (1979)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Eastern Carolina

*Source: [ga07_1979 PDF p. 35](../markdown/ga07_1979.md#ga7-pdf-p35)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Scriptural view of Christian liberty is clearly stated in Romans 14:21 and 1 Cor. 8:13, and especially 1 Peter 2:16; and

Whereas, the consumption of alcoholic beverages is one of the most serious stumbling blocks in our country today; Be It Resolved, by the General Assembly in an *in thesi* deliverance that total abstinence from alcoholic beverages, except in medicinal use, be the approved position of our denomination in this matter. Adopted at the stated meeting, January 27, 1979. Attest: C. Gene Parks Stated Clerk Not received by the Assembly § 7-33; p. 92.

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Committee on Judicial Business finds Overture 19 in order and advises referral</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
5. Concerning Overture No. 19 to the Seventh General Assembly from Eastern Carolina Presbytery, the Committee found Overture 19 to be in order and advised the Clerk of the General Assembly to refer it to the Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Seventh GA minutes, printed p. 181 (PDF p. 183), Appendix E, Advice to the Stated Clerk, item 5](../markdown/ga07_1979.md#ga7-pdf-p183)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Sub-Committee on Judicial Business recommends finding Overture 19 constitutional and changing its wording</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
The Committee understood the words &quot;in thesi&quot; to mean a teaching or a didactic statement in theory which does not bind the conscience of the body, and therefore, on motion made and seconded the Committee found that Overture 19 is constitutional, recommended it be sent to the Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures, and recommended that the Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures change the final phrase to read, &quot;be the course of wisdom recommended by our denomination in this matter.&quot;
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Seventh GA minutes, printed p. 92 (PDF p. 94), 7-33, report on Overture 19](../markdown/ga07_1979.md#ga7-pdf-p94)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Assembly defeats the subcommittee recommendation, 132–306</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
The recommendation was defeated, by vote of 132 for, 306 against.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Seventh GA minutes, printed p. 92 (PDF p. 94), 7-33, vote](../markdown/ga07_1979.md#ga7-pdf-p94)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Moderator rules that the Assembly refused to receive Overture 19</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
The moderator ruled that this in effect meant the Assembly had refused to receive Overture 19.
</blockquote>

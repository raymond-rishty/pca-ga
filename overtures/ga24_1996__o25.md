# GA24 O25 — Amend BCO 46-5 in Accordance with Proposed Alternative A

**Assembly:** 24th (1996)  ·  **Source:** Ascension Presbytery "Amend BCO 46-5 with Proposed Alternative A"

*Source: [ga24_1996 PDF p. 306](../markdown/ga24_1996.md#ga24-p306)*

---

## Submitted overture

“Amend BCO 46-5 with Proposed Alternative A” the presbytery of the ascension hereby overtures the 24th General Assembly to begin the process of amending BCO 46-5 in accordance with proposed Alternative A (M23GA, pp. 221-226). Adopted on October 28, 1995 at the stated meeting of the presbytery of the Ascension. Attested by: Is/ Frederick R. Neikirk, Stated Clerk

# GA24 O25 — Amend BCO 46-5 in Accordance with Proposed Alternative A

**Assembly:** 24th (1996)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 25 From Ascension Presbytery  ·  **Disposition:** Answered by reference  ·  **BCO:** 46-5

*Source: [ga24_1996 p. 306](../markdown/ga24_1996.md#ga24-p306)*

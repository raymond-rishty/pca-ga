# GA6 O9 — Establish Dental Insurance Program for Ministers and Employees

**Assembly:** 6th (1978)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Southern Florida

*Source: [ga06_1978 PDF p. 34](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p34)*

---

## Submitted overture

“It was moved to properly overture the Trustees of Insurance of the General Assembly for a dental insurance program for ministers and other employees. Motion carried.” Earl S. Mizell, Stated Clerk Presbytery of Southern Florida Answered in the negative, §6-104, III. 8; p. 108. To the Committees of Commissioners on Insurance and Annuities and of Administration

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Southern Florida Presbytery adopts a motion to seek dental insurance</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
It was moved to properly overture the Trustees of Insurance of the General Assembly for a dental insurance program for ministers and other employees. Motion carried.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Sixth GA minutes, printed p. 32 (PDF p. 34), Overture 9; Presbytery motion](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p34)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The Insurance commissioners recommend rejecting the dental plan and returning it for study</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
2. That the proposed Dental Insurance Plan be rejected at this time and the proposed Dental Insurance Plan be returned to the Board of Trustees for study and possible recommendation to a future General Assembly.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Sixth GA minutes, printed p. 108 (PDF p. 110), 6-104, III, Recommendation 2](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p110)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The Sixth General Assembly adopts the recommendation on the dental plan</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Adopted.

# GA17 O19 — Amend BCO 37-3 to Apply to Presbyteries as Well as Sessions

**Assembly:** 17th (1989)  ·  **Source:** Delmarva Presbytery

*Source: [ga17_1989 PDF p. 168](../markdown/ga17_1989.md#ga17-pdf-p168)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas BCO 37 is clearly intended to cover the cases of teaching elders as well as regular church members, and

Whereas BCO 37 contains several references which are inconsistent with this intention, Therefore Be It Resolved, that the General Assembly of the PCA amend BCO 37 in the following ways: 1. In section 37-3, add "(or presbytery)" after "the session" on line 3, line 6 and line 25. 2. In section 37-3 add "(or moderator)" after "the minister" on line 7 and line 19. Adopted at the February 11, 1989 meeting of Delmarva Presbytery. Attest: Bruce B. Howes, Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA17 — proposal referred for a conforming amendment draft</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Recommendation: That Overture 19 from the Delmarva Presbytery be referred to the Committee on Judicial Business and that the Committee be instructed to draft amendments which will meet the concern of the Overture. Grounds: The proposed amendments do not fully deal with the question of what jurisdiction does a presbytery have (over against a session&#x27;s jurisdiction-see BCO 6-2,4; 11-4; and 12-5) for the restoring of an excommunicated teaching elder (see BCO 13-9; 34-4; 36-7; and 37-7). Adopted
</blockquote>

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA19 — CCB majority proposes a new BCO 37-5</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
The Committee recommends that a new 37-5 be inserted, to read as follows: &quot;Prior to the restoration of an excommunicated teaching elder, the Session shall request the Presbytery of record to absolve him from the censure of excommunication. Following the removal of the censure by the Presbytery, the Session shall proceed with restoration.&quot; Renumber the remaining paragraphs accordingly.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt · GA19 minutes, printed p. 230 (PDF p. 234)](../markdown/ga19_1991.md#ga19-pdf-p234)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA19 — minority opinion opposes the majority draft</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
This minority opinion, which would be filed as a Protest if the majority opinion were that of a court, is in protest of the majority opinion requiring that a Session obtain permission of a higher court in order to exercise a power assigned exclusively to the Session by the PCA Constitution.
</blockquote>

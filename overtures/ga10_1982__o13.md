# GA10 O13 — Amend BCO 25-3 to Reduce Congregational Meeting Quorum for Very Large Churches

**Assembly:** 10th (1982)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Southern Florida Whereas, the Book of Church Order 25-3 sets the quorum

*Source: [ga10_1982 p. 46](../markdown/ga10_1982.md#ga10-p46)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Book of Church Order 25-3 sets the quorum for congregational meetings; and

Whereas, a different quorum is required for churches of more than one hundred resident communing members, (1/4 of membership for churches of less than 100; 1/6 for those over 100); and

Whereas, churches with more than 2,000 members experience special difficulty in reaching the quorum required because of multiple services and other problems peculiar to very large churches; Therefore, the Presbytery of Southern Florida respectfully proposes that Chapter 25-3 of the Book of Church Order be amended to allow congregations of more than two thousand resident communing members to use a quorum of one eighth of their resident communing members. Adopted by the Presbytery of Southern Florida, January 19, 1982. EARL S. MIZELL, STATED CLERK Referred to Committee of Administration §10-75, III, 13, p. 102.

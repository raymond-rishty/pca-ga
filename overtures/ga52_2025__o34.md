# GA52 O34 — Amend RAO 16 to Require Reporting on Session and Diaconate Membership and Duties

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** Savannah River Presbytery (to CCB, OC)  ·  **Disposition:** RAO amendment failed: two-thirds vote not met (929-730)

*Source: [ga52_2025 p. 1272](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p1272)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas the 45th General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America adopted the 4th recommendation from the Study Committee on the Role of Women in the Ministry of the Church which, in its final form stated: "That sessions, if possible, establish a diaconate of qualified ordained men."; and

Whereas the rationale in the body of the report explaining this recommendation stated that “There is at least anecdotal evidence that some PCA churches are choosing not to establish an ordained diaconate, even with qualified candidates, because the church wishes to be free to establish a body of unordained servants, both male and female (Book of Church Order 9-7)", and similar reports of this practice persist within our denomination; and

Whereas BCO 9-7 recognizes an unofficial and unordained role within the church called “assistants to the deacons” comprised of “godly men andS women of the congregation to assist the deacons in the caring for the sick, the widow, the orphans, the prisoners, and other who may be in distress or need"; and

Whereas at its 50th General Assembly, the PCA approved an amendment to the Book of Church Order (BCO) 7-3 and affirmed it at its 51st General Assembly which prohibits assigning the titles connected to the ecclesial offices of pastor, elder, or deacon to non-ordained members;

Therefore be it resolved the Savannah River Presbytery respectfully overtures the 52nd General Assembly of the PCA to protect the constitutional practice of ordaining a Diaconate by adding the following to RAO 16-4.c.1 (underlining for additions; <del>strikethrough</del> for deletions):

A current Directory of Presbytery, including (1) a list of all teaching elders, with their addresses and their status; and of all churches and missions with the address of the church, and the name and address of the Clerk of Session, (2) the list of persons who comprise the Session

1270

and the Diaconate of each church arranged by their ordained or given titles; and if no Diaconate exists, a report why it has not been established and whether or not the duties have devolved upon the ruling elders, unordained diaconal assistants, or any other committee or member of the church, (23) a listing of all officers and committee members of the regular committees of presbytery, and (34) a list identifying all candidates under care, interns, and licentiates of the presbytery with their addresses and their status.

Be it further resolved to add the following to RAO 16-6.c.1: Exceptions of substance: Apparent violations of the Scripture or serious irregularities from the Constitution of the Presbyterian Church in America, actions out of accord with the deliverances of the General Assembly, and matters of impropriety and important delinquencies, and any non-compliance with RAO 16-3.e.5-6 and RAO 16-4.c.1 <del>should</del> shall be reported under this category.

Adopted by the Savannah River Presbytery at its Stated Meeting, April 15, 2025. Attested by RE W. Glynn Dyer, stated clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA52 — CCB advice on Overture 34</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 34 is in conflict with the Constitution in that if a church does not have Deacons, BCO 9-2 requires that the duties of the office “shall devolve upon the ruling elders&quot; of that church but the amendment as presently written suggests that the duties of the diaconate may devolve upon other persons or entities other than the ruling elders. Adopted 8-0-0
</blockquote>

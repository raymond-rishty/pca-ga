# GA7 O2 — Study the Imbalance Between Ruling and Teaching Elder Representation

**Assembly:** 7th (1979)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Southern Florida

*Source: [ga07_1979 p. 29](../markdown/ga07_1979.md#ga07-p29)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, The General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America has made an effort to increase the participation of Ruling Elders in the functions of the Assembly, and

Whereas, some Ruling Elders are discouraged over the imbalance between Ruling and Teaching Elders representatives to the Presbytery and the General Assembly, Be It Therefore Resolved, that the Seventh General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America ask the Permanent Committee on Judicial Business to study this imbalance and make recommendations to General Assembly as to a solution which would be in line with the Presbyterian Church in America's Doctrine of the Parity of Elders. Adopted at the Fall Meeting, October 17, 1978. Earl S. Mizell Stated Clerk Referred to the Permanent Committee on Administration § 7-90, 31; p. 140.

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Permanent Committee on Administration recommends a negative answer</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
8. Regarding Overture 2, referred by the Seventh General Assembly to the Committee, the following response is recommended: That the Assembly has been careful to maintain parity between ruling and teaching elders in all its functions and committees, and that the present structure of the Assembly encourages parity, with ruling elders from vacant churches and extra ruling elders from the larger churches tending to equalize representation; therefore, further legislation designed to perfect parity is not necessary at this time.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Eighth GA minutes, printed p. 129 (PDF p. 131), Appendix A, Permanent Committee on Administration report, item 8](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga8-p131)*

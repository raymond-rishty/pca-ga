# GA47 O43 — Form a Study Committee on Domestic Abuse and Sexual Assault

**Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Source:** Metro Atlanta Presbytery (to AC, OC) “Formation of a Study Committee on Domestic Abuse and Sexual Assault” (Version 8)  ·  **Disposition:** Answered by reference

*Source: [ga47_2019 p. 705](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p705)*

---

## Submitted overture

703

Whereas, the 42nd General Assembly previously exhorted the PCA and its courts to respond boldly against one form of these sins, namely the sexual abuse of children; and

Whereas, the PCA report on “Divorce and Remarriage” was limited in its focus and does not adequately define the scope and nature of domestic abuse and sexual assault nor adequately address many pastoral concerns regarding the care of those victimized by such sins; and

Whereas, no standardized resources for pastoral care on these sins and their complex consequences exist for officers, church courts, and agencies in the PCA; and

Whereas, no clear guidelines for policy or practice with regard to these sins exist for the officers, church courts, and agencies in the PCA that would protect individuals and congregations; and

Whereas, the Rules of Discipline protect against unjust accusation (BCO 31- 8), they also make no consideration for the unique heinousness of these sins and their practical effect on victims;

Therefore, be it resolved that Metro Atlanta Presbytery hereby overtures the 47th General Assembly of the PCA to form a committee to study:

- **(1)** the best practices and policy guidelines for elders, church courts, and agencies for justly and lawfully protecting against these sins, for providing safety for potential victims who come forward, and for responding to those guilty of these sins; and

- **(2)** the possibility of changes to the Rules of Discipline that would appropriately protect victims of these sins within a biblical process; and

- **(3)** the pastoral resources available for the care of survivors of these sins as well as ministry and counsel for those guilty of such sins;

- **(4)** the possible need for repentance as a church; and

- **(5)** to include in their report a definition of the scope and nature of domestic abuse and sexual assault; and

Be it further resolved that the committee shall be appointed by the moderator, and shall include among its members or advisory members at least two subject-matter experts and at least two women; and

Be it further resolved that the budget for the study committee be set at $15,000/year and that funds be derived from gifts to the AC designated for that purpose, with Metro Atlanta Presbytery committed to offer $1,000; and

Be it further resolved that the committee shall present their findings and recommendations for consideration by the 48th General Assembly. Adopted by Metro Atlanta Presbytery at its stated meeting, May 7, 2019 Attested by /s/ TE Randy Schlichting, stated clerk

# GA8 O3 — Amend BCO 25-6 through 25-9 via BCO 27-2

**Assembly:** 8th (1980)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Southern Florida

*Source: [ga08_1980 p. 39](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga08-p39)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, it appears that the First General Assembly, when formulating the current wording of paragraphs 25-6 through 25-9 of the Book of Church Order, intended to allow, though not to recommend, limited (rotational) terms of active service for Ruling Elders and Deacons (see Recommendation No. 2, Report of Permanent Sub-Committee on Judicial Business to the Sixth General Assembly, page 188, Minutes of the Sixth General Assembly), and

Whereas, many individuals within our denomination, in particular churches both using and not using limited terms, doubt or question whether the current wording of the Book of Church Order does in fact allow limited terms of active service for Ruling Elders and Deacons as it appears was intended by the First General Assembly, and

Whereas, many of our particular churches are using limited terms of active service for their Ruling Elders and/or Deacons, and strongly desire to continue to do so, and

Whereas, the particular churches using limited terms of active service also desire that there be no doubt or questioning as to whether the procedure they a reusing is allowed by the Book of Church Order, and

Whereas, the widespread use within our denomination of a procedure, the allowableness of which under the Book of Church Order is extensively doubted and questioned, erodes the authority of and respect for the Book of Church Order and is inimical to effective church government. Therefore, the Presbytery of Southern Florida hereby overtures the Eighth (1980) General Assembly to initiate action under the provisions of paragraph 27-2 of the Book of Church Order to amend and thereby clarify the Book of Church Order by adding the following new paragraph: "25-10. Instead of electing Ruling Elders and Deacons for indefinite or unlimited terms of active service, a particular church, by a majority vote a ta congregational meeting, may adopt a system providing for the election of Ruling Elders or Deacons, or both, for limited terms of active service. In such cases, the terms of active service shall be at least three years, except to initiate the system or to fill unexpired terms; and the Session and the Board of Deacons each shall consist of at least three classes elected at regular intervals. When a congregation limits terms of active service, it shall designate the period, if any, during which officers shall be ineligible for re-election to active service.” "A Ruling Elder whose term of active service in a particular church has expired, if otherwise eligible, may represent that church in the higher courts. Adopted at the Stated Meeting of Southern Florida Presbytery, October 16, 1979 Attest: Earl S. Mizell Stated Clerk Answered in the negative. § 8-88, III, 12; p. 118.

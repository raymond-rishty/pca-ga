# GA49 O8 — Amend BCO 33-1 and 34-1 to Establish Percentage Threshold for Original Jurisdiction

**Assembly:** 49th (2022)  ·  **Source:** Houston Metro Presbytery (to CCB, OC) “Amend BCO 33-1 and BCO 34-1 Establishing Percentage  ·  **Disposition:** Approved → sent to presbyteries; ratification not located

*Source: [ga49_2022 p. 1313](../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p1313)*

---

## Submitted overture

Be it resolved that BCO 33-1 and 34-1 be amended as follows (<del>strike</del> <del>through</del> for deletions, underlining for new wording): 33-1. Process against all a church members, <del>other than</del> <del>ministers of the Gospel</del>, shall be entered before the Session of the church to which such members belongs, <del>exeept in</del> <del>cases of appeal</del>. However, if <del>the-Session refuses to act in</del> <del>doctrinal cases or instances of public scandal and two</del> <del>other</del> Sessions of at least ten percent (10%) of churches in the same Presbytery request the Presbytery of which the church is a member to <del>initiate proper or appropriate action in a case of</del> <del>process and thus</del> assume original jurisdiction for a case of process (<del>to first receive and initially hear and determine</del>) <del>and</del> <del>authority</del>, the Presbytery shall do so. 34-1. Process against a minister shall be entered before the Presbytery of which he is a member. However, if the <del>Presbytery refuses to act in doctrinal cases or cases of public</del> <del>scandal and two other</del> at least ten percent (10%) of Presbyteries request the General Assembly to assume original jurisdiction for a case of process (<del>to first receive and</del> <del>initially hear and determine</del>), the General Assembly shall do SO. So that BCO 33-1 and 34-1, as amended, would read:

33-1. Process against a church member shall be entered before the Session of the church to which such member belongs. However, if the Sessions of at least ten percent (10%) of churches in the same Presbytery request the Presbytery of which the church is a member to assume original jurisdiction for a case of process, the Presbytery shall do so. 34-1. Process against a minister shall be entered before the Presbytery of which he is a member. However, if at least ten percent (10%) of_Presbyteries request the General Assembly to assume original jurisdiction for a case of process, the General Assembly shall do so.

## Submitted overture

Whereas, deaf people in the United States need Christ just as much as we, and

Whereas, it does not appear that the Presbyterian Church in America is actively engaged in meeting this need: Calvary Presbytery, through its Bills and Overtures Committee, respectfully requests the Assembly’s serious consideration of the following: 1. That the deaf are just as entitled to the Gospel as are the many people to whom we send missionaries and Bible translators. 2. That there are as of 1982 approximately 13.5 million hearing-impaired people in the U.S., of these 1,767,046 are deaf. And of these, 500,000 were born deaf or became deaf before adulthood. 3. The Baptists have had a ministry to the deaf for over 100 years. Their first missionary to the deaf was appointed in 1906. 4. The Great Commission says we are to take the Gospel into all the world and to all people. Therefore, we respectfully petition the General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to pursue investigation of this need and to take whatever action may be deemed appropriate. Adopted at the Spring Stated Meeting of Calvary Presbytery, April 25, 1985. Attest: Ralph A. Price, Stated Clerk Answered in the affirmative, 13-48, III, 28, p. 116.

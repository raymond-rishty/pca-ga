# GA52 O16 — Amend BCO 36-5 to Conform Language to BCO 37-3 Regarding Suspension from Office

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 16 from Mississippi Valley Presbytery (to CCB, OC)

*Source: [ga52_2025 PDF p. 1201](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-pdf-p1201)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas the censure of indefinite suspension is “administered to an accused who, upon conviction, remains impenitent”(BCO 30-1), and is administered “until he exhibits signs of repentance, or until by his conduct, the necessity of the greatest censure be made manifest" (BCO 30-3), and

Whereas with respect to church officers, "suspension from Sacraments shall always be accompanied by suspension from office. But suspension from office is not always necessarily accompanied with suspension from Sacraments” (BCO 30-3), and

Whereas the language stipulated in BCO 36-5 to be used for the administration of the censure of indefinite suspension does not address the case when an officer is indefinitely suspended from his office but not from the Sacraments as clearly as it could, and

Whereas the language stipulated in BCO 37-3 to be used for the restoration of one who has been indefinitely suspended more clearly addresses the case when an officer is indefinitely suspended from his office but not from the Sacraments, and

1199

Whereas there is wisdom in bringing the language of BCO 36-5 into conformity with the language of BCO 37-3,

Therefore, be it resolved that BCO 36-5 be amended by adding “/or" to the word “and” in "(and from the exercise of your office)" (underlining for additions):

Whereas, you, (here describe the person as a teaching elder, ruling elder, deacon, or private member of the church) are convicted by sufficient proof (or are guilty by your own confession) of the sin of (here insert the offense), we the Presbytery (or Church Session) in the name and by the authority of the Lord Jesus Christ, do now declare you suspended from the Sacraments of the Church (and/or from the exercise of your office), until you give satisfactory evidence of repentance.

So that the amended section will read as follows:

Whereas, you, (here describe the person as a teaching elder, ruling elder, deacon, or private member of the church) are convicted by sufficient proof (or are guilty by your own confession) of the sin of (here insert the offense), we the Presbytery (or Church Session) in the name and by the authority of the Lord Jesus Christ, do now declare you suspended from the Sacraments of the Church (and/or from the exercise of your office), until you give satisfactory evidence of repentance.

Adopted by the Mississippi Valley Presbytery at its stated meeting, February 4, 2025. Attested by TE Chris Wright, stated clerk

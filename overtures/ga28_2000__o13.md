# GA28 O13 — Amend BCO 21 to Allow Modified Re-Ordination Procedures for Former PCA Ministers

**Assembly:** 28th (2000)  ·  **Source:** Susquehanna Valley Presbytery "Re-ordination of Former PCA Ministers"

*Source: [ga28_2000 p. 289](../markdown/ga28_2000.md#ga28-p289)*

---

## Submitted overture

“Re-ordination of former PCA Ministers” whereas the Book of Church Order rightly calls fora presbytery to divest a minister if he continues on the rolls without call for over three years (BCO 34-10, 13-2), and

Whereas, this procedure is rarely followed, and

Whereas, this leads to men not being as closely connected to sessions as is good for them or as is good for the work of the church, and

Whereas, this often puts pressure on presbyteries to approve “calls” which are questionable, and

Whereas, one reason for this failure of presbyteries to act is that both the man and his presbytery are hoping a call will come and if it does after he is divested he would have to go through the entire ordination process again, Therefore, the Presbytery of Susquehanna Valley hereby overtures the General Assembly to add a new section 5 as follows to chapter 21 of the BCO and renumber the following sections: NEW 21-5: Modified procedures may be used for the re-ordination of a former PCA minister who receives a call to a particular church after he was divested without censure at his request (38-2) or was divested of his office without censure against his will (34-10). The presbytery in which he is being called shall carefully investigate the circumstances under which he was divested. If the presbytery decides that the reason he was divested was simply a lack of call, it may proceed to give him the trials for ordination outlined in 21-4 without going through the normal candidate, licensure, and internship procedures. At its discretion, the presbytery may accept his previous ordination in lieu of examining him in the Greek, and Hebrew languages, church history, and the history of the PCA. Adopted by the Presbytery of Susquehanna Valley on February 18, 2000. Attested by: /s/ David F. Rundle, Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">CCB advice on Overture 13 (minutes excerpt)</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
OVERTURE 13 – From Susquehanna Valley Presbytery, “Re-ordination of Former PCA Ministers.&quot; Response – It is the opinion of the Committee that Overture 13 as presented is not in technical conflict with any other provisions of the Constitution. However, the Committee would point out that BCO 34-10 does not mandate divestiture in every case (&quot;...it shall be the duty of the Presbytery...to inquire...and, if necessary, to institute judicial proceedings....If it shall appear that his neglect proceeds only from his lack of acceptance to the church, Presbytery may... divest him of his office....&quot;). The Committee is also concerned that the language proposed for the new BCO 21-5 raises questions about the nature and importance of the call to the ministry (&quot;... simply a lack of call....&quot;). Finally, the Committee notes the apparent inconsistency in the handling of one who has been removed from office by discipline (deposition - BCO 36-7) who would be restored as per BCO 37-5 and one who is divested without censure (BCO 34-10) who could be restored only after reexamination and re-ordination. Perhaps a proposed amendment clarifying the relationship between divestiture and deposition would be helpful. The response was adopted by proper motion, 8-0-0. [Text and action at 28-72, III, 11, p. 287.]
</blockquote>

# GA47 O35 — Affirm the PCA's Commitment to the Sanctity of Biblical Sexual Relationships

**Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Source:** Savannah River Presbytery (to OC) “Affirm the PCA's Commitment to the Sanctity of Biblical  ·  **Disposition:** Out of order

*Source: [ga47_2019 p. 679](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p679)*

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Scripture is infallible and inerrant, the only rule of faith and practice, and the only touchstone of truth; and

Whereas, the Constitution of the Presbyterian Church in America “is subject to and subordinate to the Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments;" and

Whereas, every officer (teaching elders, ruling elders, and deacons) in the Presbyterian Church in America vows that they "believe the Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments, as originally given, to be the inerrant Word of God, the only infallible rule of faith and practice;" and

Whereas, all church courts in the Presbyterian Church in America have the power "to bear testimony against error in doctrine and immorality," "to require obedience to the laws of Christ," and "to cut off the contumacious and impenitent from the congregation of believers" (BCO 11-2); and 677

Whereas, every PCA Presbytery has power “to condemn erroneous opinions which injure the purity and peace of the Church" (BCO 13-9f); and

Whereas, the Revoice Conference, held at Memorial Presbyterian Church in St. Louis, Missouri on July 26-28, 2018, was for the purpose of "Supporting, encouraging, and empowering gay, lesbian, same-sex attracted and other LGBT+ Christians;" and

Whereas, some within the Presbyterian Church in America are claiming that same-sex attraction is not necessarily a sin but can be a disposition given to some: "that such a disposition of sinful desires are God given for the acceptance of all believers and the Church, and for the promotion of the Glory of God;" or "that individuals with sinful homosexual feelings of same-sex attraction have no control over these feelings and/or attractions; that though they may be unwanted, they are helpless to avoid and are in no need of repentance;" or who answer affirmatively: “Can a person claim to be born with same-sex attraction in the same manner as the man born blind?" or are in agreement with: “I believe my same-sex attractions are broken, but I do not believe they are sinful. It is not sinful for me to be attracted to another man;" or asserting that same-sex attraction and orientation are permissible under the Gospel as long as the person remains celibate; even though these assertions are contrary to what Jesus said that any and all lust in the heart is a sin: “I say to you that everyone who looks at a woman with lust for her has already committed adultery with her in his heart" (Matthew 5:28); and

Whereas, the Scripture teaches that same-sex attraction is an evidence that God has given over such people to “the lusts of their hearts to impurity, so that their bodies would be dishonored among them" and “to degrading passions" in which “women exchanged the natural function for that which is unnatural" and "the men abandoned the natural function of the woman and burned in their desire toward one another" because they “exchanged the truth of God for a lie” and “did not see fit to acknowledge God any longer"(Romans 1:24-28); and

Whereas, homosexuality is a sin from which God delivered some of the Corinthian Christians (“such were some of you..): “Or do you not know that the unrighteous will not inherit the kingdom of God? Do not be deceived; neither fornicators, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor effeminate, nor homosexuals, nor thieves, nor the covetous, nor drunkards, nor revilers, nor swindlers, will inherit the kingdom of God. Such were some of you; but you were washed, but you were sanctified, but you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and in the Spirit of our God"(1 Corinthians 6:9-11); and continues to deliver believers from this and other sins; and

678

Whereas, same-sex attraction is lust in the very same manner that heterosexual attraction is lust, and both are violations of the seventh commandment as the Larger Catechism teaches: Question 138: What are the duties required in the seventh commandment? Answer: The duties required in the seventh commandment are, chastity in body, mind, affections, words, and behavior; and the preservation of it in ourselves and others; watchfulness over the eyes and all the senses; temperance, keeping of chaste company, modesty in apparel; marriage by those that have not the gift of continency, conjugal love, and cohabitation; diligent labor in our callings; shunning all occasions of uncleanness, and resisting temptations thereunto.

Question 139: What are the sins forbidden in the seventh commandment? Answer: The sins forbidden in the seventh commandment, besides the neglect of the duties required, are, adultery, fornication, rape, incest, sodomy, and all unnatural lusts; all unclean imaginations, thoughts, purposes, and affections; all corrupt or filthy communications, or listening thereunto; wanton looks, impudent or light behavior, immodest apparel; prohibiting of lawful, and dispensing with unlawful marriages; allowing, tolerating, keeping of stews, and resorting to them; entangling vows of single life, undue delay of marriage; having more wives or husbands than one at the same time; unjust divorce, or desertion; idleness, gluttony, drunkenness, unchaste company; lascivious songs, books, pictures, dancings, stage plays; and all other provocations to, or acts of uncleanness, either in ourselves or others.

Therefore, be it resolved, that in light of the fact that this current issue is once again confronting the PCA, the 47th General Assembly take the following actions:

That the 47th General Assembly reaffirm the action of the 5th General Assembly:

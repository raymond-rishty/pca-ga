# GA2 O11 — Direct Mission to the World Committee to Use Women Biblically on the Field

**Assembly:** 2nd (1974)  ·  **Source:** Westminster Presbytery

*Source: [ga02_1974 PDF p. 29](../markdown/ga02_1974.md#ga2-pdf-p29)*

---

## Submitted overture

We, the members of Westminster Presbytery, solemnly overture General Assembly to encourage the Mission to the World Committee to be carefully Biblical in its use of women on the mission field, particularly as regards evangelism, preaching, and church development; remembering Paul's admonition to Timothy (I Timothy 2:12): &gt; "But I do not allow a woman to teach or exercise authority over a man, but to remain quiet." NASB

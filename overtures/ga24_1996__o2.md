# GA24 O2 — Amend BCO 7-2 to Restrict Permanent Committee Voting to Ordained Officers

**Assembly:** 24th (1996)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Covenant "Amend BCO 7-2 To Limit Voting Members on Permanent Committees"

*Source: [ga24_1996 PDF p. 289](../markdown/ga24_1996.md#ga24-pdf-p289)*

---

## Submitted overture

“Amend BCO 7-2 To Limit Voting Members on Permanent Committees”

Be it resolved that BCO 7-2 be amended by appending the following sentence at the end of 7-2: “The members of the permanent committees of the higher courts must be either Elders or Deacons; non-ordained individuals may serve these committees in a non-voting advisory capacity.” So that 7-2 now reads: 7-2. the ordinary and perpetual classes of office in the church are Elders and Deacons. within the class of elder are the two orders of teaching elders and ruling Elders. The Elders jointly have the government and spiritual oversight of the Church, including teaching. Only those elders who are specially gifted, called and trained by God to preach may serve as Teaching Elders. The office of deacon is not one of rule, but rather of service both to the physical and spiritual needs of the people. In accord with Scripture, these offices are open to men only. the members of the permanent committees of the higher courts must be either Elders or Deacons; non-ordained individuals may serve these committees in a non-voting advisory capacity. adopted at the fall stated meeting of covenant presbytery on october 3,1995. Attested by: /s/ Grover E. Gunn III, Stated Clerk i. That Overture 3 from the presbytery of pacific northwest be answered in the negative. Adopted Grounds: The BCO provides sufficient flexibility in these circumstances (BCO 8-4, 8-5 , 8-7, and 20-1).

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">CCB advice on GA24 Overture 2</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Overture 2 - from Covenant Presbytery &quot;Amend BCO 7-2, Re: Limit Voting Members on Permanent Committees&quot; Response: It is the advice of the CCB that Overture 2 is in conflict with the Constitution of the PCA in that BCO 14-1(10 and 11) allow for the election of only elders to &quot;permanent committees,&quot; even though BCO 9-5 authorizes the appointment of deacons by the higher courts to serve on committees, especially as treasurers. It is noted that the proposed amendment does not fit well in BCO 7-2 and might be better addressed at some other portion of the BCO. It is also noted that the intent of the Overture should be clarified with &quot;[w]hereas&quot; paragraphs and that some effort should be made to define &quot;permanent committee&quot; in light of BCO 14-1 (11 and 12).
</blockquote>

# GA7 O7 — Establish an Ad Interim Committee to Study Dispensationalism and Covenant Theology

**Assembly:** 7th (1979)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of North Georgia  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga07_1979 p. 42](../markdown/ga07_1979.md#ga07-p42)*

---

The North Georgia Presbytery respectfully overtures the General Assembly to set up an Ad Interim Committee for the purpose of studying the relationship between dispensationalism and covenant theology. Adopted by North Georgia Presbytery at its stated meeting, January 21, 1978. Attest: Robert G. Valentine Stated Clerk Answered, by reference to the Committee for Christian Education, §6-63, II. A. p. 80. To be reported back to the Eighth General Assembly, § 7-49, p. 115.

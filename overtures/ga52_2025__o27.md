# GA52 O27 — Erect Ad Interim Committee to Receive Suggestions on Permanent Committee and Agency Operations

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** the Northwest Georgia Presbytery (to OC, AC, CC, CDM, CTS, Geneva, MNA, MTW, PCAF, RH, RUF

*Source: [ga52_2025 PDF p. 1245](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-pdf-p1245)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas every church and organization deserves loving, thoughtful, and wise review of its operations and expenses from time to time, and the highest levels of PCA committees and agencies have not had such in decades; and

Whereas the proposal below has the goal and potential to bring greater unity and increasing support from churches and Presbyteries for the concerted work of all PCA units; and

Whereas the PCA should desire to have growing and optimal support from the grassroots; and

Whereas total receipts to all PCA agencies now exceed $200,000,000 annually (for which we give praise to God), and we wish to use all the Lord's resources optimally, including possibly re-directing some of those funds back to the lower courts for vital ministry, such as church planting, revitalization, missions, supporting interns in smaller churches, etc., which may be a helpful step to unleashing Presbyteries and Sessions to reach their full potential with less overhead; and

Whereas even if fears about well-intended reviews arise—turf protection may yet occur in fine churches—for the good of the church and its future improved stewardship, we commend this process, which we believe is fair, transparent, and at its heart providing maximum input from the grass roots to the General Assembly; and

Whereas we may give thanks for each of our elected Coordinators and leaders, we are also blessed with many gifted servants, relieving the practice of lengthy tenures of office; and

1243

Whereas we all wish to adapt so that more PCA congregations will support more of a united denominational effort, willingly and eagerly, not as paying obligatory apportionments; and

Whereas a method exists to invite widely-representative input as a basis for such a helpful review at no cost to the GA budget with much potential gain to use for greater mission;

Therefore, we request the 52nd General Assembly to approve (per RAO 9-2) an ad interim Committee on Operations, Resources, and Expenses, appointed by the Moderator by August 1, 2025, with said committee (per RAO 9-4) tasked to solicit and receive any recommendations for improvement, optimization, and greater transparency from the lower courts for all funded General Assembly works, which shall all then be collated, evaluated, and reported (a) on its website regularly and (b) directly to the 53rd General Assembly. Further, each Presbytery is encouraged to communicate its ideas for improvement regarding all funded committees or agencies, seeking to use optimally all resources and GA income while also increasing support from the grass roots for denominational units. Funding of $10,000 for this committee (RAO 9-

5. and its website that will report submitted suggestions monthly shall be shared between the Session originating this overture and any other voluntary contributors.

Adopted by the Northwest Georgia Presbytery at its 57th stated meeting, April 1, 2025 Attested by TE Robby Baxter, stated clerk

11. Grounds: The Overture is unnecessary. The annual budgets of every Committee and Agency are reviewed thoroughly and audited annually (RAO 14-7h).

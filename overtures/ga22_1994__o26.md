# GA22 O26 — Expand the Geographical Boundaries of Philadelphia Presbytery

**Assembly:** 22nd (1994)  ·  **Source:** Philadelphia Presbytery

*Source: [ga22_1994 PDF p. 174](../markdown/ga22_1994.md#ga22-pdf-p174)*

---

## Submitted overture

"Expand Geographical Boundaries of Philadelphia Presbytery" BE IT

RESOLVED that Philadelphia Presbytery of the Presbyterian Church in America overtures the 22nd General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America, meeting in Atlanta, Georgia, June 6-10, 1994, to extend the geographical boundaries of Philadelphia Presbytery to include those counties necessary to include Scranton and Wilkes-Barre and extend to the New York border, namely Carbon, Lackawanna, Luzerne, Monroe, Pike, Susquehana, Wayne, and Wyoming Counties. Adopted by Presbytery, November 13,1993, Newtown Square, PA. Attested by: /s/ Frank D. Moser, Stated Clerk 18. That the GA respond to Overture 27 from Tennessee Valley Presbytery to divide and form a new Presbytery to be called Nashville Presbyterian the affirmative. Adopted

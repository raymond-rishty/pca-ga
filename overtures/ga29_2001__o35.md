# GA29 O35 — Remove Concurring Opinions from SJC Report in Judicial Matter 99-1

**Assembly:** 29th (2001)  ·  **Source:** Western Carolina Presbytery (to B&O) "Remove Concurring Opinion in Judicial Matter 99-1"

*Source: [ga29_2001 p. 245](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p245)*

---

## Submitted overture

"Remove Concurring Opinion in Judicial Matter 99-1"

Whereas, the Western Carolina Presbytery has issued a call to the Presbyterian Church in America to bring the John Wood matter to closure and to endeavor to pursue together both the purity, peace and unity of the church; and

Whereas, we question the propriety of the Standing Judicial Commission attaching concurring opinions to a report that they were not going to proceed to judicial process, since concurring opinions are provided for only in cases of judicial process and to include these concurring opinions tend to disturb the peace of the Church, after the decision has been reached; and

Whereas, to allow the personal allegations against individual members of the Assembly to be placed in the Minutes of the General Assembly can only bring hurt to the individuals concerned, and cause resentments that are destructive of the purity, peace and unity of the Church; and

Whereas, this is not the proper venue for such allegations, since TE's who have acted improperly should be charged in the courts of original jurisdiction, where they can have a fair trial; and

Whereas, we do not believe that the provision for concurring opinions was ever intended to provide a forum for personal attack as occurs in this report; Therefore, Western Carolina Presbytery urges that all of the concurring opinions be removed from the SJC report, and that they not appear in the Minutes of the General Assembly. Adopted by Western Carolina Presbytery at a called meeting on May 8, 2001. Attested by: /s/ Skip Gillikin, Stated Clerk

*Source: [Excerpt · ga29_2001 minutes, printed p. 242 (PDF p. 244), 29-44, III, Recommendation 22a](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p244)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Committee recommends SJC procedures on temperate language</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
- **b.** Whereas, we find intemperate language in the first concurring opinion to SJC Case 99-1; Whereas, we find that the SJC did not make a determination as to whether the language in the first concurring opinion was temperate; Whereas, we find there is no present procedure for making determination of temperance prior to publication of concurring opinions; and Whereas, we believe it is important to protect the names of our brothers; therefore, We recommend that the 29th General Assembly direct the SJC to draft and present to the 30th General Assembly procedures to be placed in the SJC Manual for examining concurring and dissenting opinions to be sure such opinions are in temperate language before they are added to SJC reports. Adopted
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt · ga29_2001 minutes, printed p. 242 (PDF p. 244), 29-44, III, Recommendation 22b](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p244)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Committee recommends removal of the concurring opinion</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
- **C.** Whereas, we are thankful for the unity reached by our committee in finding the offending concurring opinion in SJC Case 99-1 at pp. 2047-2050 to be intemperate; Whereas, we anticipate future corrective action by the SJC; Whereas, Paragraph III of the Preface to the BCO affirms that the Constitution of the PCA &quot;... is subject to and subordinate to the Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments, the inerrant Word Of God...&quot;; therefore, We recommend that the first concurring opinion (pp. 73-76) be removed on the basis of justice and fairness (Micah 6:8; Matthew 18:15-17; 1 Timothy 5:19; and James 3:5-6).
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt · ga29_2001 minutes, printed p. 242 (PDF p. 244), 29-44, III, Recommendation 22c](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p244)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">SJC requests an additional year to complete the assignment</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
The SJC officers appointed a committee to review the assignment from the 29th General Assembly. That committee brought forward a preliminary report to the full SJC at its March meeting. That report noted the complexity of the issues involved including apparent conflicts among the Book of Church Order, the Rules of Assembly Operation and the Manual of the Standing Judicial Commission. After adopting a preliminary recommendation, the SJC was unable to complete its work on this important matter. The SJC, therefore, asks the indulgence of the General Assembly in granting it an additional year to further study the matter and complete its work to be brought to the 31st General Assembly. Adopted
</blockquote>

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">SJC presents draft temperate-language procedures to GA31</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
It is recommend to General Assembly to amend the Standing Judicial Commission Manual by inserting a new section numbered 20.12 as follows.<br>
<br>
20.12 All concurring opinions and all dissenting opinions, which do not qualify as minority decisions under terms of BCO, shall be reviewed by the full commission to determine whether or not such opinions are couched in temperate language.
</blockquote>

# GA29 O23 — Require Exceptions for Views Other than Six 24-Hour Creation Days

**Assembly:** 29th (2001)  ·  **Source:** Mississippi Valley Presbytery (to B&O &CCB) "Require Exceptions for Views Other than Six 2  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga29_2001 p. 199](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p199)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Bible, in Genesis 1, Exodus 20:11, and Exodus 31:17 teaches that God created the heavens and the earth, the sea and all that is in them in six days, and

Whereas, our doctrinal standards (WCF 4, LC 15, and SC 9) affirm that it pleased God, by the word of his power, to create the world and all things in the space of six days, and all very good. Therefore, Be It Resolved, with charity toward all members and with fidelity to the original authors of our confessional standards, our understanding is that, unless evidence to the contrary is found, the Westminster Standards’ phrase “in the space of six days” means that the six days of creation were days of normal duration with evening and morning. Accordingly, for the peace of the church, any future candidates who differ with this original meaning should request an exception to the Confession, unless documentation that the Westminster Assembly meant otherwise is firmly established. We also pledge to make these future determinations, with care, charity, honesty and impartiality. adopted at the stated meeting of mississippi valley presbytery on february 6, 2001. Attested by: /s/R o ger G. Collins, Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">CCB advice on overtures concerning the Creation Study Committee report (minutes excerpt)</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Item 1: Advice to the Stated Clerk in the matter of the role of in thesi statements. Since the adoption of the Creation Study Committee report as amended last year, I have received inquires as to the status of the report in relation to the constitution. Moreover, there are overtures this year to require exceptions for any views other than 24-hour creation days (Overture 7, 23) to allow exceptions for other view (Overture 20), and to affirm the Position of the 28th General Assembly on diversity of views on creation days (Overture 30).<br>
<br>
My advice has been as follows:<br>
<br>
- **a.** The Creation Study Committee Report has the status of any other study committee report, such as those on the gifts of the Holy Spirit and Freemasonry, that is, it is the thinking of a particular General Assembly on a specific subject.<br>
<br>
- **b.** The Creation Study Committee Report, as with all other study committee reports adopted by a General Assembly, does not amend the constitution (BCO Preface, III. &quot;The Constitution Defined&quot; and BCO 26-1); only amendments effected by the proper constitutional procedures are actual and lawful amendments (BCO 26-2, 26-3 and 26-6).<br>
<br>
- **C.** The Creation Study Committee Report has the status of being among those decisions of the General Assembly that are to be given &quot;due and serious consideration by the Church and its lower courts when deliberating matters related to such actions&quot; (BCO 14-7).<br>
<br>
- **d.** Overtures relating to the Creation Study Committee Report would have the same status as the report, i.e. of being among those decisions of the General Assembly that are to be given &quot;due and serious consideration by the Church and its lower courts when deliberating matters related to such actions&quot; (BCO 14-7). Response: The CCB ratifies the advice of the Stated Clerk relative to the Role of in thesi Statements dated April 21, 2001, and offers the following additional advice: It is the purview of the lower courts to determine the suitability of men applying for reception/ordination with regard to the men&#x27;s views on the matters raised in in thesi statements, subject always to proper review and/or judicial process. (BCO 13-6 and 13-9.a) (BCO 24-1) (BCO 11-3,4) Adopted by CCB
</blockquote>

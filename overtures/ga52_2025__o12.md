# GA52 O12 — Amend BCO 58-5 to Restrict Distribution of Lord's Supper to Officers

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** the Providence Presbytery (to CCB, OC) "Amend BCO 58-5 to Specify Only Officers May Distri  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga52_2025 p. 1191](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p1191)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas our Confession of Faith (29.3), Catechisms (Larger Catechism 169, 173, and 176) and the declarations of our own denomination* call for the Supper to be administered by ministers,

And whereas the keys of the Kingdom and the sacraments are to be in the hands of the ordained offices of the church (Matthew 16:19),

1189

And whereas the role of the Session in discipline impinges upon the serving of the Supper (BCO 30-3; cf. WCF 30:1-4)

And whereas not since the beginning of Presbyterianism has the administering of the sacraments been in the hands of the non-ordained membership (see also the Scots Confession, Chapter 22**),

And whereas in the Presbyterian church the passing of the elements by the laity has historically been done while seated, while those standing in front of the congregation represent the administering and serving role, and having non-ordained people standing with the minister and serving the Supper confuses the role of ordained office,

And whereas there are no positive Scriptural mandates to allow non-ordained members to administer and serve communion that would encourage us to change our practice,

And whereas the Directory of Worship (58-5) only implies (or assumes) the role of the officers in the serving of the Supper, not foreseeing the cultural trends in our contemporary society,

Be it resolved that we overture the 52nd General Assembly to amend the Directory of Worship in BCO 58-5, third paragraph as follows (underlining for additions):

Here the bread is to be distributed by the minister or to other officers for distribution. After having given the bread, he shall take the cup, and say: In the same manner, He also took the cup, and having given thanks as has been done in His name, He gave it to the disciples, saying, “This cup is the new covenant in My blood, which is shed for many for the remission of sins. Drink from it, all of you."

While the minister is repeating these words, let him give the cup directly to the people or to the officers for distribution.

1190

Adopted by the Providence Presbytery at its stated meeting, February 4, 2025. Attested by TE Adam Tisdale, Stated Clerk

BCO 30-3. Suspension from Sacraments is the temporary exclusion from those
ordinances, and is indefinite as to its duration. There is no definite suspension
from the Sacraments. Suspension from office is the exclusion of a church
officer from his office. This may be definite or indefinite as to its duration.
With respect to church officers, suspension from Sacraments shall always be
accompanied by suspension from office. But suspension from office is not
always necessarily accompanied with suspension from Sacraments. Definite
suspension from office is administered when the credit of religion, the honor
of Christ, and the good of the delinquent demand it, even though the delinquent
has given satisfaction to the court. Indefinite suspension is administered to the
impenitent offender until he exhibits signs of repentance, or until by his
conduct, the necessity of the greatest censure be made manifest. In the case of
indefinite suspension from office imposed due to scandalous conduct, the
procedure outlined in BCO 34- 8 shall be followed.

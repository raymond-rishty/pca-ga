# GA18 O13 — Amend BCO 58-4 to Clarify Church Membership

**Assembly:** 18th (1990)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 13 From the Presbytery of the Southwest (to CCB/B&O)

*Source: [ga18_1990 PDF p. 125](../markdown/ga18_1990.md#ga18-p125)*

---

## Submitted overture

123

<a id="ga18-p126"></a>
<!-- PAGE ga=18 pdf_page=126 printed_page=124 -->

Therefore, be it resolved, that the General Assembly amend BCO 58-4 Line 14, by inserting the word "ordinarily" before the phrase "communicants in good standing in any evangelical church..."

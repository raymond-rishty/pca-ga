# GA16 O18 — Amend BCO 56-4 and 57-5 to Include Adopted Children in Covenant Membership

**Assembly:** 16th (1988)  ·  **Source:** Covenant Presbytery

*Source: [ga16_1988 PDF p. 61](../markdown/ga16_1988.md#ga16-p61)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the benefits of the covenant family and the sign and seal of infant baptism are applied to the children of believing parents regardless of whether the children are born to or adopted by the parents and ... (Genesis 17:22ff, WCF 28-4)

Whereas, the efforts of the Lord's church to provide alternatives to abortion have brought about increasing numbers of children being adopted by covenant households and ...

Whereas, believers in the Lord Jesus Christ are the heavenly Father's adopted sons and daughters having all the rights and privileges of the children of God and are fellow heirs with Jesus ... Therefore, Be It Resolved that the Book of Church Order, 56-4, lines 29 &amp; 30, which presently reads "By virtue of being born of believing parents children are . . ." be changed to read, "By virtue of being children of believing parents they are . . ." And Be It Further Resolved that the Book of Church Order, 57-5(1), which presently reads, "Of the number of those who were baptized in infancy as members of the Church of God by birthright,...” be changed to read, "Of the number of those who were baptized in infancy as members of the Church of God as children,..." Adopted by Covenant Presbytery at the Spring Stated Meeting, March 1, 1988 at Cleveland, Mississippi. Attest: Barry G. Waugh, Stated Clerk See 16-77, III, 35, p. 179.

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
35. That Overture 18, p. 59, from Covenant Presbytery be answered by:<br>
<br>
- **a.** amending BCO 56-4, lines 29 &amp; 30, which presently reads, &quot;By virtue of being born of believing parents children are...&quot; be changed to read, &quot;By virtue of being children of believing parents they are...&quot;<br>
<br>
- **b.** The current language of BCO 57-5(1) be retained. Explanatory Note: The Committee thought that reference to &quot;born of believing parents&quot; needed to be changed but that &quot;birthright&quot; has always included adopted children.

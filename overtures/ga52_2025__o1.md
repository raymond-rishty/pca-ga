# GA52 O1 — Amend BCO 32-2 to Clarify Court's Authority to Investigate Before Filing Charges

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 1 from Northern New England Presbytery (to CCB, OC)  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga52_2025 p. 1165](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p1165)*

---

## Submitted overture

(to CCB, OC)
“Amend BCO 32-2 to Clarify that a Court May Investigate Whether
Charges Should Be Filed"

Be it resolved that BCO 32-2 be amended by striking the current language and replacing it with the following (deletions indicated by <del>strikethrough</del>; additions by underlining).

Existing language:
32-2. <del>Process against an offender shall not be commenced unless</del>
<del>some person or persons undertake to make out the charge; or unless</del>
<del>the court finds it necessary, for the honor of religion, itself to take the</del>
<del>step provided for in BCO 31 2</del>.

Proposed language:
32-2. Prior to commencing process against an alleged offender,
regardless of how allegations arose, the court shall investigate to the
extent necessary to determine:
1)if it is a personal or general offense

2. if personal, whether Matthew 18 has been followed

3. if the nature of the alleged offense rises to a level warranting judicial process

4. if the accuser is like any of those described in BCO 31-8

5. if there is a strong presumption of guilt

6. if it is reasonable to expect the offense can be proven through judicial process.

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA52 — CCB advice on Overture 1</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
In the opinion of the CCB Overture 1 is in conflict with the Constitution in the following ways:<br>
• The proposed amendment creates a conflicting constitutional threshold regarding the nature of an offense that is subject to judicial process. Specifically, Overture 1 requires that &quot;the alleged offense rises to a level warranting judicial process,&quot; but, according to BCO 29- 1, the &quot;proper object of judicial process” is “anything in the doctrines or practice of a Church member professing faith in Christ which is contrary to the Word of God&quot;;<br>
• The proposed amendment creates a conflicting constitutional threshold regarding the standard of proof required to commence judicial process when charges are presented by a voluntary prosecutor. Specifically, Overture 1 requires that there must be &quot;a strong presumption of guilt&quot; (proposed BCO 32-2.e.) and (assuming the list is conjunctive) that “it is reasonable to expect the offense can be proven through judicial process&quot; (BCO 32-2.f.), but BCO 31-9 only requires &quot;probable cause&quot; for charges from a voluntary prosecutor; and<br>
• The proposed amendment removes agency from someone seeking to become a voluntary prosecutor. Specifically, the logic of the Rules of Discipline presumes that individuals have the right to bring charges and prosecute them voluntarily (see, e.g., BCO 31-6, -7, -9), but Overture 1 places an absolute investigatory and high moral burden on the court in every case where charges are laid before the court by an individual.<br>
<br>
Additionally, in the opinion of CCB, Overture 1 is ambiguous in that it is unclear whether the items listed are conjunctive or disjunctive. Adopted 8-0-0
</blockquote>

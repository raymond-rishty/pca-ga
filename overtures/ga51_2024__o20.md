# GA51 O20 — Amend BCO 31, 32, and 35 to Reform Judicial Process

## Submitted overture

[Note: This overture was passed by the Session of Fountain Square Presbyterian Church on February 1, 2024, submitted to Central Indiana Presbytery at its stated meeting on February 9, 2024, and rejected by Central Indiana Presbytery at that meeting. (The relevant extract of the Presbytery minutes has been provided to the Stated Clerk of the PCA according to RAO 11-10.)]

BE IT

RESOLVED that the Book of Church Order (BCO) Chapters 31, 32, and 35 be amended as follows, and that these proposed amendments be referred to the Study Committee proposed in Overture 4 to the 51st General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America: (deletions are denoted throughout by <del>strikethroughs</del>, additions are underlined).

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA51 — Committee on Constitutional Business advice on Overture 20 (minutes excerpt)</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 20 is in grave conflict with the  <br>
Constitution. By way of example, we note only a few conflicts:  <br>
The Overture is in conflict with BCO 13-1, -2; 43 in that the amended  <br>
BCO 31-4 limits the rights of a member of Presbytery;  <br>
The Overture is in conflict with 1 Timothy 5:19 in that the proposed  <br>
BCO 35-4 permits conviction of a charge without two witnesses;  <br>
The Overture is in conflict with BCO Preliminary Principle 8 in that  <br>
the proposed BCO 31-2 requires mandatory reporting of a mere  <br>
allegation of an offense to &quot;any associated entity&quot; of the accused  <br>
person, in violation of the Preliminary Principle&#x27;s requirement of  <br>
justice because the accused person is effectively rendered guilty  <br>
before any due process and the court or its clerk in the mandatory  <br>
reporting may be guilty of libel or slander; and  <br>
The Overture is in conflict with BCO 8-10, 13-1 in that the limitations  <br>
on representatives in proposed BCO 32-7 remove the right of full  <br>
participation from some members of the Court.  <br>
Adopted 8-0-0
</blockquote>

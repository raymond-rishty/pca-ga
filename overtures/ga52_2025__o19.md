# GA52 O19 — Amend BCO 57-5 to Revise the Membership Vows

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** the Eastern Carolina Presbytery (to CCB, OC) “Amend BCO 57-5 to Revise the Membership Vows  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga52_2025 p. 1205](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p1205)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas our current five membership vows have been unchanged for many years without review;

Whereas our current five membership vows have some antiquated language that must be explained;

Whereas our current five membership vows are inherited from other Reformed denominations, and we are an “ecclesia reformata, semper reformanda" denomination;

Whereas our current five membership vows mention nothing of the love of God for sinners;

Whereas our current five membership vows give no indication of what human beings are saved unto, just what we are saved from;

Therefore, be it resolved that the General Assembly amend BCO 57-5 to revise our membership vows to include 2 addenda and one language update (underlining for additions; <del>strikethrough</del> for deletions):

Add an entire new first vow for membership and renumber the existing vows.

1. Do you agree that you are made in the image of God, designed by God to glorify God and enjoy Him forever?

Insert the words “both beloved and" into the former vow #1.

12. Do you acknowledge yourselves to be both beloved and sinners in the sight of God, justly deserving His displeasure, and without hope save in His sovereign mercy?

Change the word “study” to "promote” in the final vow.

56. Do you submit yourselves to the government and discipline of the Church, and promise to <del>study</del> promote its purity and peace?

1203

So that the amended vows would read as follows:

1. Do you agree that you are made in the image of God, designed by God to glorify God and enjoy Him forever?

2. Do you acknowledge yourselves to be both beloved and sinners in the sight of God, justly deserving His displeasure, and without hope save in His sovereign mercy?

3. Do you believe in the Lord Jesus Christ as the Son of God, and Savior of sinners, and do you receive and rest upon Him alone for salvation as He is offered in the Gospel?

4. Do you now resolve and promise, in humble reliance upon the grace of the Holy Spirit, that you will endeavor to live as becomes the followers of Christ?

5. Do you promise to support the Church in its worship and work to the best of your ability?

6. Do you submit yourselves to the government and discipline of the Church, and promise to promote its purity and peace?

Adopted by the Eastern Carolina Presbytery at its stated meeting, January 25, 2025 Attested by TE Wes Martin, stated clerk

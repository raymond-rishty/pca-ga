# GA52 O9 — Amend BCO 14-1 to Make AC Members Appointed by Committees Non-voting

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** James River Presbytery (to CCB, OC, AC, CC, CDM, CTS, Geneva, MNA, MTW, PCAF, RH, RUF)

*Source: [ga52_2025 PDF p. 1183](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-pdf-p1183)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas the permanent committees and agencies of the General Assembly exist to serve the church and to promote the Great Commission (BCO 14-1.5);

Whereas the PCA has, since its founding, been committed to a grass roots system of church government;

Whereas the General Assembly's Administrative Committee exercises tremendous influence over the PCA's national operations, including the nomination of the Stated Clerk; the preparation of budgets for the permanent committees and agencies; arranging for the General Assembly to meet each year; and the hiring, retention, and dismissal of numerous employees;

Whereas, as presently constituted in BCO 14-1.12 and RAO Article V, the Administrative Committee consists of eleven (11) members elected at large by the General Assembly (BCO 14-1.12.a; RAO 5-1.a) and nine

1181

- **(9)** members appointed by the permanent committees and agencies (BCO 14-1.12.b, RAO 5-1.b);

Whereas this means that 45% of the votes on the Administrative Committee, as presently constituted, are cast by men appointed to the Committee rather than elected;

Whereas, per BCO 14-1.12 and RAO 5-3, “The chief administrative officers of the program Committees and Agencies may attend any meeting of the Administrative Committee. They shall be entitled to the privilege of the floor, but shall not have a vote and must be excluded when an executive session is called;" and

Whereas the PCA will be better served if the voting members of the Administrative Committee are directly elected by the General Assembly, making the Committee more directly accountable to the will of the Assembly; now, therefore,

Be it resolved that BCO 14-1.12 is amended as follows (underlining for additions):

By inserting the words "non-voting, advisory" into the stem of BCO 14-1.12.b between the word "One" and the word "member" and by inserting the following sentence at the end of the second paragraph: "The non-voting, advisory members appointed to the Administrative Committee pursuant to subparagraph (b) above shall have the privilege of the floor but shall not be entitled to vote and must be excluded when an executive session is called." These changes shall result in an amended BCO 14-1.12 that reads:

12. The Assembly permanent committees are the Administrative Committee of General Assembly, Committee on Discipleship Ministries, Committee on Mission to North America, Committee on Mission to the World, and Committee on Reformed University Fellowship. The Administrative Committee of General Assembly (AC) shall consist of twenty (20) members:

- **a.** Eleven members in classes elected through the standard nomination and election procedure,

1182

- **b.** One non-voting, advisory member each from the following program committees or agencies:

1. Committee on Discipleship Ministries (CDM);

2. Covenant College (CC);

3. Covenant Theological Seminary (CTS);

4. Mission to North America (MNA);

5. Mission to the World (MTW);

6. PCA Foundation (PCAF);

7. Geneva Benefits Group, Inc. (Geneva);

8. Reformed University Fellowship (RUF);

9. Ridge Haven Conference Center (RH).

The eleven members at large shall serve a term of four years. The chairman of the Administrative Committee shall be one of its members at large. The non-voting, advisory members appointed to the Administrative Committee, pursuant to subparagraph (b) above, shall have the privilege of the floor but shall not be entitled to vote and must be excluded when an executive session is called. Each program committee and agency shall designate its member each year at the last meeting of the committee or board before the meeting of General Assembly. The chief administrative officers of the program committees and agencies may attend any meeting of the Administrative Committee. They shall be entitled to the privilege of the floor but shall not have a vote and must be excluded when an executive session is called. Committee on Discipleship Ministries, Committee on Mission to North America, Committee on Mission to the World, and Committee on Reformed University Fellowship shall consist of fifteen (15) men divided into five classes of three men each, with two men being TEs and one RE or two men being REs and one TE on alternate years, elected to serve five-year terms. Committees on Discipleship Ministries, Mission to North America, Mission to the World, and Reformed University Fellowship shall have one ruling and one teaching elder as alternates to fill any vacancy that may occur during the year. Persons who have served for a full term, or for at least two years of a partial term, on one of the Assembly's permanent committees or agencies shall not be eligible for re-election to an

1183

Assembly committee until one year has elapsed. (Exceptions may be permitted in agency bylaws approved by the Assembly.)

Be it further resolved that in order to harmonize the language in the Rules of Assembly Operation with this amendment to the Book of Church Order, at the time that this amendment is finally and fully adopted by the General Assembly, having met with the approval of 2/3 of the presbyteries, RAO 5-1.b is amended as follows (underlining for additions):

By inserting the words "non-voting, advisory" in the first line between the words “One” and “member” such that an amended RAO 5-1 reads:

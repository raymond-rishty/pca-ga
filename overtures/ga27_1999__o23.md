# GA27 O23 — Concur with Mid-America on Oklahoma Boundary Transfer to North Texas

**Assembly:** 27th (1999)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 23 From North Texas Presbytery (to MNA)

*Source: [ga27_1999 PDF p. 111](../markdown/ga27_1999.md#ga27-pdf-p111)*

---

## Submitted overture

"Concur with Mid-America on Presbytery Boundaries" M&amp;P that the North Texas Presbytery inform the 27th General Assembly that it concurs with the overture of Mid-America Presbytery to include the Oklahoma Counties of Love, Carter, Murray, Garvin, McClain, Cleveland and Oklahoma in the NTP." Adopted at the 58th Stated Meeting of North Texas Presbytery held April 23-24, 1999. Attested by: /s/ T. John Mulkey, Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Assembly defeats minority substitute proposing a negative answer</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
TE Jeff Black presented a Minority Report as a substitute for Recommendations 14-20. The Moderator ruled that lines &quot;That recommendations 14 – 20 be approved as an omnibus motion regarding the division of Mid-America, North Texas, Covenant and Missouri Presbyteries into new boundaries and that the remaining four churches in Mid- America Presbytery (Oklahoma) work with MNA over the next year to determine which presbytery they may wish to join&quot; would &quot;direct&quot; the remaining 4 churches to work with MNA and are out of order in that they would effectively dissolve a Presbytery without the Presbytery&#x27;s request. The Moderator&#x27;s ruling was appealed and the Moderator was sustained. The question was called on the minority report, and the minority report was defeated.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — ga27_1999 minutes, printed p. 119 (PDF p. 121), 27-31, Minority Report and floor disposition](../markdown/ga27_1999.md#ga27-pdf-p121)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Assembly approves Overture 23 in the omnibus motion</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
That recommendations 14 - 20 be approved as an omnibus motion regarding the division of Mid-America, North Texas, Covenant and Missouri Presbyteries into new boundaries and that the remaining four churches in Mid-America Presbytery (Oklahoma) work with MNA over the next year to determine which presbytery they may wish to join. Adopted
</blockquote>

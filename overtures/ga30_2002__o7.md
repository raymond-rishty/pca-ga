# GA30 O7 — Assert GA Authority Over PCA Committees & Agencies

**Assembly:** 30th (2002)  ·  **Source:** Westminster Presbytery listed three requests for action: 1) "To instruct the PCAF that it  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga30_2002 p. 95](../markdown/ga30_2002.md#ga30-p95)*

---

## Submitted overture

action: 1) “To instruct the PCAF that it must allow members of the General Assembly to inspect the records in accord with the denominational corporation charter and Delaware state law;” 2) “To instruct all of the committees and agencies, including those that may be separately incorporated, of the rights of the members of the General Assembly in this regard;” 3) “That if (the General Assembly) is unable to assert its rightful ownership of and oversight over these agencies, the Assembly begin the process of divesting itself of these agencies.” Grounds: Request 1 [see text below]: 1. The PCAF is fully under and submissive to the General Assembly in all the provisions of the BCO, RAO and its own by-laws and articles of incorporation. 2. This authority and submission is implemented by the Board of Trustees elected at-large by the General Assembly and the work of the Committee of Commissioners and their report to the General Assembly and the subsequent actions of the GA. 3. The Overture originates from a request for information that was denied by the PCAF Board of Trustees in accordance with the General Assembly approved “Confidentiality Policy.” 4. The Overture cites Delaware Civil law; however, the PCAF is incorporated under Georgia law. 5. The Overture appears to use civil law to circumvent the ecclesiastical government of the General Assembly over the PCAF and other Committees and Agencies. Request 2 [see text below]: we deem it as unnecessary because the General Assembly already has a system of oversight. The committees and agencies of the PCA are already submissive to the General Assembly in all the provisions of the BCO and RAO and their own respective bylaws, articles of incorporation or manuals of operations. Request 3 [see text below]: The General Assembly has and does continue to maintain proper oversight over its committees and agencies.

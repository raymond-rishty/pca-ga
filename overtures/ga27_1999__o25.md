# GA27 O25 — Concur with Mid-America Overture on Arkansas County Boundary Transfer to Covenant

**Assembly:** 27th (1999)  ·  **Source:** Missouri Presbytery (to MNA) "Concur with Mid-America on Presbytery Boundaries"

*Source: [ga27_1999 p. 112](../markdown/ga27_1999.md#ga27-p112)*

---

## Submitted overture

"Concur with Mid-America on Presbytery Boundaries"

Whereas, Providence Presbyterian Church, Springfield, Missouri, was originally founded as a mission church of the St. Louis Presbytery of the former Reformed Presbyterian Church, Evangelical Synod; and

Whereas, Providence Presbyterian Church desires to unite with other PCA congregations in Missouri towards mutual goals for the promotion of the Gospel within the boundaries of Missouri; and

Whereas, this includes the establishment of mission churches in the state and the goal of an RUF ministry at Southwest Missouri State University; and

Whereas, the present affiliation with Mid-America Presbytery is not best suited in meeting these goals, Therefore, Be it Resolved that Missouri Presbytery respectfully requests the 27th General Assembly to transfer the sw missouri counties of Greene, Christian, Taney, Lawrence, Stone, Barry, Jasper, Newton and McDonald to Missouri Presbytery, effective 1 July 1999. adopted at the spring stated meeting of missouri presbytery on 20 April 1999. Attested by: Is/ Scott E. Churnock, Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Assembly defeats minority substitute proposing a negative answer</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
TE Jeff Black presented a Minority Report as a substitute for Recommendations 14-20. The Moderator ruled that lines &quot;That recommendations 14 – 20 be approved as an omnibus motion regarding the division of Mid-America, North Texas, Covenant and Missouri Presbyteries into new boundaries and that the remaining four churches in Mid- America Presbytery (Oklahoma) work with MNA over the next year to determine which presbytery they may wish to join&quot; would &quot;direct&quot; the remaining 4 churches to work with MNA and are out of order in that they would effectively dissolve a Presbytery without the Presbytery&#x27;s request. The Moderator&#x27;s ruling was appealed and the Moderator was sustained. The question was called on the minority report, and the minority report was defeated.
</blockquote>

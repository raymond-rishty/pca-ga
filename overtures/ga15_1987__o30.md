# GA15 O30 — Amend BCO 19-6 to Drop Unlicensed Candidates from Presbytery Rolls

**Assembly:** 15th (1987)  ·  **Source:** Philadelphia Presbytery

*Source: [ga15_1987 PDF p. 54](../markdown/ga15_1987.md#ga15-p54)*

---

## Submitted overture

It is our recommendation that the phrase, "A person who fails to renew his licensure before its expiration date will be dropped from the rolls of Presbytery" be added as a final sentence in BCO 19-6. Adopted at the March 14, 1987 Stated Meeting of the Philadelphia Presbytery. Attest: Iain Crichton, Stated Clerk Adopted as amended. See 15-83, III, 36, p. 178 for amended text.

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Permanent Judicial Business recommends referral of Overture 30</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
36. That Overture 30 from the Presbytery of Philadelphia be found in order and referred to the Committee of Commissioners on Judicial Business with the notation that the language needs clarification:<br>
<br>
1. The roll is not specified.<br>
<br>
2. The procedure is not identified.<br>
<br>
3. An individual does not renew his license. It is renewed by the Presbytery at its discretion.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt · 15th GA minutes, Appendix J, printed p. 340 (PDF p. 342)](../markdown/ga15_1987.md#ga15-p342)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Committee recommends an amended BCO 19-6 answer</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
36. That Overture #30, from Philadelphia Presbytery (15-3, p. 52) be answered by amending BCO 19-6 by adding the following sentences at the end: &quot;The licentiate must apply for renewal prior to expiration. If the license expires, the Stated Clerk shall report the expiration to the presbytery and to the individual&#x27;s session, and such action shall be recorded in the minutes. The procedures of BCO 19-2 must be followed for re-licensure and such fact shall be recorded in the minutes.&quot;
</blockquote>

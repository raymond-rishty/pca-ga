# GA19 O14 — Instruct Clerk Regarding Constitutionality of Appeals

**Assembly:** 19th (1991)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 14 From the Presbytery of the Ascension (to B&O) "Instruct Clerk re: Constitutionality of Appeals"

*Source: [ga19_1991 PDF p. 60](../markdown/ga19_1991.md#ga19-pdf-p60)*

---

## Submitted overture

*The GA19 docket lists the title and referral but does not reproduce the submitted proposal text. The cited minutes PDF page was reviewed; the text remains unavailable.*


## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA19 - Appendix C negative recommendation on Overture 14</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
39. That Overture 14 be answered in the negative. GROUNDS: The Stated Clerk was responding to an appeal to reopen a case which the 16th GA had turned down in 1988 (see 16MGA 16-77, III, 25, p. 176).
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt - GA19 minutes, printed p. 269 (PDF p. 273)](../markdown/ga19_1991.md#ga19-pdf-p273)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA19 - Committee of Commissioners recommends an affirmative answer as amended</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
11. That the Assembly answer Overture 14 in the affirmative, as amended: Now be it resolved that the 19th General Assembly, instruct the Clerk of the General Assembly that the constitutionality of all judicial appeals and complaints to the General Assembly shall be adjudged only by the Standing Judicial Commission. Grounds: See BCO 42-8.
</blockquote>

*Source: [GA19 minutes, printed p. 167 (PDF p. 169)](../markdown/ga19_1991.md#ga19-pdf-p169)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA19 - Assembly adopts affirmative answer as amended</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Adopted
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt - GA19 minutes, printed p. 167 (PDF p. 169)](../markdown/ga19_1991.md#ga19-pdf-p169)*

# GA14 O52 — Require General Assembly Arrangements to Use College or Conference Center Facilities

**Assembly:** 14th (1986)  ·  **Source:** Ascension Presbytery

*Source: [ga14_1986 PDF p. 46](../markdown/ga14_1986.md#ga14-p46)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas there has been much concern throughout the General Assembly and the Church-at-large at the huge expenditures to have and accomodate the General Assembly; and

Whereas in last year’s denominational survey of churches not sending delegates to General Assembly the largest response for not attending was the high cost of attending the General Assembly; and

Whereas we are commanded by our Lord to be good stewards of His resources; and

Whereas the General Assembly is a biblical and necessary part of the Presbyterian Church in America; and

Whereas we should help and encourage all of the eligible brethren to attend and participate;

Be it resolved that each year’s General Assembly arrangements committee be limited to use the facilities of colleges or conference centers, so that we might maintain a reasonable cost for all of the commissioners. Attest: Frank D. Moser, Stated Clerk See 14-74, III, 19, p. 171.

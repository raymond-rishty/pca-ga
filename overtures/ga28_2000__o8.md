# GA28 O8 — Evangelical Presbyterian Church Receiving Suspended PCA Teaching Elder

**Assembly:** 28th (2000)  ·  **Source:** Illiana Presbytery (to IRC)  ·  **Disposition:** Referred

*Source: [ga28_2000 p. 66](../markdown/ga28_2000.md#ga28-p66)*

---

## Submitted overture

"Evangelical Presbyterian Church Receiving Suspended PCA Teaching Elder"

Whereas, Illiana Presbytery imposed a censure of indefinite suspension on TE David W. Baer in 1998, and

Whereas, Illiana Presbytery’s action of censure was communicated in the Spring of 1999 to TE Corbett Heimburger, Chairman of the Evangelical Presbyterian Church’s Mid-America Presbytery’s Candidates and Credentials Committee, and

Whereas, TE Heimburger indicated that the EPC would not receive TE Baer as long as he was under discipline in the PCA, and

Whereas, that censure has not been lifted by Illiana Presbytery but has been left in force by a Subcommittee of the General Assembly’s Standing Judicial Commission which declined the appeal of TE Baer in June of 1999, and

Whereas, the EPC Mid-America Presbytery examined and voted to receive TE Baer into their fellowship on October 23, 1999 despite his still being under censure in the PCA, and

Whereas, the EPC Mid-America Presbytery at the same meeting approved the appointment of TE Baer as organizing pastor of Trinity Fellowship Church, composed of people who had left TE Baer’s former PCA Church, Westminster PCA, in Godfrey, Illinois, and

Whereas, the EPC Mid-America Presbytery rejected the Illiana Presbytery’s Censure and the SJC Subcommittee’s decision as being in error, Therefore, Illiana Presbytery respectfully overtures the General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to take this matter into advisement in regard to current or future relations with the EPC and to assign the Interchurch Relations Committee to investigate this action by the EPC and to recommend an appropriate response and/or actions to be taken by PCA churches that have relations with EPC denominational churches in their area. Adopted at the Winter Stated Meeting of Illiana Presbytery on January 15, 2000. Attested by: /s/ Dale L. Eisenreich, Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA28 — Assembly adopts the response to Overture 8 (minutes excerpt)</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
5. Overture 8-2000 from Illiana Presbytery, &quot;Evangelical Presbyterian Church Receiving a Suspended PCA Teaching Elder&quot; be answered by the General Assembly&#x27;s adopting the following response to Overture 8: a.) That the 28th General Assembly of the PCA recognizes that, while the EPC may have acted within its own constitutional authority when it received into its membership, Rev. David Baer, a TE under the discipline of Illiana Presbytery of the PCA, nevertheless, such action by the EPC represents a disregard of biblical judicial process and the judgment that our Lord declares to be His own (Matt. 18:15-18). This action undermines the goodwill that has characterized the relationship between our denominations. Adopted b.) That the General Assembly of the PCA requests that the EPC honor the judicial process and the ecclesiastical censures of the courts of this church. Adopted c.) That the General Assembly direct the Stated Clerk of the PCA and the Chairman of the PCA Interchurch Relations Committee to meet with the Stated Clerk of the EPC and the Chairman of their corresponding committee to find a way to respect each other&#x27;s judicial processes in similar situations for the reputation of Christ and the peace of His Church and to likewise address other Reformed bodies who apparently disregard our discipline when requested by a PCA presbytery and propose resolution to like disciplinary issues. Adopted as amended
</blockquote>

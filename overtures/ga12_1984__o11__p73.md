# GA12 O11 — Amend BCO 19-2 to Require Written Subscription Statement at Licensure

**Assembly:** 12th (1984)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Grace Whereas, the Tenth General Assembly directed the Permanent Sub-com

*Source: [ga12_1984 PDF p. 73](../markdown/ga12_1984.md#ga12-p73)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Tenth General Assembly directed the Permanent Sub-committee on Judicial Business to prepare an amendment to the BCO setting forth a procedure by which ordinands shall set forth in writing a declaration of any scruple they might have with regard to the confession of faith and Catechisms', and

Whereas, the status of licensure provides Presbytery with opportunity to form a better judgment of and to counsel with those who may be, in due time, ordained to the ministry of the Word,

Therefore, be it resolved that the Presbytery of Grace overture the Eleventh General Assembly to amend BCO 19-2 by the addition of a new sub-paragraph E, which shall read in such or similar language as the following: “ E. Read before the Presbytery and file the same with the stated clerk a written statement of his subscription to the confession of faith and catechisms and of any scruple that he might have with regard to any part of them.” Attest: vaughn E. hathaway , jr ., stated clerk Answered in the negative by 12th General Assembly, 12-53, II, 64, p. 141, thus carried over to the Thirteenth General Assembly

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Permanent Committee recommends an affirmative answer with revised subscription language</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
13. That Overture 11 from Grace Presbytery to the Eleventh General Assembly to amend BCO 19-2 by adding a new paragraph E. be answered in the affirmative. The new paragraph E. is to read, as follows: &quot;Presbytery shall require that all applicants for licensure subscribe to the following statements: &#x27;I.., do sincerely receive and adopt the Confession of Faith and Catechisms of this Church without any exceptions or reservations.&#x27;; or &#x27;I,..., do sincerely receive and adopt the Confession of Faith and Catechisms of this Church with the following exceptions or reservations to wit,...&#x27; The Presbytery shall record its evaluation of each exception or reservation so stated as to its seriousness as a departure from the Confessional articles. Acceptance of statements of exceptions or reservations in such cases requires a threefourths approval of the Presbytery.&quot;
</blockquote>

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Permanent Committee proposes uniform BCO language for licensure and ordination</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
33. That in order to provide uniform language to record scruples, the following amendments to the BCO be adopted:<br>
<br>
1. Amend BCO 19-2 to add a final paragraph:<br>
&#x27;Presbytery shall require that all applicants for licensure subscribe to one of the following statements: &#x27;I, ______________, do sincerely receive and adopt the Confession of Faith and Catechisms of this Church without any exceptions or reservations.&#x27;; or &#x27;I, ______________, declare the following exceptions or reservations with regard to adoption of the Confession of Faith and Catechisms of this Church, to wit....&#x27; The Presbytery shall record its evaluation of each exception or reservation so stated as to its seriousness as a departure from the Confessional articles. Acceptance of an applicant stating such exceptions or reservations shall require a three-fourths approval of Presbytery.&#x27;<br>
<br>
2. Amend BCO 21 by the addition of a new paragraph 5 as follows, and the remaining paragraphs be renumbered:<br>
&#x27;Presbytery shall require that all applicants for ordination subscribe to one of the following statements: &#x27;I, ______________, do sincerely receive and adopt the Confession of Faith and Catechisms of this Church and approve of the form of government and rules of discipline of the Presbyterian Church in America without any exceptions or reservations.&#x27;; or &#x27;I, ______________, declare the following exceptions or reservations with regard to adoption of the Confession of Faith and Catechisms of this Church or to the approval of the form of government and rules of discipline of the Presbyterian Church in America, to wit....&#x27; The Presbytery shall record its evaluation of each exception or reservation so stated as to its seriousness as a departure from the Confessional articles. Acceptance of an applicant stating such exceptions or reservations shall require a three-fourths approval of Presbytery.&#x27;
</blockquote>

*Source: [14th GA minutes, printed p. 338 (PDF p. 340), Appendix I, Report of the Committee on Judicial Business, Recommendation 33; PDF image checked](../markdown/ga14_1986.md#ga14-p340)*

# GA14 O25 — Amend BCO to Allow Internship Requirements to Be Met Prior to Licensure

**Assembly:** 14th (1986)  ·  **Source:** Evangel Presbytery

*Source: [ga14_1986 PDF p. 53](../markdown/ga14_1986.md#ga14-pdf-p53)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Book of Church Order of the Presbyterian Church in America requires that candidates for the ministry serve at least one year of internship prior to ordination to the ministry, and,

Whereas, said Book of Church Order - Eighth Edition printed in 1984 requires that said candidate must be a licentiate prior to serving his internship, and,

Whereas, experience in the Presbytery of Evangel during the past five years has shown that some candidates prior to licensure have already met some of the requirements they would normally fulfill during the period of internship, and a repetition of the fulfillment of these requirements would serve no useful purpose, now therefore,

Be it resolved that the Book of Church Order be amended to permit, at the discretion of the presbytery, the intern to fulfill some of the internship requirements prior to licensure. Adopted at the Winter Stated Meeting of Evangel Presbytery, January 28, 1986. Attest: Hubert C. Stewart, Stated Clerk Answered by response to Overture 4. See 14-52, 31, p. 127.

# GA28 O21 — Take Disciplinary Measures Against Tennessee Valley Presbytery

**Assembly:** 28th (2000)  ·  **Source:** Louisiana Presbytery "Disciplinary Measures Against Tennessee Valley Presbytery"

*Source: [ga28_2000 PDF p. 80](../markdown/ga28_2000.md#ga28-p80)*

---

## Submitted overture

“Disciplinary Measures Against Tennessee Valley Presbytery”

**Whereas**, several presbyteries of the Presbyterian Church in America (PCA) have formally brought to the attention of Tennessee Valley Presbytery (TVP) the fact that one of its churches has allowed women to preach; and

**Whereas**, TVP, to date, has refused to deal adequately with the situation; and

Whereas, our Constitution tells us that it is “incumbent” upon a higher court that has been “well advised” of serious neglect or irregularity, to “take cognizance of the same, and to examine, deliberate and judge in the whole matter” (BCO 40-4), and

Whereas, the 14th General Assembly, in approving the report of the blue-ribbon Ad Interim Committee on the General Assembly, approved the following principle: “In the event the lower court does nothing [with regard to discipline], by virtue of its ecclesiastical authority the higher court may (a) ignore the failure to act, or (b) counsel, advise, exhort, and urge the lower court to comply, or (c) reprimand or rebuke the lower court, or (d) suspend one or all of the ecclesiastical privileges of the lower court with respect to the higher courts -- e.g., to overture or reference a matter to the higher courts, to vote upon amendments to the Standards, to vote at the higher courts, or even to have commissioners at the higher courts, or (e) as a last resort ‘act against’ the lower court by dismissing it from the fellowship” (M14GA, pp. 103, 436); and

Whereas, given the great turmoil which TVP’s failure to act has engendered throughout the PCA, it does not seem prudent for the General Assembly to ignore this willful failure, and

Whereas, since sister presbyteries have already counseled and urged TVP to act in a satisfactory manner and it has refused to heed this advice, it seems that the second option has already, in essence, been exercised, and

Whereas, we do not want to proceed immediately to the drastic measure of exscinding TVP from our fellowship without giving it at least one more opportunity to repent; Now Therefore, Be it Resolved, that Louisiana Presbytery hereby overtures the 28th General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to take appropriate disciplinary measures against Tennessee Valley Presbytery for its failure to uphold the standards of our church, said disciplinary measures being that of a reprimand or rebuke, along with the pronouncement that if TVP does not act in a satisfactory fashion by the time of the 29th General Assembly, its ecclesiastical privileges of having commissioners seated at the General Assembly and of having representatives from the Presbytery to be nominated to any General Assembly committees, commission, board or agency, will be revoked; And Be It Further Resolved, that Louisiana Presbytery hereby overtures the 28th General Assembly to enact these measures by any lawful means possible, including, if deemed necessary, the conduct of a trial on the floor of the assembly (BCO 40-5 and 40-6). Adopted by Louisiana Presbytery at the 63rd Stated Meeting, Opelousas, Louisiana, on April 15, 2000 Attested by: /s/ James A. Jones, Jr., Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA28 — CCB finds Overture 21 inconsistent with the Constitution (minutes excerpt)</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
OVERTURE 21 – From Louisiana Presbytery, &quot;Disciplinary Measures Against Tennessee Valley Presbytery.&quot; Response – It is the opinion of the CCB that Overture 21 as worded is not consistent with our Constitution since it pre-determines the guilt of a presbytery and prescribes censures. In order for such a determination to be made, and such a censure to be administered, then the procedures of BCO 40-4 through 40-6 would need to be followed. These procedures always provide for a hearing of specific charges in an appropriate court and with opportunity for defense before any individual or entity is reprimanded or rebuked. The Review and Control process is one way that such a matter might properly come before the Assembly so that a presbytery&#x27;s actions could be reviewed. Additionally our Constitution does not allow for the &quot;the conduct of a trial on the floor of the Assembly&quot; since all judicial matters are referred to the SJC (BCO 15-1); and this matter could be referred to the SJC by the GA (BCO 15-4). The response was adopted by proper motion, 8-0-0.
</blockquote>

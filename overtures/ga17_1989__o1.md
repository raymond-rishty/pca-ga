# GA17 O1 — Make BCO 19-10 Declaration Optional

**Assembly:** 17th (1989)  ·  **Source:** Delmarva Presbytery

*Source: [ga17_1989 PDF p. 161](../markdown/ga17_1989.md#ga17-p161)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas the necessity of reading these sentences (BCO 19-10) may present hardships upon a candidate, i.e. one who is in a seminary 1000 miles away at the time of the Presbytery meeting. Therefore: We, the Delmarva Presbytery do overture the General Assembly to make the declaration of BCO 19-10 optional by changing BCO 19-10 to read as follows: "When an applicant is... the Moderator of the Presbytery shall offer a prayer suitable for the occasion, and shall address the applicant, if present, as follows...." Adopted at the 24th meeting of Delmarva Presbytery. Attest: Bruce B. Howes, Stated Clerk

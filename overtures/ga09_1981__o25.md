# GA9 O25 — Clarify the Boundary Between Central Georgia and North Georgia Presbyteries

**Assembly:** 9th (1981)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Central Georgia Whereas, the boundaries between Central Georgia Presbyte

*Source: [ga09_1981 PDF p. 51](../markdown/ga09_1981.md#ga9-pdf-p51)*

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the boundaries between Central Georgia Presbytery and North Georgia Presbytery are not clearly defined, and

Whereas, Central Georgia Presbytery seeks to establish new churches in the northern part of the Presbytery Therefore, Central Georgia Presbytery respectfully overtures the Ninth General Assembly to carefully define the boundaries between Central Georgia Presbytery and North Georgia Presbytery to include Spaulding County in Central Georgia Presbytery. Adopted at the Spring Meeting of Central Georgia Presbytery, April 14, 1981. Attest: Walter Worsham, Stated Clerk Presbytery of Central Georgia Answered in the negative. §9-35, III, 11, p. 87 TO COMMITTEE OF COMMISSIONERS ON MISSION TO THE WORLD

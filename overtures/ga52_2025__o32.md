# GA52 O32 — Amend BCO 36 to Allow Elevation of Censure Without Entirely New Trial

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** Houston Metro Presbytery (to CCB, OC)

*Source: [ga52_2025 PDF p. 1268](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-pdf-p1268)*

---

## Submitted overture

Be it resolved that BCO 36 be amended to add a new paragraph, BCO 36-8, as follows (underlining for additions):

<table><tbody><tr><td>36-8. When members of a court wish to consider the elevation of an</td></tr><tr><td>indefinite suspension, whether from office or from the Sacraments</td></tr><tr><td>(BCO 36-5) to the censure of deposition or excommunication, it shall</td></tr><tr><td>not be required to initiate process de novo according to BCO 31-2 or</td></tr><tr><td>BCO 32-2. The indefinitely suspended person is not in good standing</td></tr><tr><td>and has already been found impenitent, resulting in indefinite</td></tr><tr><td>suspension (BCO 30-3). However, the indefinitely suspended person</td></tr><tr><td>has a right to a hearing in the matter. The court supervising the</td></tr></tbody></table>

indefinite suspension may consider a motion to find him "incorrigible
and contumacious" (cf. BCO 30-4) and present evidence to that effect.
The court shall give at least ten (10) days' notice of such a motion to
the indefinitely suspended person and permit him the privilege of the
floor to plead and give a defense before the original trial court. The
court, upon completing its hearing, shall vote to determine whether or
not to elevate the indefinite suspension to deposition or
excommunication.

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA52 — CCB advice on Overture 32</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 32 is ambiguous in that it is not clear whether the &quot;hearing&quot; in the added paragraph is governed by other provisions in the Rules of Discipline, such as BCO 32-10, -13, -15, -16, -17, -18; and BCO 35, and whether the accused has the right to Appeal an elevated suspension.<br>
<br>
CCB notes that the phrase “original trial court” is potentially ambiguous in that it is not clear if the phrase refers to the exact commission or group of presbyters who heard the evidence at the original trial; whether the phrase refers simply to the Session or Presbytery in which the trial was held; or whether the phrase could include another court that has assumed jurisdiction under BCO 37-7.<br>
<br>
CCB also notes that this overture is in conflict with Overture 38. Adopted 8-0-0
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt · ga52_2025 minutes, printed pp. 256–257 (PDF pp. 258–259), Appendix C, item U](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-pdf-p258)*

# GA8 O7 — Study committee on dispensationalism vs. covenant theology

**Assembly:** 8th (1980)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of North Georgia  ·  **Disposition:** Answered by reference

*Source: [ga08_1980 p. 47](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga08-p47)*

---

The North Georgia Presbytery respectfully overtures the General Assembly to set up an Ad Interim Committee for the purpose of studying the relationship between dispensationalism and covenant theology. Adopted by North Georgia Presbytery at its stated meeting, January 2 1, 1978. Attest: Robert G. Valentine Staled Clerk Answered in the negative. § 8-29, III, 26; p. 74.

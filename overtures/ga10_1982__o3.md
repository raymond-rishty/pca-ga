# GA10 O3 — Amend BCO 11-3 to Allow Presbyteries to Elect a Moderator for One Year

**Assembly:** 10th (1982)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Pacific Be It Resolved that the Book of Church Order Chapter 11-3 be ame

*Source: [ga10_1982 PDF p. 45](../markdown/ga10_1982.md#ga10-p45)*

---

## Submitted overture

Be It Resolved that the Book of Church Order Chapter 11-3 be amended so that the second sentence shall read: The Moderator of the Presbytery and of the General Assembly shall be chosen at each stated meeting of these courts, except that presbyteries may, if they desire, elect a Moderator to serve for a term of one year. Taken from the September 25, 1980, Minutes of Pacific Presbytery Meeting. WILLIAM F. WOODHALL, STATED CLERK (Received too late for consideration by the Ninth General Assembly.) Answered in the affirmative as amended §10-75, III, 13, p. 102.

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The Committee of Commissioners recommends an affirmative answer with amended wording</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
13. That Overture 3, (p. 43) be answered in the affirmative as amended: Be it resolved that the Book of Church Order chapter 10-3 be amended so that the second sentence shall read: The Moderator of the Presbytery may be elected at each stated meeting of the court. or for a period of up to one year. The Moderator of the General Assembly shall be chosen at each stated meeting. Grounds: To provide flexibility in the work of presbyteries and accommodate former RPCES practices.

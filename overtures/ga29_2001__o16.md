<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA30 sent-down amendment text (minutes excerpt)</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
43-1 A complaint is a written representation made against some act or decision of a court of the Church. It is the right of any communing member of the church in good standing to make complaint against any action of a court to whose jurisdiction he is subject, except that no complaint is to be considered in a judicial case while an appeal is <del>taken</del> pending.
</blockquote>

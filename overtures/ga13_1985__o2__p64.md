# GA13 O2 — Strike BCO 38-2 Regarding Voluntary Abstention from the Lord's Supper

**Assembly:** 13th (1985)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Louisiana

*Source: [ga13_1985 p. 64](../markdown/ga13_1985.md#ga13-p64)*

---

Whereas, the only proper reason for abstaining from the Lord's table would be that one faces judicial charges, or is presently under judicial censure, or struggles personally with moral conviction of unrepeated sin, we therefore find no biblical or experiential support for the case described in *BCO* 38-2, and

Whereas, for a person under discipline, the suspension from the table of the Lord is by no means a permanent condition but leads to further discipline and possibly excommunication, and

Whereas, a person who abstains from communion, if he be truly a Christian, cannot be content to remain indefinitely in abstention, and should not be encouraged to remain so, Therefore, we overture the Tenth General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to strike 38-2 from the BCO altogether. Attest: Baker S. Smith, Stated Clerk Carried over for one more year of study, 12-53, II, 16, p. 127. See 13-45, III, 11, p. 103. 12-10, D, p. 71

# GA28 O4 — Clarify Use of Professional Counsel in Cases of Process

**Assembly:** 28th (2000)  ·  **Source:** James River Presbytery (to CCB & B&O)

*Source: [ga28_2000 PDF p. 291](../markdown/ga28_2000.md#ga28-pdf-p291)*

---

## Submitted overture

"Clarify Use of Professional Counsel in Cases of Process"

Whereas, BCO 32-19 forbids the use of professional counsel "as such" in cases
of process but permits an accused to be represented before the Session of
the church of the accused by a member of that church, and before any
other court by a member of that court; and

Whereas, the phrase "as such" is subject to different interpretations; and

Whereas, the limitation on representation before a court higher than the Session
may destroy the continuity of representation of the accused and
otherwise prejudice his defense;

Therefore, Be it Resolved that the James River Presbytery respectfully overtures the General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to clarify, amend, and restate BCO 32-19 so that it will read in its entirety as follows:

32-19
An accused person may be counseled and
represented before the Session or any other court by any
communing member of the same particular church of which
the accused is a member, or by any member of or
commissioner to a court before which the accused appears;
provided, however, that the individual serving as counsel
may not serve for a fee or other remuneration.
Notwithstanding the foregoing, payments may be made to
reimburse or cover any direct expenses incurred or to be
incurred by such counsel in the course of representation. If
such counsel is a member of or commissioner to the court
before which the accused appears, he may not sit in judgment
in the case.

Adopted at the Spring Stated Meeting of James River Presbytery on April 8, 2000. Attested by: /s/ Eugene H. Friedline, Stated Clerk

That BCO 32-19 be amended by deleting the current language (indicated by strikeouts) and replacing it with the following new language.<br>
<br>
32-19. No professional counsel shall be permitted as such to appear and  <br>
plead in cases of process in any court; but an accused person may, if he  <br>
desires it, be represented before the Session by any communing member of  <br>
the same particular chureh, or before any other court, by any member of that  <br>
court. A member of the court so employed shall not be allowed to sit in  <br>
judgment in the case.<br>
<br>
32-19. The accused shall be entitled to be represented before the courts of this<br>
church by counsel, who shall have the right to be heard by oral and/or written<br>
argument. Any such counsel shall be a communing member of the Presbyterian<br>
Church in America. Any member of a court so engaged shall not be allowed to<br>
sit in judgment of the case. No person shall accept any fee or other remuneration<br>
for any service rendered as counsel, but reimbursement may be made to cover<br>
any personal expenses incurred or to be incurred by such counsel in the course<br>
of representation.
</blockquote>

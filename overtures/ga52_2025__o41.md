# GA52 O41 — Direct the AC to Collect and Report Each Congregation's Public Worship Service Times

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** the Calvary Presbytery (to AC) “Direct the AC to Collect & Report Each Congregation's Publ  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga52_2025 p. 1288](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p1288)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas the worship of God, and particularly the corporate public worship of God, is vital to the Christian faith (WLC 117); and,

Whereas proper observance of the Fourth Commandment requires due diligence and preparation (WLC 121); and

Whereas due diligence and preparation requires knowing when particular congregations will gather for public worship; and,

Whereas the charge of keeping the Sabbath is more specially directed to superiors (WLC 118); and

Whereas information about public worship service times are at present occasionally unavailable and often tedious for individuals to collect through publicly accessible channels; and,

Therefore, be it resolved that the 52nd General Assembly direct the Administrative Committee to collect and report each congregation's public worship service times (inclusive of all stated worship services) on the church directory map available at https://www.pcaac.org/churchdirectory/.

Therefore, be it further resolved that the 52nd General Assembly encourage the Administrative Committee to seek the assistance of the several Presbyteries in this task.

Adopted by Calvary Presbytery at its stated meeting of April 24, 2025. Attested by /s/ RE Melton L. Duncan, Stated Clerk

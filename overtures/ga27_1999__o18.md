# GA27 O18 — Change Boundaries Between Mid-America and North Texas Presbyteries

**Assembly:** 27th (1999)  ·  **Source:** Mid-America Presbytery (to MNA) "Change the Boundaries Between Mid-America and North Texas

*Source: [ga27_1999 PDF p. 109](../markdown/ga27_1999.md#ga27-p109)*

---

## Submitted overture

"Change the Boundaries Between Mid-America and North Texas Presbyteries"

Whereas, Heritage Presbyterian Church, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, desires to unite with the north texas presbytery towards the mutual goal of the proclamation of the gospel; and,

Whereas, Fleritage Presbyterian Church, believing that her ministry will be able to serve and be adequately served in the North Texas Presbytery, THEREFORE, BE IT THEREFORE

RESOLVED, that Mid-America Presbytery respectfully overtures the 27th General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to re-draw the boundaries of Mid- America and North Texas Presbyteries to transfer the 1-35 corridor counties of Love, Carter, Murray, Garvin, McClain, Cleveland, and Oklahoma in the State of Oklahoma from Mid-America Presbytery to North Texas Presbytery, effective 1 July 1999. In Action Taken at the 36th stated meeting of Mid-America Presbytery, 9 April 1999. Attested by: /s/ John O. Butler, Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Assembly considers and defeats the minority substitute</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
TE Jeff Black presented a Minority Report as a substitute for Recommendations 14-20. The Moderator ruled that lines &quot;That recommendations 14 – 20 be approved as an omnibus motion regarding the division of Mid-America, North Texas, Covenant and Missouri Presbyteries into new boundaries and that the remaining four churches in Mid- America Presbytery (Oklahoma) work with MNA over the next year to determine which presbytery they may wish to join&quot; would &quot;direct&quot; the remaining 4 churches to work with MNA and are out of order in that they would effectively dissolve a Presbytery without the Presbytery&#x27;s request. The Moderator&#x27;s ruling was appealed and the Moderator was sustained. The question was called on the minority report, and the minority report was defeated.
</blockquote>

# GA52 O46 — Change the Boundaries of Georgia Foothills and Metro Atlanta Presbyteries

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** the Metro Atlanta Presbytery (to MNA) "Change the Boundaries of Georgia Foothills and Metr

*Source: [ga52_2025 p. 1299](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p1299)*

## Submitted overture

Whereas Lumpkin County, GA is the farthest northern point of Metro Atlanta Presbytery; and

Whereas it lies geographically in the northeast Georgia mountains, near many churches within the current boundary of Georgia Foothills Presbytery; and

Whereas Georgia Foothills Presbytery desires to start an RUF campus ministry at University of North Georgia located in the city of Dahlonega in Lumpkin County; and

Whereas the Session of Creekstone Church, the only PCA church located in Lumpkin County, is agreeable to come into Georgia Foothills Presbytery; and

Whereas the minutes of the 46th General Assembly established the boundary of Georgia Foothills Presbytery to be the Georgia counties of Hall, Barrow, Jackson, Clarke, Oconee, Habersham, and Union counties; also the counties of Oglethorpe, Elbert, Madison, Hart, Franklin, Banks, White, Stephens, Rabun, and Towns; and Gwinnett county north of the defined boundaries, from east to west, of Highway 316, then Sugarloaf Parkway to Peachtree Industrial Boulevard, then an imaginary line westward from the intersection of Peachtree Industrial Boulevard and Sugarloaf Parkway to the Chattahoochee River; and

Whereas the minutes of the 46th General Assembly established the boundary of Metro Atlanta Presbytery to be the Georgia counties of Dekalb, Fulton, Clayton, Cobb (East of I-75), Dawson, Lumpkin, Coweta, Heard, Spalding, Troup, Merriweather, Pike, Fayette, Forsyth, Rockdale, Newton, Henry, and Walton; and Gwinnett county south of the defined boundaries, from east to west, of Highway 316, then Sugarloaf Parkway to Peachtree Industrial Boulevard, then an imaginary line westward from the intersection of Peachtree Industrial Boulevard and Sugarloaf Parkway to the Chattahoochee River;

1297

Therefore be it resolved that Metro Atlanta Presbytery overtures the 52nd General Assembly as follows: That Lumpkin County be transferred from Metro Atlanta Presbytery to Georgia Foothills Presbytery; By adding Lumpkin County to the list of Georgia counties included within the boundary of Georgia Foothills Presbytery; and By removing Lumpkin County from the list of Georgia counties included within the boundary of Metro Atlanta Presbytery.

Adopted by the Metro Atlanta Presbytery at its stated meeting, May 6, 2025 Attested by TE Randy Schlichting, stated clerk

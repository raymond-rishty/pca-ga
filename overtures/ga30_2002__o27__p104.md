# GA30 O27 — Add to BCO 26-1 Procedure for Conflicts Between Scripture & Constitution

**Assembly:** 30th (2002)  ·  **Source:** Eastern Canada Presbytery: Add to BCO 26-1 Procedure for Dealing with Alleged Conflicts Be  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga30_2002 p. 104](../markdown/ga30_2002.md#ga30-p104)*

---

for dealing with alleged conflicts between scripture and the constitution of the PCA It is the opinion of the CCB that the Overture is in conflict with other parts of the Constitution. Specific areas of conflict include, but are not limited to: 1. The Overture would allow a General Assembly to avoid the provisions of the BCO without appropriate constitutional process and would result in serious constitutional uncertainty. (BCO 26-2). 2. The Overture allows modification of virtually any action of the General Assembly or commission thereof, with the sole limitation that an actual judgment of a judicial commission may not be changed or deleted, thereby undermining the very principle of orderly Church Government which the BCO is designed to protect. [See BCO Preface II.2], 3. The Overture is in conflict with BCO 15.1 and 15.5, in that it allows for modification of a commission report. 4. The Overture requires the CCB to propose business, inconsistent with RAO 7-2 and the Operations Manual for CCB. 5. Finally, reference is made to the recommendation of the CCB with respect to Constitutional Inquiry #4 of the 29th General Assembly: “The presumption that we need to suspend our rules in order to obey Christ is an opinion that the CCB is not willing to affirm. i four standards are shown to be out of accord with Scripture, then there are proper procedures to follow, at each level, in order to change those standards. ” [For text and action, see 30-53, III, 18, p. 273.] Adopted by CCB

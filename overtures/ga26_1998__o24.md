# GA26 O24 — Amend BCO 58-4 to Clarify Fencing of the Lord's Table

**Assembly:** 26th (1998)  ·  **Source:** New Life Presbyterian Church "Amend BCO 58-4 to Clarify the Fencing of the Lord's Table"

*Source: [ga26_1998 PDF p. 209](../markdown/ga26_1998.md#ga26-pdf-p209)*

---

## Submitted overture

“Amend BCO 58-4 to Clarify the Fencing of the Lord’s Table”

Whereas, the Scriptures teach that the Lord’s Supper is open to those who profess true faith in the Person and work of Jesus Christ alone for the forgiveness of their sins; and

Whereas, there is confusion among numerous churches concerning the meaning of BCO 58-4, resulting in a significant number of our churches practicing a “face value” reading and application of the last paragraph of BCO 58-4; and

Whereas, the historic interpretation of the language of BCO 58-4 goes beyond the requirements of both the Scriptures and the Westminster Standards concerning admission to the Lord’s Table; and

Whereas, there are certain rare and extraordinary cases where a Session would be prudent in fencing the Lord’s Table to admit profession believers who at the time of the celebration are not on the membership roll of an evangelical church. Therefore we recommend that the third paragraph of BCO 58-4 be revised as follows: **Current BCO 58-4**: Since, by our Lord’s appointment, this Sacrament sets forth the Communion of the Saints, the minister, at the discretion of the Session, before the observance begins, may either invite all those who profess the true religion, and are communicants in good standing in any evangelical church, to participate in the ordinance; or may invite those who have been approved by the Session, after having given indication of their desire to participate. It is proper also to give a special invitation to non-communicants to remain during the service. **Proposed Change**: Since, by our Lord’s appointment, this Sacrament sets forth the Communion of the Saints, the minister, at the discretion of the Session, before the observance begins, may either invite: (1) all those who profess the true religion, and are communicants in good standing in any evangelical church to participate in the ordinance; or may invite those (2) the communicant members of this church, and those communing members of other churches who have been approved by the Session, after having given indication of their desire to participate. In addition, the minister may invite those who profess the true religion and are baptized, but due to extraordinary circumstances acceptable to the Session are not currently on the roll of any evangelical church, who have been approved by the Session after having given indication of their desire to participate. It is also proper to give a special invitation to non-communicants to remain during the service.

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA26 — CCB advice on Overture 24 as submitted</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Overture 24 - from New Life Presbyterian Church &quot;Amend BCO 58-4 To Clarify the Fencing of the Lord&#x27;s Table&quot; Response: It is the advice of the Committee that Overture 24 is in conflict with the Constitution of the PCA, for example at least with WCF 25-3 and BCO 57, in that it calls for inviting to the Lord&#x27;s Table those who are not communicant members in good standing of the Visible Church. Furthermore, the 21st General Assembly, in response to a previous overture (see M21GA, p.141-143), came to the same conclusion, to wit, that the invitation to the Lord&#x27;s Table is to be extended only to those who are communicant members in good standing of some part of the Visible Church, citing the biblical evidence.
</blockquote>

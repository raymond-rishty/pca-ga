# GA11 O30 — Adopt RPCES Study Report on Beverage Use of Alcohol as Denominational Position

**Assembly:** 11th (1983)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of New River

*Source: [ga11_1983 p. 41](../markdown/ga11_1983.md#ga11-p41)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Scriptures do speak clearly regarding the issue of the use of alcoholic beverages, and

Whereas, the Word of God does teach Christian Liberty in Romans 14:21, I Cor. 8:13, and I Peter 2:16, and since this is the basic position adopted by previous Assembly actions, and

Whereas, there are many in our denomination who believe that the issue of the use of alcoholic beverages needs to be investigated further,

Therefore, be it resolved by the General Assembly to adopt 'the study committee report and resolutions on Beverage Use of Alcohol' of the previous RPCES 155th General Synod, May 20, 1977, pp.126-141, as the approved position of our denomination in this matter. Attest: R. NORMAN EVANS, STATED CLERK Answered in the negative. §11-50, II, 4, p. 116.

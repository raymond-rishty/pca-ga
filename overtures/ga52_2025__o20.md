# GA52 O20 — Transfer Lowndes County from Covenant to Mississippi Valley Presbytery

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** the Covenant Presbytery (to MNA) "Change the Boundaries of Covenant and Mississippi Valley  ·  **Disposition:** Adopted (final)

*Source: [ga52_2025 p. 1206](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p1206)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas the geographic center of Covenant Presbytery has shifted significantly north and west in recent years as evidenced by the frequency of stated meetings in the Memphis area and parts of Arkansas; and

Whereas Lowndes, the county in Mississippi where Main Street Presbyterian
Church (the only PCA church in the county) is located, is on the

1204

southernmost border of Covenant Presbytery and borders The Presbytery of the Mississippi Valley on its southern border; and

Whereas the Session of Main Street Presbyterian Church wants to have more
regular participation in the stated meetings of Presbytery; and

Whereas the Session of Main Street Presbyterian Church finds this
participation difficult due to the locations where many of the stated
meetings of Covenant Presbytery are held; and

Whereas locations of the stated meetings of The Presbytery of the Mississippi Valley are usually closer to Columbus, MS, and often significantly so, than the locations of the stated meetings of Covenant Presbytery; and

Whereas Covenant Presbytery has in the past acted in similar situations to allow the transfer of churches in Winona, MS; Ackerman, MS; Columbia, TN; Fayetteville, AR; Charleston, AR; and Stamps, AR, to Presbyteries adjacent to Covenant Presbytery, and to receive a PCA mission in Joplin, MO, into Covenant Presbytery from an adjacent Presbytery;

Therefore, be it resolved that Covenant Presbytery in concurrence with the
Session of Main Street Presbyterian Church, Columbus, MS and The
Presbytery of the Mississippi Valley, overtures the 2025 General
Assembly to transfer Lowndes County in Mississippi from the
geographic bounds of Covenant Presbytery to the geographic bounds
of The Presbytery of the Mississippi Valley.

Adopted by Covenant Presbytery at its stated meeting, February 4, 2025 Attested by TE Robert Browning, stated clerk

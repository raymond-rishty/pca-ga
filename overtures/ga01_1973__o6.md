# GA1 O6 — Appoint Committee to Add Scripture Proof Texts to the BCO

**Assembly:** 1st (1973)  ·  **Source:** Covenant Presbytery

*Source: [ga01_1973 PDF p. 21](../markdown/ga01_1973.md#ga1-pdf-p21)*

---

## Submitted overture

That a committee of scholars be appointed by the First General Assembly to study and to provide Scrip tural proof texts for each part of the denomination's Book of Church Order similar to the practice in the early years of the Presbyterian Church, U. S.

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA1 — Overture covered by Theological Committee action (minutes excerpt)</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Rev. Charles Dunahoo, Chairman of the Constitutional Documents Committee, presented the Committee&#x27;s report, which was adopted:<br>
<br>
1. That Overtures 3 (page 20) and 6 (page 21)have been covered in 1-79, which is to apply to the Book of Church Order as well as to the Confession and Catechisms.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt · ga01_1973 minutes, printed p. 64 (PDF p. 64)](../markdown/ga01_1973.md#ga1-pdf-p64)*

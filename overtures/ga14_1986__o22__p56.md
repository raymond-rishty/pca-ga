# GA14 O22 — Transfer Catawba County from Western Carolina to Central Carolina Presbytery

**Assembly:** 14th (1986)  ·  **Source:** Central Carolina Presbytery  ·  **Disposition:** Adopted (final)

*Source: [ga14_1986 p. 56](../markdown/ga14_1986.md#ga14-p56)*

---

The 23rd Stated meeting of Central Carolina Presbytery respectfully requests the 14th General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to approve the transfer of Catawba County North Carolina from the jurisdiction of the Western Carolina Presbytery, with the consent of both presbyteries. Catawba County is home for a mission work begun by Central Carolina Presbytery, the New Covenant Mission of Hickory NC. It is the opinion of the MNA Committee of this presbytery that the New Covenant Church belongs to the Asheville-Black Mountain marketing area. Also, its culture is so dissimilar to the Central Piedmont area of our presbytery that its interests would be better served in the Western Carolina Presbytery. So, with the consent of both presbyteries and the mission church, we make this request. Attest: Stephen O. Stout, Stated Clerk *Answered in the affirmative, 14-43, III, 18, p. 117.*

# GA47 O33 — Amend BCO 57-5 to Add a Trinitarian Vow for Church Membership

**Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Source:** Tennessee Valley Presbytery (to CCB, OC) “Amend BCO 57-5 to Add Vow Regarding Trinity"  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga47_2019 p. 672](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p672)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, there is but one only, living, and true God, who exists as three persons—God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit—of one substance, power, and eternity (WCF 2; WLC 9; WSC 6); and

670

Whereas, the doctrine of the Trinity is foundational, essential, and vital to the Christian faith; indeed, presbyteries have been instructed to give due and serious consideration to "Appendix W," which includes the affirmation: "the confession of the Trinity is the heartbeat of the Christian religion" (as adopted by the 45th General Assembly, Minutes, 29 and 624); and

Whereas, the visible church is made up of those who profess faith in the Lord Jesus Christ (BCO 2-1), and such profession of faith is a profession of the whole Christ, who is both truly God and truly man; and

Whereas, we live in a pluralistic age, where there is confusion over the essential being of God, illustrated by the fact that the vast majority of self-professing evangelicals in America affirm that “Jesus is the first and greatest being created by God" (Ligonier Ministries and LifeWay Research, “The State of Theology" 2018); and

Whereas, the doctrine of the Trinity has been and is currently under assault by the unbelieving world, by cults, by unitarians, and by various religions; and

Whereas, Mormons, Jehovah's Witnesses, and other non-Christian faiths could affirm our current membership vows as stated; and

Whereas, the organization of a particular church in the Presbyterian Church in America includes the pronouncement and declaration of its organization in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit (BCO 5-8); and

Whereas, when unbaptized persons apply for admission into the Church, they shall make a public profession of their faith, in the presence of the congregation, and thereupon be baptized in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit (BCO 56-6; 57-3), as shall the children of believing parents; and

Whereas, "every Christian Church, or union or association of particular churches, is entitled to declare the terms of admission into its communion and the qualifications of its ministers and members" (Preface to the BCO II.2); and

Whereas, at least three fellow NAPARC churches include a membership vow requiring affirmation of the Holy Trinity; and

Whereas, the current membership vows of the Presbyterian Church in America reference “God" (BCO 57-5), but do not require faith in God as He exists in three persons;

Therefore, be it resolved to amend the Book of Church Order 57-5 by adding the following question to the membership vows [as question 2, thereby renumbering questions 2–5 as questions 3–6]:

671

2. Do you believe in the one, true, and living God who exists in three persons—God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit—who are of the same substance, equal in power and glory?

So that the membership vows in Book of Church Order 57-5 would read:

1. Do you acknowledge yourselves to be sinners in the sight of God, justly deserving His displeasure, and without hope save in His sovereign mercy?

2. Do you believe in the one, true, and living God who exists in three persons—God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit—who are of the same substance, equal in power and glory? 2.3. Do you believe in the Lord Jesus Christ as the Son of God, and Savior of sinners, and do you receive and rest upon Him alone for salvation as He is offered in the Gospel? 3.4. Do you now resolve and promise, in humble reliance upon the grace of the Holy Spirit, that you will endeavor to live as becomes the followers of Christ? 4.5. Do you promise to support the Church in its worship and work to the best of your ability? 5.6. Do you submit yourselves to the government and discipline of the Church, and promise to study its purity and peace?

Adopted by the Tennessee Valley Presbytery at its stated meeting, April 13, 2019 Attested by TE Jacob A. Bennett, stated clerk

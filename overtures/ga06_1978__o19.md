# GA6 O19 — Amend BCO 15-2 to Remove Cap on Commissioners from a Single Congregation

**Assembly:** 6th (1978)  ·  **Source:** Covenant Presbytery. Covenant Presbytery overtures the Sixth General Assembly of the Presb

*Source: [ga06_1978 PDF p. 38](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p38)*

---

## Submitted overture

Covenant Presbytery overtures the Sixth General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to amend The Book of Church Order (chapter 15-2) by deleting the words “but not to ex ceed five commissioners from any one congregation” and ending the sentence with the word ‘thereof.” Adopted: January 10, 1978 Attest: T. R. Patete, Stated Clerk Answered in the negative, §6-111, III. 11; p. 110.

Adopted: January 10, 1978

*Source: [Excerpt — Sixth GA minutes, printed p. 36 (PDF p. 38), Overture 19; Presbytery adoption date](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p38)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The Permanent Subcommittee recommends a negative answer with substitute wording</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
17. That overture 19 from Covenant Presbytery concerning a proposed amendment to Paragraph 15-2 of the BCO be answered in the negative and that the following be substituted:<br>
&quot;Paragraph 15-2 of the BCO be amended by adding in the last phrase, between the words &#x27;five&#x27; and &#x27;commissioners&#x27; the words &#x27;ruling elder,&#x27; so that said last phrase shall read, &#x27;but not to exceed five ruling elder commissioners from any one congregation.&#x27;&quot;
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Sixth GA minutes, printed p. 195 (PDF p. 197), Appendix E, Recommendation 17; wording visually checked against the PDF](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p197)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The Judicial Business commissioners recommend a negative answer</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
11. That Overture 19 from Covenant Presbytery concerning a proposed amendment to Paragraph 15-2 of the BCO be answered in the negative. Grounds: The effect of the substitution by the Permanent Sub-Committee is accomplished by the proposed amendment to the Book of Church Order (cf. III. 1. of this Report).
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Sixth GA minutes, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 112), 6-111, III, Recommendation 11](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p112)*

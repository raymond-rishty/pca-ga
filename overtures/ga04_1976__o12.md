# GA4 O12 — Launch a PCA-Wide Capital Funds Campaign

**Assembly:** 4th (1976)  ·  **Source:** Central Georgia Presbytery

*Source: [ga04_1976 PDF p. 32](../markdown/ga04_1976.md#ga04-p32)*

---

## Submitted overture

Central Georgia Presbytery overtures the Fourth General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to immediately begin a Capital Funds Campaign to undergird the total work of the Presbyterian Church in America. Adopted by Central Georgia Presbytery July 10, 1976. Walter V. Worsham, Stated Clerk Central Georgia Presbytery Referred to the Permanent Committee on Administration for Study, paragraph 4-76.

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA4 commissioners refer Overture 12 for study and a later answer</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
7. That Overture 12 from the Presbytery of Central Georgia (p. 30), be referred to the Permanent Committee on Administration for study and answer to the 1977 General Assembly.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Fourth GA minutes, printed p. 82 (PDF p. 84), 4-76, item 7](../markdown/ga04_1976.md#ga04-p84)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA5 Administration reports the referral to its Stewardship subcommittee</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
II. Capital Funds Campaign: The matter of capital funds campaign, which was referred to the Permanent Committee on Administration for study and response to the 1977 General Assembly, has been committed to the Sub-committee on Stewardship, which is to report directly to the Assembly (4-76, Item I,9).
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Fifth GA minutes, printed p. 111 (PDF p. 113), Appendix A](../markdown/ga05_1977.md#ga05-p113)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA5 receives the Stewardship concurrence as information</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
5. That the concurrence of the Sub-Committee on Stewardship with the request of the Committee on Mission to the United States to enter a Capital Fund Campaign with a $5,000,000 goal over the next five years be received as information;
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Fifth GA minutes, printed p. 88 (PDF p. 90), 5-80, Recommendation 5](../markdown/ga05_1977.md#ga05-p90)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA5 commissioners recommend the five-year capital campaign</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Recommendation nine (9) (p. 159) was adopted unanimously by your Committee.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Fifth GA minutes, printed p. 99 (PDF p. 101), 5-100, Committee discussion](../markdown/ga05_1977.md#ga05-p101)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA5 approves the five-year, five-million-dollar campaign</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
9. That the General Assembly approve a five year, five million dollar capital funds campaign for Mission to the United States, to be used primarily for the purchase of land and the construction of new churches. This recommendation is made in light of the fact that a great number of our Presbyterian Church in America congregations do not have their own land or &quot;church building,&quot; and that number will increase each year as new congregations are begun throughout the United States;
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Fifth GA minutes, printed p. 100 (PDF p. 102), 5-100, Recommendation 9](../markdown/ga05_1977.md#ga05-p102)*

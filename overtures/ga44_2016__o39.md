# GA44 O39 — Amend BCO 38-1 and 42-2 to Allow Appeal in Cases Without Process

**Assembly:** 44th (2016)  ·  **Source:** Pacific Northwest Presbytery (to CCB, OC)  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga44_2016 p. 641](../markdown/ga44_2016.md#ga44-p641)*

---

## Submitted overture

(to CCB, OC)
“Amend BCO 38-1 and 42-2 to Allow an Appeal Instead of
a Complaint in a Case Without Process"

Be it resolved, that the 44th General Assembly amend BCO 38-1 and 42-2 as
follows (<del>strike-through</del> for deletion, underlining for additions):
38-1. When any person shall come forward and make his
offense known to the court, a full statement of the facts shall
be recorded and judgment rendered without process. In
handling a confession of guilt, it is essential that the person
intends to confess and permit the court to render judgment
without process. Statements made by him in the presence of
the court must not be taken as a basis of a judgment without
process except by his consent. In the event a confession is
intended, a full statement of the facts should be approved by
the accused, and by the court, before the court proceeds to a
judgment. The accused has the right of <del>eomplaint</del> appeal
against the judgment.
42-2. Only those who have submitted to a regular trial, or a
case without process (BCO 38-1), are entitled to an appeal.

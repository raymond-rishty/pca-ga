# GA52 O38 — Amend BCO 30-4 to Require New Process for Elevation of Indefinite Suspension to Excommunication

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** the Southeast Alabama Presbytery (to CCB, OC) “Amend BCO 30-4 to Require a New Case with P  ·  **Disposition:** Answered by reference

*Source: [ga52_2025 p. 1277](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p1277)*

---

## Submitted overture

Be it resolved that BCO 30-4 be amended as follows (underlining for additions):

30-4. Excommunication is the excision of an offender from the communion of the Church. This censure is to be inflicted only on account of gross crime or heresy and when the offender shows himself incorrigible and contumacious. Elevating indefinitesuspension from the Sacraments to excommunication shall require a new case of process establishing that the matter is a gross crime or heresy and that the offender has shown himself incorrigible and contumacious. The design of this censure is to operate on the offender as a means of reclaiming him, to deliver the church from the scandal of his offense, and to inspire all with fear by the example of his discipline.

So that BCO 30-4 as amended would read:

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA52 — CCB advice on Overture 38</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 38 is in conflict with the Constitution in that BCO 30-4 refers to the infliction of an initial censure of excommunication after the judgment of a court and is not intended to offer a definition of excommunication per se, therefore this overture is incompatible with those parts of the BCO which require excommunication for acts not necessarily categorized as “gross crime or heresy” such as BCO 32-6, 33-2, -3, and 34-4.<br>
<br>
CCB also notes that this overture is in conflict with Overture 32 Adopted 8-0-0
</blockquote>

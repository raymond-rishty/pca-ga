# GA22 O16 — Amend BCO 32-3b to Permit Waiving the Waiting Period

**Assembly:** 22nd (1994)  ·  **Source:** Eastern Canada Presbytery

*Source: [ga22_1994 PDF p. 244](../markdown/ga22_1994.md#ga22-pdf-p244)*

---

## Submitted overture

"Amend BCO 32-3b to Permit Waiving the Waiting Period" If the proposed amendments of BCO 32-2 & 32-3 are approved,

Whereas, attendance at meetings of Presbytery is at times expensive both in time and money,

Whereas, in some circumstances all parties may be prepared to deal with a charge presented to Presbytery, without delay Therefore, The Presbytery of Eastern Canada overtures the 22nd General Assembly to amend BCO 32-3 b, by adding after "citation" the words "(in exceptional circumstances and with the consent of all parties, the requirement for a ten day waiting period may be waived)".

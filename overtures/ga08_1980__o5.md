# GA8 O5 — Reconsider the site of the 1982 General Assembly

**Assembly:** 8th (1980)  ·  **Source:** Warrior Presbytery

*Source: [ga08_1980 PDF p. 32](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga08-p32)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Committee on Administration of the Presbyterian Church in America has decided against pursuing the possibility of a meeting of the General Assembly in Pittsburgh in 1982; and

Whereas, the specific reason for this decision was the potential embarrassment to the dignity and the integrity of the PCA's relationship to the Reformed Presbyterian Church of North America, a Church which shares with the PCA membership in the North American Presbyterian and Reformed Council and whose facilities the PCA would have been using at the very time that Church would have been meeting in Grand Rapids, Michigan, with the other Churches in NAP ARC; and

Whereas, both proper decorum and courtesy would suggest that the PCA should honor its commitment to practice fellowship with the Churches in NAPARC; and

Whereas, new developments have surfaced in the relationship enjoyed between the PCA and the Reformed Presbyterian Church, Evangelical Synod, the Orthodox Presbyterian Church and the Reformed Presbyterian Church of North America, as a result of which a concurrent meeting of general assemblies and General Synods would seem to be most useful at this time; and

Whereas, the travel distance does not seem to be a prohibitive consideration, it being farther from Jackson, Mississippi, to Fort Lauderdale, Florida, where the Assembly is scheduled to meet in 1981, than it is from Jackson to Grand Rapids; Now Therefore, Be It Resolved, that the Presbytery of Warrior does overture the General Assembly to reconsider its decision on the site of the 1982 General Assembly meeting, with a view of joining the other denominations in NAPARC in Grand Rapids. Attested: T.E. FredrickS. Carr Stated Clerk 15 January 1980 Answered in the affirmative. § 8-103, III, 2, p. 121.

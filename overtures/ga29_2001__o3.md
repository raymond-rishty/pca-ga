# GA29 O3 — Amend BCO 20-2 to Include Senior Pastor on Associate Search Committee

**Assembly:** 29th (2001)  ·  **Source:** Susquehanna Valley Presbytery (to CCB & B&O) "Amend BCO 20-2 to Include Pastor in Search C

*Source: [ga29_2001 p. 192](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p192)*

---

## Submitted overture

“Amend BCO 20-2 to Include Pastor in Search Committee for Assistant or Associate Pastor”

Whereas, the *Book of Church Order* does not address the matter of searching for an assistant or associate pastor, and

Whereas, for the peace and unity of the church it is vital that there be harmony among the pastoral staff of a church, and

Whereas, any new assistant or associate pastor must be agreeable to and compatible with the senior pastor,

Therefore be it resolved that the following be added to the BCO 20-2: “When a search committee for an associate pastor is formed by a congregation, the senior pastor shall automatically be a member of the committee. Before proposing a candidate to the congregation, the committee shall assure itself that the candidate is acceptable to and has the full support of the senior pastor. “In the case of a call to an assistant pastor, Session may act as the search committee or it may appoint church members as a search committee to bring a candidate for Session approval. In either case, the senior pastor shall be a member of the search committee.” Adopted by the Susquehanna Valley Presbytery on September 16, 2000. Attested by: D. F. Rundle, Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">3. CCB advice</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Overture #3 is in conflict with the other parts of the Constitution. Teaching Elders are not members of the local congregation and therefore cannot be a member of the congregation&#x27;s pulpit committee. The proposed mandate of the overture that they be so would interfere with the right of the congregation to determine those who will rule over them, a privilege which undergirds BCO 20. Having said that, the Committee does recognize the wisdom of consulting with the senior pastor in the selection of an Assistant or Associate Pastor.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt · ga29_2001 minutes, printed p. 139 (PDF p. 141), CCB report, Overture 3](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p141)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Committee of Commissioners recommends a negative answer</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
3. That Overture 3 from Susquehanna Valley Presbytery (&quot;Amend BCO 20-2 to Include Pastor in Search Committee for Assistant or Associate Pastor&quot;) be answered in the negative. Grounds: There are many ways for a church to involve a senior pastor in the search process. It is not wise to mandate a specific BCO procedure regarding senior pastor involvement in calling an assistant or an associate pastor.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt · ga29_2001 minutes, printed p. 190 (PDF p. 192), 29-44, III, Recommendation 3](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p192)*

# GA14 O23 — Dismiss the Ad-Interim Committee on the General Assembly

**Assembly:** 14th (1986)  ·  **Source:** Grace Presbytery  ·  **Disposition:** Adopted (final)

*Source: [ga14_1986 p. 46](../markdown/ga14_1986.md#ga14-p46)*

---

Whereas, it has been demonstrated within the brief history of the Presbyterian Church in America that amendment of our constitutional standards has been better effected when these changes have been instigated by the “minor” courts than when they have been generated from within the committees of the General Assembly;

Therefore, be it resolved that Grace Presbytery overture the Fourteenth General Assembly to dismiss the Ad-Interim Committee on the General Assembly with thanks. Adopted at the Forty-Ninth Stated Meeting of Grace Presbytery, January 14, 1986, meeting at the Mount Olive Presbyterian Church, Mount Olive, Mississippi. Attest: Vaughn E. Hathaway, Jr. Stated Clerk Answered in the negative, 14-85, III, 1, p. 187.

# GA8 O28 — Reaffirm the 1973 commitment to church unity

**Assembly:** 8th (1980)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of the Ascension

*Source: [ga08_1980 PDF p. 38](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga8-pdf-p38)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Presbyterian Church in America did, when she was first called into being as a separate Church in December, 1973, address a letter to all churches in which she affirmed, "As this new member of the family of churches of the Lord jesus Christ comes into being, we necessarily profess the Biblical doctrine of the unity of all who are in Christ. . . We greet all believers in an affirmation of the bond of Christian brotherhood. We invite into ecclesiastical fellowship all who maintain our principles of faith and order” ; and

Whereas, the Presbyterian Church in America is committed to the inerrancy of the Bible in the original autographs and thus to its infallible authority over us in all matters of faith and life; and

Whereas, the Presbyterian Church in America is committed to the Reformed Faith as set forth in the westminster confession and catechisms and to presbyterian government asset forth in the Book of Church Order; and

Whereas, as a young Church which is yet in forming, the Presbyterian Church in America would benefit from the wisdom and experience of others who hold a like faith and polity and who have preceded us in similar church developments; and

Whereas, in the Presbytery of the Ascension and many other presbyteries there is duplication of efforts and resources by those who share a common faith as a basis of unity along with many expressions of desire for such unity; and

Whereas, the Presbyterian Church in America recognizes that the Orthodox Presbyterian Church, the Reformed Presbyterian Church, Evangelical Synod, and the Reformed Presbyterian Church of North America all hold the same faith as the Presbyterian Church in America; Therefore, the Presbytery of the Ascension respectfully overtures the Eighth General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to invite the Orthodox Presbyterian Church, the Reformed Presbyterian Church, Evangelical Synod, and the Reformed Presbyterian Church of North America to join the Presbyterian Church in America under the terms of her Constitution. Adopted the twenty-sixth day of February, 1980, at Greensburg, Pa. Frank D. Moser. Stated Clerk Answered by letter of invitation. See § 8-43, IV, 5; p. 80ff.

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
5. That the Eighth General Assembly of the PCA reaffirm at this time that portion of its original letter from our First General Assembly to all Churches, which reads: “As this new member of the family of Churches of the Lord Jesus Christ comes into being, we necessarily profess the Biblical doctrine of the unity of all who are in Christ... We greet all believers in an affirmation of the bond of Christian brotherhood. We invite into ecclesiastical fellowship all who maintain our principles of faith and order.”
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Eighth GA minutes, printed p. 80 (PDF p. 82), 8-43, IV, Recommendation 5](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga8-pdf-p82)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Assembly adopts the substituted invitation text, 423–124</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
The Assembly resumed regular session and voted to adopt the amendment on the floor, by substituting the shortened form of the “Letter” for the longer form originally recommended by the Sub-committee on Interchurch Relations. The vote was 423 affirmative, 124 negative.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Eighth GA minutes, printed p. 82 (PDF p. 84), 8-43, IV, Recommendation 5 floor proceedings](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga8-pdf-p84)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Assembly adds the constitutional-process paragraph and adopts the amended letter, 525–38</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
It is to be understood that this invitation is an action of the Eighth General Assembly, and to receive any denomination which responds to this invitation it should be stated that for the Presbyterian Church in America to consummate any such union it would have to go through the constitutional process of approval by subsequent General Assemblies and three-fourths of our presbyteries. In this constitutional process each denomination must be dealt with separately. The amended substitute for Recommendation No. 5 of the Committee of Commissioners on Interchurch Relations was then adopted by vote of 525 affirmative, 38 negative.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Eighth GA minutes, printed p. 82 (PDF p. 84), 8-43, IV, amended Recommendation 5](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga8-pdf-p84)*

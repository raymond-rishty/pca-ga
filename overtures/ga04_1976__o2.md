# GA4 O2 — Study Coordinating All Committee Publications into One Monthly Publication

**Assembly:** 4th (1976)  ·  **Source:** Presbytery of Southern Florida

*Source: [ga04_1976 PDF p. 34](../markdown/ga04_1976.md#ga4-pdf-p34)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas the permanent Committees of the General Assembly are experiencing financial difficulties, and

Whereas our local congregations are being inundated with literature which includes duplicate letters to clerks and ministers, expensive pamphlets (Horizon), committee reports, missionaries’ letters, prayer needs from Mission to the World, Mission to the U.S., the publication from the Committee on Education, “Continuing” as well as other special announcements, pleas for support etc., and

Whereas it is impossible to include all these materials in Sunday Bulletins nor can such duplication of energy, resources, stationery, postage and handling justify the tremendous costs of their production.

Now therefore be it resolved that the Presbytery of Southern Florida petitions the Fourth General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America To appoint from this Assembly, two commissioners from each Presbytery represented to study the matter of having this Assembly, direct, coordinate all these publications from the permanent committees into one monthly publication which is to include missionary reports, educational endeavors, etc. And that this committee report as early as possible to the Assembly its recommendation for bringing into existence a co-ordinated monthly publication and how it could be financed through advertisements, and subscriptions, etc. Joe Warner, Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The Christian Education committee recommends a denominational magazine plan</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
16. Regarding a Church Magazine<br>
<br>
A. That the General Assembly adopt the following procedure in phasing the present “Continuing...” into a denominational magazine: Its design shall be as follows:<br>
<br>
1. News Content — the primary purpose of the magazine shall be to provide news of the denomination concerning the work of the major committees, the presbyteries and churches.<br>
<br>
2. Editorial Policy — the magazine shall contain no purely editorial material.<br>
<br>
3. Special Features — space shall be devoted regularly to the following features:<br>
<br>
a. Family Section: including a Bible study suitable for family devotions.<br>
<br>
 b. Discussion: pro and con on issues currently under study by any committee of the church.<br>
<br>
 c. Theological Section: devoted only to studies in areas already recognized by the denomination (e.g. the Westminster Standards, Book of Church Order, etc.).<br>
<br>
 d. Leadership Section: designed especially for training officers in the church.<br>
<br>
 e. Feature Section: containing articles on music, book reviews, specialized ministries, biographies.<br>
<br>
4. Advertising — directory-type advertising shall be included for approved Christian enterprises and for churches and individuals in the P.C.A.<br>
<br>
5. Size, Layout, Frequency — the magazine shall be published monthly on 6” x 9” stock and shall contain suitable art work and photographs.<br>
<br>
B. The Committee for Christian Education and Publications shall bring a full mock-up of the proposed magazine to the Fifth General Assembly.<br>
<br>
C. $10,000 shall be added to the Christian Education budget for the purpose of developing the mock-up and the Christian Education staff shall be permitted to cancel one or two issues of “Continuing...” in order to prepare the mock-up.<br>
<br>
D. Plans shall be presented to the Fifth General Assembly to begin publishing the magazine on January 1, 1978, contingent upon appropriate financial capability.<br>
<br>
E. An Assistant Coordinator for Publications shall be presented to the Fifth General Assembly for examination and approval.<br>
<br>
F. That the amount of $25,000 be added for the purpose of hiring appropriate editorial personnel.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Fourth GA minutes, printed pp. 85–86 (PDF pp. 87–88), 4-80, Report of the Committee on Christian Education and Publications, Recommendation 16](../markdown/ga04_1976.md#ga4-pdf-p87)*

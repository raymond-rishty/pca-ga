# GA19 O15 — Divide the Presbytery of the Ascension into Two Presbyteries

**Assembly:** 19th (1991)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 15 From the Presbytery of the Ascension

*Source: [ga19_1991 p. 118](../markdown/ga19_1991.md#ga19-p118)*

---

## Submitted overture

"Divide Ascension Presbytery" The Presbytery of Ascension overtures the 19th General Assembly to divide the Presbytery of the Ascension into two presbyteries. The boundary to be along the northern border of the following counties: in Ohio - Tuscarawas, Carroll, and Columbiana; and in Pennsylvania - Washington, Allegheny, Westmoreland, Indiana, Cambria, and Blair. This division is to be effective January 1, 1992. Adopted at the January 1991 Stated Meeting of the Presbytery of the Ascension, on January 12, 1991. Attested by: /s/ Frederick R. Niekirk Stated Clerk

# GA28 O11 — Authorize Formation of a Provisional Presbytery in Russia

**Assembly:** 28th (2000)  ·  **Source:** Mississippi Valley Presbytery (to MTW)

*Source: [ga28_2000 PDF p. 259](../markdown/ga28_2000.md#ga28-pdf-p259)*

---

## Submitted overture

From the Presbytery of Mississippi Valley “Authorize Formation of a Provisional Presbytery in Russia”

**Whereas**, TE Blake Purcell is a member of Mississippi Valley Presbytery, and

**Whereas**, Mississippi Valley Presbytery has authorized TE Blake Purcell to serve out of bounds as an evangelist in St. Petersburg, Russia, and

**Whereas**, Mississippi Valley Presbytery has entrusted to TE Blake Purcell (BCO 8-6) "the power to organize churches, and, until there is a Session in the church so organized, to instruct, examine, ordain, and install ruling elders and deacons therein, and to receive or dismiss members;" **Therefore, Be it Resolved** that Mississippi Valley Presbytery overture the 28th General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America, according to BCO 15-6, to exercise its "power to commit to a commission, consisting of not less than three elders, the task of forming a provisional Presbytery in a foreign country [Russia] where there exists no compatible indigenous presbyterian and reformed church. Such a commission shall have authority to act as the Presbytery in all matters pertaining to the establishment and ordering of a national church and shall report annually to the General Assembly. The commission shall be dissolved when there are at least three national teaching elders and three organized churches under its care, and these shall then constitute a separate national church." Adopted at the Winter Stated Meeting of The Presbytery of the Mississippi Valley on February 15, 2000. Attested by: /s/ Roger G. Collins, Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">MTW Permanent Committee — communicates its opinion on Overture 11 (minutes excerpt)</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
That the following statement be communicated to the Stated Clerk of the General Assembly and to the Stated Clerk of Mississippi Valley Presbytery as the opinion of CMTW regarding Overture 11 – from the Presbytery of Mississippi Valley (Docket page FM 53): While we rejoice in any establishment of Reformed and Covenantal churches around the world, the PCA has given the mandate to MTW, as recorded in the MTW Manual: The relationship of the Committee to the presbyteries and sessions of the denomination is defined by the duties assigned to the Committee by the General Assembly. Its role is to serve and offer coordinating facilities to these church courts. It seeks to aid these courts in the search for candidates for mission service overseas, in processing candidates, maintaining essential services for, and communication with, missionaries on the field, and in keeping the home church aware of and supportive of the ministries of such missionaries. Section 1.01 paragraph 2 The primary thrust will be in the familiar denominational pattern of developing a mission work in particular countries that is completely Presbyterian and Reformed in character, with all the missionaries on such fields being missionaries of the PCA, though some may be serving on a cooperative basis as having come from sister churches in the Reformed family of denominations. Section 1.01 paragraph 11. It is the opinion of CMTW that the Presbytery of Mississippi Valley in accordance with BCO 8-6, has the power to authorize the evangelist to ordain and install ruling elders and deacons in the congregation which he is planting in St. Petersburg; it is also the opinion of CMTW that it would be unwise for the General Assembly to establish a commission to establish a provisional Presbytery in Russia until such time as it is possible to coordinate the establishment of a Presbytery with the developing work of MTW in Russia. Heretofore, when BCO 15-6 has been implemented, it has been initiated and coordinated by MTW. CMTW believes this brings order and avoids confusion to young and emerging churches.
</blockquote>

# GA27 O26 — Change Presbytery Boundaries Between North Texas and Southwest Presbyteries

**Assembly:** 27th (1999)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 26 From North Texas Presbytery (to MNA)

*Source: [ga27_1999 p. 113](../markdown/ga27_1999.md#ga27-p113)*

---

“Change Boundaries Between North Texas and Southwest Presbyteries”

Whereas, a large portion of we stand northwest texas is currently out-of- bounds, and

Whereas, North Texas Presbytery (BTP) and the Southwest Church Planting Network have already planted a church in that area and are considering future work in the area, Be it Therefore Resolved, that the North Texas Presbytery respectfully overtures the 27th General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to move the current western north-south boundary of nt p west to coincide with the eastern north-south boundary of southwest Presbytery to include all currently out-of-bounds Texas counties within the bounds of nt p effective 1 July 1999. Adopted at the 5 8th' stated meeting of north texas presbytery on 24 April 1999. Attested by: /s/ T. John Mulkey, Stated Clerk 22 That Overture 3 from heartland presbytery requesting division of the current Heartland and Siouxlands Presbyteries into three presbyteries, effective January 1, 2000, be deferred until such time as Siouxlands Presbytery takes action on the request Adopted

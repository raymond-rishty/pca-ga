# GA52 O15 — Amend BCO 36-4 to Add Language for Administering Definite Suspension from Office

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** Mississippi Valley Presbytery (to CCB, OC)  ·  **Disposition:** Final approval reported by GA53 Stated Clerk (2026)

*Source: [ga52_2025 p. 1199](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p1199)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas the censure of definite suspension from office is “administered to an
accused who, upon conviction, satisfies the court as to his repentance
and makes such restitution as is appropriate" (BCO 30-1), and

Whereas the censure of definite suspension from office is “administered when
the credit of religion, the honor of Christ, and the good of the
delinquent demand it, even though the delinquent has given
satisfaction to the court” (BCO 30-3), and

Whereas BCO 36-4 gives no specific language by which the censure of definite suspension from office is to be administered and the offending brother is to be addressed, necessitating Courts to determine for themselves how to communicate the censure, and

Whereas BCO 37-1 currently contains language to declare to a brother when
he is “restored by the court at the end of the term of his suspension,"

Therefore, be it resolved that BCO 36-4 be amended by adding the following language (underlining for additions):

36-4.
Definite suspension from office should be administered
in the presence of the court alone or in open session of the court, as it
may deem best, and public announcement thereof shall be at the
court's discretion. When the court has resolved to pass this sentence,
the moderator shall address the offending brother to the following
purpose:

Whereas, you,
(here describe
the brother as a teaching elder, ruling elder, or deacon) are
convicted by sufficient proof (or are guilty by your own
confession) of the sin of
(here insert the
offense), and have satisfied the court as to your repentance
by the grace of God; yet for the credit of religion, the honor
of Christ, andyour
owngoodwe
the
Presbytery (or Church Session)
in the name and by the authority of the Lord Jesus Christ,
do now declare you suspended from the exercise of your
office, and all
thefunctionst
thereof, for
(here state the definite period of time
of the suspension).

To this shall be added such advice or admonition as may be judged necessary, and the whole shall be concluded with prayer to almighty God that He would follow this act of discipline with His blessing.

So that the amended section will read as follows:

Adopted by the Mississippi Valley Presbytery at its stated meeting, February 4, 2025. Attested by TE Chris Wright, stated clerk

# GA52 O44 — Transition byFaith Magazine to a Press-Release-Based Publication

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** the Pee Dee Presbytery (to AC) “Transition by Faith to a Press-Release-Based Publication"  ·  **Disposition:** Adopted (final)

*Source: [ga52_2025 p. 1295](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p1295)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas by Faith magazine was established as the official publication of the Presbyterian Church in America (PCA) to serve as a voice for the denomination; and

Whereas the PCA is committed to maintaining a clear distinction between denominational communications and editorial commentary, ensuring that official communications remain impartial and representative of the church as a whole; and

1293

Whereas the purpose of denominational communication should be to provide clear and accurate information regarding the actions, policies, and ministries of the PCA without editorializing or presenting particular viewpoints that may not reflect the consensus of the denomination (1 Corinthians 14:40); and

Whereas by Faith magazine has, at times, functioned as more than a source of official denominational news, engaging in commentary and editorial perspectives that may not always represent the collective voice of the General Assembly (BCO 14-1.5; WCF 31.3); and

Whereas many churches and Presbyteries would benefit from an official PCA publication that focuses on disseminating essential information, press releases, and updates from ministries and Presbyteries in a way that prioritizes clarity, transparency, and denominational unity (Ephesians 4:3); and

Whereas transitioning by Faith into a press-release-based platform would enhance trust in denominational communications by ensuring that its content remains neutral, factual, and reflective of the entire PCA rather than selective viewpoints; and

Whereas such transition and narrowing of focus would be accompanied by a decrease in expenditures for by Faith, which currently is ~$400,000 (M51GA, 2024, Appendix A, 139), assisting the Administrative Committee in meeting its budget; and

Whereas the reputation of Christ and His Church should not be compromised by unnecessary controversies or unbalanced representation in denominational communications (2 Corinthians 8:21);

Therefore, be it resolved that the 52nd General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America directs the Administrative Committee to transition by Faith magazine from its current editorial format to a press-releasebased publication, limited to the following types of content:

1. Official press releases from the Administrative Committee and other denominational agencies regarding actions taken by the

1294

General Assembly, significant initiatives, and denominational decisions.

2. Ministry updates from PCA churches, Presbyteries, and denominational agencies, highlighting the work of the church in missions, discipleship, and service.

3. Announcements and reports from the General Assembly and Presbyteries, ensuring transparency and accessibility of denominational proceedings.

Be it further resolved that the Administrative Committee develop and implement a plan to restructure by Faith within one year of the adoption of this overture, ensuring that the new format aligns with the PCA's commitment to truth, transparency, and denominational unity

Adopted by the Pee Dee Presbytery at its stated meeting, April 26, 2025 Attested by TE Mark Horne, Stated Clerk

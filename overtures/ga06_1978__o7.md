# GA6 O7 — Study the Relationship Between Dispensationalism and Covenant Theology

**Assembly:** 6th (1978)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of North Georgia The North Georgia Presbytery respectfully overtures the Ge

*Source: [ga06_1978 p. 32](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p32)*

---

## Submitted overture

The North Georgia Presbytery respectfully overtures the General Assembly to set up an Ad Interim Committee for the purpose of studying the relationship between dispensationalism and covenant theology. Adopted by North Georgia Presbytery at its stated meeting, January 21, 1978. Attest: Robert G. Valentine Stated Clerk Answered by reference to the Committee for Christian Education, §6-63, II. A. p. 80.

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">North Georgia Presbytery adopts Overture 7</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Adopted by North Georgia Presbytery at its stated meeting, January 21, 1978.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Sixth GA minutes, printed p. 30 (PDF p. 32), Overture 7; originating Presbytery adoption](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga6-p32)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Committee of Commissioners recommends a doctrinal statement and study</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
A. Re Overture 7. Your Committee recommends that the General Assembly affirm that Dispensationalism is basically incompatable with Covenant Theology. Your Committee also recommends that the Christian Education Committee of the General Assembly be instructed to prepare and make available for distribution a study documenting the errors of modern Dispensationalism in the light of our doctrinal standards.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Sixth GA minutes, printed p. 80 (PDF p. 82), 6-63, II, A](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga6-p82)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The Sixth General Assembly adopts the recommendation</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Adopted.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Sixth GA minutes, printed p. 80 (PDF p. 82), 6-63, II, A adoption marker](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga6-p82)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The study is carried forward for a report to GA8</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
To be reported back to the Eighth General Assembly, § 7-49, p. 115.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Seventh GA minutes, printed p. 41 (PDF p. 43), prior-Assembly business notation](../markdown/ga07_1979.md#ga7-p43)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Permanent Committee reports progress on the ordered study</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
The Sixth General Assembly referred two matters to the CE &amp; P Committee for study, namely, Dispensationalism (6-63, II, A, p. 80) and Theonomy (6-63, II, D, p. 81). The Committee has approved the content of a lengthy paper on Dispensationalism by the Rev. Grover Gunn, a PCA pastor. The Committee plans to make this paper available in the near future.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Seventh GA minutes, printed p. 193 (PDF p. 195), Appendix F, Permanent Committee report](../markdown/ga07_1979.md#ga7-p195)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Committee of Commissioners recommends a negative answer</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
26. That Overture 7 from the Seventh General Assembly (p. 45) be answered in the negative.<br>
<br>
Grounds: The Committee for Christian Education and Publications has already studied the matter and plans to publish a paper on Dispensationalism at a later date.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Eighth GA minutes, printed p. 74 (PDF p. 76), 8-29, III, Recommendation 26](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga8-p76)*

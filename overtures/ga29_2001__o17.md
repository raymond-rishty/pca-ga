<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Overtures Committee recommendation (minutes excerpt)</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
13. Answer Overture 17 from Central Carolina Presbytery (&quot;Add a New Chapter 44 to BCO and Appendix H&quot;) in the affirmative, with amendment, by appending the proposed language with suitable amendments and the flowchart to the BCO as Appendix H – “Suggested Procedures for Presbytery Judicial Commissions Acting as Appellate Courts.&quot; Grounds:<br>
<br>
1. The overture is intended to offer assistance in cases in which commissions act as appellate courts. To make this procedural advice available quickly, it was deemed wise to avoid the amendment procedure.<br>
<br>
2. We believe this extensive procedure best serves the Church by being placed in the Appendix, where it is non-binding.
</blockquote>

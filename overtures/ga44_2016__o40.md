# GA44 O40 — Amend BCO 43-1 to Clarify Timing of Higher Court Complaint Review

**Assembly:** 44th (2016)  ·  **Source:** Pacific Northwest Presbytery (to CCB, OC)  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga44_2016 p. 643](../markdown/ga44_2016.md#ga44-p643)*

---

## Submitted overture

“Amend BCO 43-1 to Clarify the Timing of Higher Court
Review of Complaints in Judicial Cases"

(to CCB, OC)

Be it resolved, that the 44th General Assembly amend BCO 43-1 as follows (<del>strike-through-for</del> deletions; underlining for additions):

43-1. A complaint is a written representation made against
some act or decision of a court of the Church. It is the right of
any communing member of the Church in good standing to
make complaint against any action of a court to whose
jurisdiction he is subject., <del>except that no complaint is</del>
<del>allowable in a judicial case in which an appeal is pending</del>.

During the process of a judicial case, a higher court shall not
consider a timely-filed complaint against an action or ruling of
the lower court until a judgment has been rendered, and:

there is an acquittal or a dismissal of all charges,

the deadline for filing an appeal has expired,

the accused has abandoned his appeal, or

an appeal has been adjudicated.

However, by a 2/3 vote the higher court may consider a pre-
judgment complaint, and it shall record a reason why the
circumstances warrant doing so.

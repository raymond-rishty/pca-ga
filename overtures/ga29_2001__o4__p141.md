# GA29 O4 — Amend BCO to Change Procedure for Dissolving Pastoral Relationships

**Assembly:** 29th (2001)  ·  **Source:** Eastern Carolina Presbytery: Change Procedure for Pastoral Relationship Dissolution It is  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga29_2001 p. 141](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p141)*

---

It is the opinion of the CCB that Overture #4 is not in conflict with the other parts of the Constitution. It is also the opinion of the CCB that the offered revision offers very little substantive change to our present standards when they are properly understood and carried out – except for the proposed provision that a Commission can never be used by a presbytery to dissolve the pastoral relationship which seems unnecessarily restrictive for perfunctory cases without objection from any party. *Adopted by CCB*

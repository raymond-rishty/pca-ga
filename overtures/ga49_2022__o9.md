# GA49 O9 — Amend BCO 34-1 to Establish Percentage Threshold for Original Jurisdiction Requests

**Assembly:** 49th (2022)  ·  **Source:** Calvary Presbytery (to CCB, OC) “Amend BCO 34-1 Establishing Percentage Threshold for Orig  ·  **Disposition:** Answered by reference to Overture 8

*Source: [ga49_2022 p. 1314](../markdown/ga49_2022.md#ga49-p1314)*

---

## Submitted overture

Be it resolved that BCO 34-1 be amended as follows (<del>strike-through</del> for deletions, underlining for new wording): BCO 34 - Special Rules Pertaining to Process Against a Minister (Teaching Elder) 34-1. Process against a minister shall be entered before the Presbytery of which he is a member. However, <del>if the Presbytery</del> <del>refuses to act-in</del> doctrinal cases or cases of public scandal, if <del>two other</del> 10% of Presbyteries request the General Assembly to assume original jurisdiction (to first receive and initially hear and determine), the General Assembly shall do so. So that BCO 34-1 reads:

1297

34-1. Process against a minister shall be entered before the Presbytery of which he is a member. However, in doctrinal cases or cases of public scandal, if 10% of Presbyteries request the General Assembly to assume original jurisdiction (to first receive and initially hear anddetermine), the General Assembly shall do so.

Rationale: BCO 34-1 as currently written has long existed within the PCA as a constitutional variant. Themeaning of the phrase "if the Presbytery refuses to act" has no clear definition and has made application of its usage ineffective.

This overture seeks to do two things simultaneously: first it clearly defines the terms of General Assembly original jurisdiction of a presbytery while secondly making it the process more difficult to do so than the current threshold of 2 presbyteries. The standard of request will increase from 2 presbyteries to 10% of presbyteries.

Adopted by Calvary Presbytery at its stated meeting, October 28, 2021 Attested by /s/ Melton L. Duncan, stated clerk

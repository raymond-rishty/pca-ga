# GA8 O10 — Adopt a denomination-wide petition form for new churches

**Assembly:** 8th (1980)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Eastern Carolina

*Source: [ga08_1980 p. 42](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga08-p42)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Book of Church Order, Paragraph 5 1.1 clearly states that a petition shall be received and - adopted by the presbytery of those persons seeking to be organized into a pc . A. congregation, and

Whereas, the Book of Church Order does not have a worded petition form for new congregations to use; Therefore, the Presbytery of Eastern Carolina respectfully overtures the Eighth General Assembly to adopt the following petition as the official petition form of the denomination to be used in organizing new congregations. Believing, that all Scripture is self-attesting and being Truth, requires the human mind whole-heartedly to subject itself in all its activities to the authority of Scripture complete as the Word of God. standing written in the sixty-six books of the Holy Bible, all therein being verbally inspired by Almighty God, and therefore without error; and Believing, that the Westminster Confession of Faith as adopted by the Presbyterian Church in America and the Larger and ShorterCatechisms set forth the system of doctrine taught in Scripture, and recognizing that subscription to this system of doctrine is required by all church officers, and therefore is to be learned, taught, and proclaimed for the edification and government of Christian people, for the propagation of the faith and for the evangelization of the world by the power of the Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ; and Accepting, the Book of Church Order of the Presbyterian Church in America as setting forth a reasonable and practical formulary for church organization; therefore Be It Resolved: 1 That we, the undersigned, do covenant together to form an association to be known as ---------------------------Presbyterian Church, o f________________ and 2. That this association shall have as its purpose to perpetuate the Gospel of our Lord Jesus as it is proclaimed in the Scriptures and declared in the Westminster standards; and 3. That we met in -------------------------o n --------------------------and therefore present this petition to --------------------------Presbytery for organization as required in Chapter 5 of the Book of Church Order. Adopted by Eastern Carolina Presbytery on Jan. 26. 1980. Attest: C. Gene Parks Stated Clerk Answered in the negative. § 8-50, III, 5; p. 86.

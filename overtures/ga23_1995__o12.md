<h3 class="overture-action-heading">CCB answers Overture 12 on recording commission actions</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
It is the advice of the CCB that neither BCO 15-1, nor RAO 14-10, requires minutes of a commission of presbytery or a session to be included in the minutes of the appointing court. It is required that the appointing court record the action of its commission.
</blockquote>

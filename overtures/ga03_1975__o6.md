# GA3 O6 — Amend BCO 14-7 to Require Presbytery Approval of Changes to Minister's Call

**Assembly:** 3rd (1975)  ·  **Source:** Warrior Presbytery

*Source: [ga03_1975 PDF p. 36](../markdown/ga03_1975.md#ga03-p36)*

---

## Submitted overture

Warrior Presbytery respectfully overtures the Third General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to add to the Book of Church Order I-14-7, line 23: "To approve or disapprove any change in a minister's terms of call enacted by the congregation before they become binding." Answered in the negative, paragraph 3-17.

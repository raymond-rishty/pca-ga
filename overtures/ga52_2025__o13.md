# GA52 O13 — Amend BCO 25-1 to Limit Voting to Members in Good Standing

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** the Great Lakes Presbytery (to CCB, OC) “Amend BCO 25-1 to Specify Only Members in Good St  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga52_2025 p. 1197](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-p1197)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas BCO 24-3 states that “All communing members in good and regular standing, but no others, are entitled to vote."

Whereas BCO 25-1 states that all communing members are entitled to vote and does not qualify that only members in good and regular standing may vote, it is inconsistent with the BCO.

Be it resolved that BCO 25-1 be amended to specify which communing members are entitled to vote (underlining for additions; <del>strikethrough</del> for deletions):

25-1. The congregation consists of all the communing members of a particular church, and <del>they</del> only members in good and regular standing are entitled to vote.

So that the amended paragraph would read:

25-1. The congregation consists of all the communing members of a particular church, and only members in good and regular standing are entitled to vote.

Adopted by the Great Lakes Presbytery at its stated meeting, January 11, 2025. Attested by Ryan Potter, stated clerk

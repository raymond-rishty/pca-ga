# GA18 O29 — Direct PCA Messenger to Support PCA Positions

**Assembly:** 18th (1990)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 29 From Central Carolina Presbytery (to CE)

*Source: [ga18_1990 PDF p. 102](../markdown/ga18_1990.md#ga18-pdf-p102)*

---

## Submitted overture

16. OVERTURE 29 From Central Carolina Presbytery (to CE) "Ask Messenger to Support PCA Positions"

Whereas, the Messenger is the official publication of the General Assembly of the PCA, instructing its membership on positions of the PCA; and

Whereas, the editor of the PCA Messenger wrote that the report of the special committee to study abortion "did not attempt to define when life began" (January, 1990, p.3), although the 6th and 8th General Assemblies adopted the following statement, "That because Scripture clearly affirms the sanctity of human life and condemns its arbitrary destruction, we affirm that the intentional killing of an unborn child between conception and birth, for any reason at any time, is clearly a violation of the Sixth Commandment"; Therefore, the Session of the Prosperity Presbyterian Church of Charlotte, NC, humbly requests the Central Carolina Presbytery at its regular stated meeting on April 28, 1990, to direct the Committee on Christian Education and Publication to insure that the staff and articles of the PCA Messenger will be supportive of the biblical, confessional and ecclesiastical positions of the General Assembly of the PCA.

Adopted by the Central Carolina Presbytery at the Stated Meeting on April 28, 1990 Attested by: /s/ Stephen O. Stout, Stated Clerk

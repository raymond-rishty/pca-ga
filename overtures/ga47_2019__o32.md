## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA47 CCB finds the related BCO amendments in conflict with RAO (excerpt)</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
In the opinion of the CCB, Overtures 8, 14, 19, 21, and 32 are in conflict with RAO 5-2, as the Overtures establish additional requirements for the Chairman. It should also be noted that RAO 4- 21.c.6 does not explicitly mention non-ordained members. Additionally, RAO 4-5 would need to be changed editorially to properly reference the newly numbered sections in BCO 14-1 upon passage and adoption of the Overtures. Adopted by the CCB
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt · 47th GA minutes, printed p. 399 (PDF p. 401), CCB report, II.G](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p401)*

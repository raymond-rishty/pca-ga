# GA6 O17 — Clarify Whether the Lord's Supper May Be Administered Outside Corporate Worship

**Assembly:** 6th (1978)  ·  **Source:** Grace Presbytery

*Source: [ga06_1978 PDF p. 38](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p38)*

---

## Submitted overture

In the light of the fact that the Confession of Faith states with regard to the Lord’s Supper that the elements are not to be administered to any who are not then present in the congregation; And in view of the practice of some congregations to administer the Lord’s Supper to in dividuals who are ill or elderly or infirm;

Therefore be it resolved that Grace Presbytery hereby requests the General Assembly to in dicate whether or not the Lord’s Supper may be administered to any who are not in attendance at worship services, and if so, under what conditions this may be done. Adopted at the Stated Meeting of Grace Presbytery, held at Faith Presbyterian Church, Brookhaven, Mississippi, April 14, 1978. Attested by: Rev. Norman A. Bagby, Jr. Not answered, §6-111, III. 10; p. 110.

Adopted at the Stated Meeting of Grace Presbytery, held at Faith Presbyterian Church, Brookhaven, Mississippi, April 14, 1978.

*Source: [Excerpt — Sixth GA minutes, printed p. 36 (PDF p. 38), Overture 17; Presbytery adoption](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p38)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The Permanent Subcommittee recommends a conditional answer</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
16. Recommendation No. 16. That overture 17 from Grace Presbytery be answered as follows:<br>
&quot;The Sacrament of the Lord’s Supper may be administered to those who are ill, elderly or infirm, provided that the session authorizes an abbreviated service of worship to convene at a specified time and place, following public announcement, and with the Teaching Elder and at least one Ruling Elder in attendance.&quot;
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Sixth GA minutes, printed p. 195 (PDF p. 197), Appendix E, Recommendation 16; wording verified against the PDF](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p197)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The Judicial Business commissioners move a different conditional answer</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
10. That Overture No. 17 from Grace Presbytery be answered as follows: &quot;The Sacrament of the Lord&#x27;s Supper may be administered to those who are ill, elderly or infirm, provided a service of worship be convened following public announcement, with a Teaching Elder and at least one Ruling Elder in attendance.&quot;
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Sixth GA minutes, printed p. 110 (PDF p. 112), 6-111, III, Recommendation 10](../markdown/ga06_1978.md#ga06-p112)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The Assembly rejects the motion and leaves Overture 17 unanswered</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
This motion was lost, and thus Overture 17 was not answered by the Assembly.
</blockquote>

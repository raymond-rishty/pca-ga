# GA39 O6 — Transfer Alexander and Catawba Counties to the New Presbytery

**Assembly:** 39th (2011)  ·  **Source:** Western Carolina Presbytery (to MNA) “Concur with Central Carolina Presbytery Request to F

*Source: [ga39_2011 p. 621](../markdown/ga39_2011.md#ga39-p621)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, Central Carolina Presbytery has overtured the 39th General Assembly to form a new presbytery that would include Alexander and Catawba counties, which counties are currently within the boundaries of Western Carolina Presbytery; and

Whereas, Western Carolina Presbytery concurs with Central Carolina Presbytery in this regard;

Therefore, be it resolved that Western Carolina Presbytery hereby overtures the 39th General Assembly to include Alexander and Catawba counties within the boundaries of such a new presbytery, effective July 1, 2011.

Adopted by the Western Carolina Presbytery at its stated meeting, February 26, 2011 Attested by /s/ TE Skip Gillikin, stated clerk

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA32 — CCB says Overture 5 is not properly before it (minutes excerpt)</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Overture 5 from Westminster Presbytery: &quot;Revise Makeup of Ridge Haven Board of Trustees&quot; In the opinion of the CCB, Overture 5 is not properly before the CCB. Overture 5 is, in effect, asking for a proposal to change the governing structure for Ridge Haven. At a minimum this would include changes in the &quot;RAO&quot; and &quot;Corporate Bylaws&quot; of the PCA. Apart from specific language proposed to the General Assembly to effect these changes, CCB can provide no further advice on this matter. Adopted by CCB
</blockquote>

# GA14 O43 — Amend BCO 21-4 to Clarify Extraordinary Reception of Ministers from Other Denominations

**Assembly:** 14th (1986)  ·  **Source:** Western Carolina Presbytery

*Source: [ga14_1986 PDF p. 55](../markdown/ga14_1986.md#ga14-pdf-p55)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, 21-4 of our Book of Church Order makes provision for Presbytery to omit parts of trial for Ordination in extraordinary cases: and,

Whereas, Ordained ministers may be received by Presbytery from other denominations (13-6) following the prescribed examination; and,

Whereas, the reception of ordained ministers from other denominations is carried out in accordance with 21-4 of the Book of Church Order containing the reference to extraordinary cases; Therefore, We, the Western Carolina Presbytery, overture the General Assembly to change the Book of Church Order Chapter 21-4 by adding a clarifying sentence, to read “Ordained ministers from other denominations being considered by presbyteries for reception, may come under the extraordinary provisions.” Adopted by Western Carolina Presbytery on April 12, 1986. Attest: Bill Laxton, Stated Clerk Referred to Judicial Business Committee, 14-52, 58, p. 130.

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA15 commissioners recommend a BCO amendment</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
3. That Overture 43 to the 14th GA from Western Carolina Presbytery (15-3, p. 66) be answered by amending the BCO as follows: After the first sentence in BCO 21-4, insert the following sentence: ‘No Presbytery shall omit any of these educational requirements except in extraordinary cases, and then only with a 4/5 approval of the Presbytery. Whenever a Presbytery shall omit any of these educational requirements, it shall always make a record of the reasons for such omissions and the parts omitted’. The next sentence will begin a new paragraph, and ‘the intern’ shall be substituted for ‘He’. Grounds: The Committee concurs with the Overture that the issue has merit and needs to be remedied.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt - GA15 1987, printed p. 169 (PDF p. 171)](../markdown/ga15_1987.md#ga15-pdf-p171)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA15 refers the recommendation and substitute to Judicial Business</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
A substitute was moved and seconded, to add ‘but ordained ministers from other denominations being considered by presbyteries for reception may come under the extraordinary provisions concerning parts of trial for ordination’ (see BCO 21-4). The substitute and Recommendation 3 were then on motion referred to the Committee on Judicial Business for report to the 16th General Assembly.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt - GA15 1987, printed p. 169 (PDF p. 171)](../markdown/ga15_1987.md#ga15-pdf-p171)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Judicial Business recommends revised BCO language</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
18. That the BCO be amended as follows: (1) BCO 21-4: insert after the first sentence a requirement for a 3/4 Presbytery approval before omitting educational requirements, and require a record of reasons and omitted parts; (2) place the next sentence in a new paragraph, replacing ‘He’ with ‘the intern’; (3) add a paragraph limiting extraordinary clauses to extraordinary church circumstances or proven extraordinary gifts and urging diligence in minister reception and ordination; and (4) replace the last sentence of BCO 13-6 with: ‘Ordained ministers from other denominations being considered by presbyteries for reception may come under the extraordinary provisions set forth in BCO 21-4.’
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt - GA16 1988, printed p. 175 (PDF p. 177)](../markdown/ga16_1988.md#ga16-pdf-p177)*

# GA26 O1 — Amend BCO 13-5 to Clarify Presbytery Membership for Minister Without Call Who Needs to Relocate

**Assembly:** 26th (1998)  ·  **Source:** Potomac Presbytery "Amend BCO 13-5 to Clarify Membership of One Honorably Retired" [Should

*Source: [ga26_1998 PDF p. 200](../markdown/ga26_1998.md#ga26-pdf-p200)*

---

## Submitted overture

“Amend BCO 13-5 to Clarify Membership of One Honorably Retired” [Should be entitled “to clarify membership of a teaching elder without call Who Needs to Relocate”]

Whereas, the requirement of BCO 13-2, “A minister shall be required to hold his membership in the Presbytery within whose geographical bounds he resides...” appears in some cases to be in tension with the direction offered in BCO 13-5, “Ordinarily, only a minister who receives a call to a definite ecclesiastical work within the bound sofa particular presbytery may be received as a member of that Presbytery except in cases where the minister is already honorably retired, or in those cases deemed necessary by the Presbytery ”; Therefore, Potomac Presbytery overtures the Twenty-Sixth General Assembly to amend the Book of Church Order 13-5 by adding a new sentence after the words ‘review of the General Assembly.’, as follows: 'In such cases deemed necessary, which may include the case of a minister without call whose circumstances appear to require relocation within the bounds of that Presbytery, the time allotment of BCO 13-2 shall be counted from the day the minister was first continued on the roll without call in any Presbytery. ’ So that the amended paragraph reads, 13-5 ‘Ordinarily, only a minister who receives a call to a definite ecclesiastical work within the bounds of a particular Presbytery may be received as a member of that Presbytery except in cases where the minister is already honorably retired, or in those cases deemed necessary by the Presbytery, subject to the review of the General Assembly. In such cases deemed necessary, which may include the case of a minister without call whose circumstances appear to require relocation within the bounds of that Presbytery, the time allotment of BCO 13-2 shall be counted from the day the minister was first continued on the roll without call in any Presbytery’’ [Note: material in italics is the proposed new language in the BCO.]

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA26 — CCB advice on Overture 1 as submitted</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Overture 1 - from Potomac Presbytery &quot;Amend BCO 13-5 To Clarify Membership of One Honorably Retired&quot; Response: It is the advice of the Committee that the particular language and syntax of Overture 1 is problematic. Although it may not have been the intention of the makers of the Overture to create a third &quot;exception&quot; category of men to be received into a Presbytery, the precise language does, in fact, broaden BCO 13-5 to permit three exception categories instead of two, i.e.:<br>
- **A.** ministers who are already honorably retired,<br>
<br>
- **B.** cases deemed necessary by the Presbytery, and<br>
<br>
- **C.** ministers who are without call and who wish to relocate to the bounds of another Presbytery. The creation of the third &quot;exception&quot; category of men to be received into a presbytery is in tension with our present Constitution in that it would create the possibility that a minister &quot;without call&quot; may threaten his presbytery&#x27;s constitutional authority (BCO 13-2, last sentence) to divest him without censure (BCO 34-10) after a prolonged period.<br>
If this new language were inserted into BCO 13-5, a new presbytery would be required to examine a man for reception into the presbytery without any consideration of whether or not this man has a call to any ministry under the new presbytery&#x27;s oversight. If the man be theologically qualified, the new presbytery may have no option but to receive him. As long as the man moves every three years, the authority and jurisdiction of his presbytery (old or new) to divest him without censure, even against his will (BCO 34-10) can be effectively threatened. Finally, the creation of the third &quot;exception&quot; category creates syntactical nonsense, i.e. only a man with a call will be received unless he is without call! It is therefore the advice of the Committee that Overture 1 be remanded to Potomac Presbytery so that the language and the syntax might be corrected and perfected so as not to threaten the BCO 34-10 authority of presbytery.
</blockquote>

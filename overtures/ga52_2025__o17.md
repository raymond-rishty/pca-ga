# GA52 O17 — Transfer Lowndes County from Covenant to Mississippi Valley Presbytery

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** Mississippi Valley Presbytery (to MNA) "Change the Boundaries of Covenant and Mississippi

*Source: [ga52_2025 PDF p. 1203](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-pdf-p1203)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas the geographic center of Covenant Presbytery has shifted significantly north and west in recent years as evidenced by the frequency of stated meetings in the Memphis area and parts of Arkansas; and

Whereas Lowndes, the county in Mississippi where Main Street Presbyterian
Church (the only PCA church in the county) is located, is on the
southernmost border of Covenant Presbytery and borders The
Presbytery of the Mississippi Valley on its southern border; and

Whereas the Session of Main Street Presbyterian Church wants to have more
regular participation in the stated meetings of Presbytery; and

Whereas the Session of Main Street Presbyterian Church finds this
participation difficult due to the locations where many of the stated
meetings of Covenant Presbytery are held; and

Whereas the locations of the stated meetings of The Presbytery of the Mississippi Valley are usually closer to Columbus, MS, and often significantly so, than the locations of the stated meetings of Covenant Presbytery; and

Whereas The Presbytery of the Mississippi Valley has in the past acted in similar situations to allow the transfer of churches in Winona, MS; Ackerman, MS; Delhi, LA and Rustin, LA, into the Presbytery

Therefore, be it resolved that the Presbytery of the Mississippi Valley overtures the 2025 General Assembly to transfer Lowndes County in Mississippi from the geographic bounds of Covenant Presbytery to the geographic bounds of The Presbytery of the Mississippi Valley.

Adopted by the Mississippi Valley Presbytery at its stated meeting, February 4, 2025. Attested by TE Chris Wright, stated clerk

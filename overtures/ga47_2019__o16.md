# GA47 O16 — Amend RAO to Provide for a Moderator-Elect and Vice-Moderator

**Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Source:** Georgia Foothills Presbytery (to CCB, OC) “Amend the RAO to Provide for a Moderator-Elect  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga47_2019 p. 629](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p629)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the role of Moderator of the General Assembly is a complicated responsibility. It will be helpful for a man to have a year's notice in order to prepare for his duties, and

Whereas, some PCA presbyteries have a Moderator-In-Nomination rule in their Standing Rules, and

Whereas, other Presbyterian denominations, such as the Associate Reformed Presbyterian Church and the Evangelical Presbyterian Church elect a Moderator-Elect or Vice-Moderator, and

Whereas, the Assembly subsequent to the election of a Moderator-Elect or Vice-Moderator still would have the option of electing another man as Moderator, and

Whereas, since we use electronic voting rather than voting cards or paper ballots, it is no longer necessary to specify that paper ballots be used, and

Whereas, it is customary that the Moderator yield the chair at times. The revision to RAO 2-1 specifies who the Moderator pro tempore may be, and

Whereas, under Robert's Rules of Order the term “privileges of the floor" only means that one is allowed to be present during a meeting and does not mean that one has the right to speak (RONR [11th ed.] p. 29, footnote). The original purpose of RAO 2-3 was to give a former Moderator of the General Assembly the right to participate in debate. One must be a commissioner in order to vote.

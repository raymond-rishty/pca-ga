# GA2 O3 — Affirm Members' Right to Hold Membership in Secret Societies

**Assembly:** 2nd (1974)  ·  **Source:** Rev. Eurie Hayes Smith, III et al

*Source: [ga02_1974 PDF p. 25](../markdown/ga02_1974.md#ga02-p25)*

---

## Submitted overture

FATHERS AND BRETHREN: To the Reverend General Assembly, First Presbyterian Church, Macon, Georgia

Whereas certain branches of the Christian Church have limited or implied a limit to the free exercise of the conscience of its office bearers and membership by restricting them from secret associations and fraternities such as the Masonic order.

Now therefore, we respectfully overture the Second General Assembly of the National Presbyterian Church to affirm the right of its office bearers and constituency to hold such membership in accordance with the Westminster Confession of Faith chapters 20:II, and 22:I, II, VIIa. Rev. Eurie Hayes Smith III Rev. Edwin P. Elliott, Jr. Junius W. Brown H. H. Stillions

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">The commissioners recommend that Overture 3 be answered as outside GA jurisdiction</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
2. That Overture 3, p. 23, requesting the General Assembly to affirm the right of office bearers and constituents to hold membership in secret associations and fraternities, be answered by declaring that inasmuch as membership in the Presbyterian Church in America is under the jurisdiction of Sessions and Presbyteries, this matter does not properly come under the jurisdiction of the General Assembly. (The Committee vote was 15 to 1.)
</blockquote>

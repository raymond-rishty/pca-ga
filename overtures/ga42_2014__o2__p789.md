# GA42 O2 — Reaffirm the PCA's Position on Homosexuality and Same-sex Marriage

**Assembly:** 42nd (2014)  ·  **Source:** Westminster Presbytery (to OC) “Concerning Same-sex Marriage”

*Source: [ga42_2014 p. 789](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p789)*

---

## Submitted overture

A reaffirmation of the Presbyterian Church in America's position on Homosexuality adopted at the 5th General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America, 1977, 5-49, 4, p. 67 and 8, p. 68 (http://www.pcahistory.org/ pca/2-398.html)

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA42 — Westminster Presbytery adopts Overture 2</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Approved by Westminster Presbytery at its stated meeting, July 13, 2013
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt, 42nd GA minutes, printed p. 788 (PDF p. 790), Overture 2 originating presbytery action](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p790)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA42 — Overtures Committee recommendation 2</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
2. That Overture 2 (p. 787) be answered in the negative. Out of Order GROUNDS: The Overture seeks to re-affirm the position of the Presbyterian Church in America, and as such is not necessary and possibly out of order according to RRO 5.10.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt, 42nd GA minutes, printed p. 59 (PDF p. 61), Overtures Committee report, item 2](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p61)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">GA42 — Assembly disposition of Overtures Committee recommendation 2</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
On a point of order by TE David Coffin, the Moderator ruled that the point of order was well taken and therefore Recommendations 2 and 5 were out of order, as requesting reaffirmations of previously adopted positions (RONR [11th ed.], p. 104, ll. 24-31). The ruling was challenged, and the Moderator&#x27;s ruling was sustained.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt, 42nd GA minutes, printed p. 55 (PDF p. 57), 42-35, Overtures Committee report](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p57)*

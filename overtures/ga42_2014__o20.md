# GA42 O20 — Commend Overture 3 Amending BCO 15-5 a. and b.

**Assembly:** 42nd (2014)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 20 from Nashville Presbytery (to CCB, OC)  ·  **Disposition:** Answered by reference

*Source: [ga42_2014 p. 825](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p825)*

---

"Commend Overture 3 Regarding Amending _BCO_ 15-5 a. and b”

**Whereas** Grace Presbytery has sent an overture to the 42 [nd] General Assembly calling on the General Assembly to revise _BCO_ 15-5. a and b;

**Therefore be it resolved** that Nashville Presbytery urges the 42 [nd] General Assembly to answer in the affirmative the overture from Grace Presbytery [Overture 3] on revising _BCO_ 15-5. a and b _Adopted by Nashville Presbytery at its stated meeting, February 11, 2014_ _Attested by /s/ TE Matthew Bradley, Stated Clerk_

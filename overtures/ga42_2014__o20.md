# GA42 O20 — Affirm Overture 3 from Grace Presbytery to Amend BCO 15-5.a and b

**Assembly:** 42nd (2014)  ·  **Source:** Nashville Presbytery (to CCB, OC) "Commend Overture 3 Regarding Amending BCO 15-5 a. and b

*Source: [ga42_2014 p. 825](../markdown/ga42_2014.md#ga42-p825)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas Grace Presbytery has sent an overture to the 42nd General Assembly calling on the General Assembly to revise BCO 15-5. a and b;

Therefore be it resolved that Nashville Presbytery urges the 42nd General Assembly to answer in the affirmative the overture from Grace Presbytery [Overture 3] on revising BCO 15-5. a and b Adopted by Nashville Presbytery at its stated meeting, February 11, 2014 Attested by /s/ TE Matthew Bradley, Stated Clerk

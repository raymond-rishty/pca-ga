# GA47 O48 — Make a Stronger Statement Regarding Abortion

**Assembly:** 47th (2019)  ·  **Source:** the Session of Christ the King (to OC) Presbyterian Church, Norman, Oklahoma “Make a Stron  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga47_2019 p. 714](../markdown/ga47_2019.md#ga47-p714)*

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Word of God indicates that all human beings are created in the image of God (Genesis 1:26, 27) and consequently have great worth in God's sight, and

Whereas, God's Word also indicates that human life is therefore of such value that to destroy that life for any reason other than that which God has prescribed, which is murder, is of such seriousness as to merit capital punishment (Genesis 9:5, 6), and

Whereas, God has clearly indicated in His Word that murder is a sin against Him, according to the moral law applicable to all times and

712

circumstances, as codified in the tablets of stone known as the Ten
Commandments (Exodus 20:13; Deuteronomy 5:17), and

Whereas, the Supreme Court decision known as Roe v. Wade, which struck
down laws prohibiting the practice of abortion of unborn children and
legalized abortion in every state in the United States of America, was
predicated upon a fraudulent case involving false testimony with respect
to the circumstances surrounding it, which circumstances thus wielded
powerfully emotional and insidiously persuasive, yet mythical, import,
and

Whereas, the fraudulent nature of the entire case was irrefutably verified by
testimony subsequent to the ruling by the plaintiff in the case, Norma
McCorvey, the so-called Jane Roe, such that crucial alleged facts
testified to in court regarding her need for an abortion and injuries
suffered by her due to her inability to obtain one were untrue (as
documented in the books I Am Roe: My Life, Roe V. Wade, and Freedom
of Choice and Won by Love and the documentary film “I Was Wrong"),
and

Whereas, Ms. McCorvey spent much of the rest of her life seeking to correct
the record and advocate the pro-life cause, and

Whereas, in the course of such advocacy, Ms. McCorvey testified to the
manipulation and use of her by ideological radicals intent on promoting
the legalization of abortion by any means necessary, and

Whereas, her obviously relevant and groundbreaking testimony has been
largely ignored by both the mainstream media and our culture at large,
such that Roe v. Wade, as legal opinion, is unjustifiably and ignorantly
revered by many, and

Whereas, the practice of abortion and its promotion have long been linked
to the secular humanistic ideology undergirding the practice of eugenics,
which came to expression in the death camps of Nazi Germany, and
which was advocated in the writings of the founder (Margaret Sanger,
who publicly advocated the forced sterilization, abortion, and
elimination of whole races and classes of people) of the most prolific
dispenser of abortion today, Planned Parenthood, and

Whereas, recent video documentary evidence (made public by the Center
for Medical Progress in 2015, and validated by the investigation and
ruling of the United States Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals in January,

2019. has demonstrated that Planned Parenthood has sold body parts obtained through abortion to those wishing to harvest human organs for a variety of purposes, in violation of law, and

Whereas, certain lawmakers within the United States government have persisted and now again succeeded in forcing American taxpayers, 713

against their desires and beliefs, in multiple ways, to fund and undergird these wicked practices through government subsidization of Planned Parenthood and other less prominent abortion providers, and

Whereas, medical science has advanced to the point of demonstrating, beyond a shadow of a doubt, that the fetus within a mother's womb is a living organism, distinct from the mother's body (possessing distinct DNA, etc.), and

Whereas, the notion that a living being changes into a different type of organism by passing through a birth canal is untenable, both logically and scientifically, and

Whereas, God's Word refers to life in the womb as having personal and human existence (e.g., Psalm 139:15, 16) and metes out capital punishment for causing the death of an unborn child, confirming its status as a human being created in the image of God (Exodus 21:22-25), and

Whereas, the oft-cited cases of rape and incest do not justify the practice of abortion, since the unborn child bears no responsibility for those sins and is the one who is punished by losing his life through abortion, though innocent, which is a gross injustice and which adds sin to sin, and

Whereas, there are no such things as "unwanted children," adoption agencies having waiting lists of years consisting of couples who desperately want children and would gladly take those who are being aborted, and

Whereas, the support services for pregnant women, including the unwed, are so prolific as to render any woman contemplating an abortion utterly unjustified, if not dishonest, in basing her decision on a lack of support or ability to raise the child, and

Whereas, since Roe v. Whereas, according to statistics available to the public, in the state of New York there are now more abortions than live births, and

# GA29 O3 — Amend BCO 20-2 to Include Senior Pastor on Assistant Pastor Search Committee

**Assembly:** 29th (2001)  ·  **Source:** Susquehanna Valley Presbytery: Amend BCO 20-2 to Include Pastor in Search Committee for As  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga29_2001 p. 141](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p141)*

---

It is the opinion of the CCB that Overture #3 is in conflict with the other parts of the Constitution. Teaching Elders are not members of the local congregation and therefore cannot be a member of the congregation’s pulpit committee. The proposed mandate of the overture that they be so would interfere with the right of the congregation to determine those who will rule over them, a privilege which undergirds BCO 20. Having said that, the Committee does recognize the wisdom of consulting with the senior pastor in the selection of an Assistant or Associate Pastor. *Adopted by CCB*

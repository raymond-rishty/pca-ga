# GA7 O9 — Study Electing a Moderator-in-Nomination and Appointing Skilled Presiding Officers

**Assembly:** 7th (1979)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of the Western Carolinas Whereas, the office of Moderator of the General As

*Source: [ga07_1979 PDF p. 30](../markdown/ga07_1979.md#ga07-p30)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the office of Moderator of the General Assembly is one of complexity, requiring skills for which the one elected should prepare himself; and

Whereas, it is not deemed appropriate in the Presbyterian Church in America that one should "run" for office, but rather be elected as the Lord seems to lead at the appropriate time; and

Whereas, it is possible to impose a severe burden upon a Moderator elected to office but unprepared to serve.

Now therefore, the Presbytery of Western Carolinas does overture the Seventh General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America to take under advisement the possibility of, 1) electing a "Moderator-in-Nomination" who shall have a year to prepare himself to serve; and 2) electing or appointing through an appropriate committee one or more Presiding Officers skilled in moderatorial procedures who may be called upon to take the chair at the pleasure of the Moderator or of the General Assembly. Adopted, unanimously, by the Presbytery of the Western Carolinas, November 18, 1978. A. Boyce Spooner, Stated Clerk Answered in the negative § 7-90, 12; p. 138.

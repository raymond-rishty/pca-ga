# GA29 O38 — Erect a New Presbytery from Remaining Westminster Presbytery Churches

**Assembly:** 29th (2001)  ·  **Source:** Westminster Presbytery (to MNA) "Erect New Presbytery"

*Source: [ga29_2001 p. 183](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p183)*

---

## Submitted overture

“Erect New Presbytery” westminster presbytery hereby notifies the General Assembly of its intention to withdraw from the Presbyterian Church in America, effective at the January 2002 stated meeting of Presbytery; and the General Assembly be overtured to take the following action: That the General Assembly, under the powers granted to it under the PCA Constitution to erect new presbyteries and to unite and divide presbyteries per their request, hereby established a new presbytery, in accordance with the following criteria: those churches and ministers which have certified to the stated clerk of Westminster Presbytery by the January 2002 stated meeting of said presbytery that they wish to remain in Westminster Presbytery, shall be constituted as Westminster Presbytery; while those churches and ministers which have not certified to the stated clerk of westminster presbytery by the january 2002 stated meeting of said presbytery that they wish to remain in westminster Presbytery, shall be constituted as a new Presbytery, effective that date, with the same boundaries as the present Westminster Presbytery. Adopted by Westminster Presbytery on May 15, 2001. Attested by: Is/ D. Steven Meyerhoff, Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">MNA recommends an affirmative answer</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
24. That Overture 38 from the Westminster Presbytery be answered in the affirmative; and further,<br>
<br>
That the 29th General Assembly declare that the New Presbytery described in the Overture 38 be constituted as follows: Its geographical boundaries shall be the same as those of the current Westminster Presbytery.<br>
<br>
2. The organizing meeting of the new Presbytery shall be held on the date of the January stated meeting of the current Westminster Presbytery, in the year of 2002. The Stated Clerk of the current Presbytery shall notify all churches in the current presbytery of the time and location of that meeting.<br>
<br>
3. The name of the New Presbytery, election of the committees, standing rules and all other operational procedures of the New Presbytery shall be established by the presbytery at the January 2002 meeting, or at later more opportune times, as determined by the New Presbytery at its January 2002 meeting.<br>
<br>
4. All those churches and teaching elders who are currently member of the Westminster Presbytery shall be enrolled as members of the New Presbytery, unless they have certified to the Stated Clerk of the current Westminster Presbytery that they wish to remain in Westminster Presbytery.<br>
<br>
5. The current Stated Clerk of Westminster Presbytery, TE Steve Meyerhoff, shall convene the January 2002 meeting of the New Presbytery, and preside until the election of a Moderator.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — ga29_2001 minutes, printed p. 555 (PDF p. 557), Appendix H, Supplemental Report of the Committee on Mission to North America, Recommendation 24](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p557)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Committee of Commissioners offers a substitute response</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Finally, this Assembly answers Overture 38 in the negative, on the grounds that:<br>
<br>
1. this Assembly cannot countenance the action which gave rise to the request, without expressing our deepest concern for the integrity of the Presbytery in so acting, and sustaining a hope that our counsels may well be heeded by our brothers;<br>
<br>
2. as a complaint against Presbytery&#x27;s action to withdraw has not yet been heard, this Assembly should not act to erect a new Presbytery until all judicial remedies of dissenting members of Presbytery have been exhausted;<br>
<br>
3. that the overture improperly and imprudently contemplates an action of the General Assembly of the PCA as being qualified by the actions of an anticipated ecclesiastical body with which the Assembly will have no relations; and<br>
<br>
4. that at this time there are no churches or teaching elders qualified to make a request for admission to the PCA as a presbytery.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — ga29_2001 minutes, printed p. 180 (PDF p. 182), 29-38, proposed substitute response to Communication 5 and Overture 38](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p182)*

# GA29 O27 — Amend BCO 12-5 to Prohibit Women from Preaching

**Assembly:** 29th (2001)  ·  **Source:** Presbytery of Philadelphia: Amend BCO 12-5e to Prohibit Women from Preaching It is the opi  ·  **Disposition:** Answered in the negative

*Source: [ga29_2001 p. 144](../markdown/ga29_2001.md#ga29-p144)*

---

It is the opinion of the CCB that Overture 27 is unclear with regard to whether the Presbytery really intended to delete BCO 12-5.d and how the proposed BCO 12-5.e changes fit into the chapter. We therefore cannot comment on the constitutional consistency of the proposed changes and thus we recommend that Overture 27 be remanded to the Presbytery for clarification. Adopted by CCB

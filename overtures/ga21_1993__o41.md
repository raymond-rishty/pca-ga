# GA21 O41 — Exhort the Southern Baptist Convention to Maintain Its Stand Against Abortion

**Assembly:** 21st (1993)  ·  **Source:** OVERTURE 41 From the Presbytery of South Texas

*Source: [ga21_1993 PDF p. 163](../markdown/ga21_1993.md#ga21-p163)*

---

## Submitted overture

"Exhort SBC to Continue Strong Stand Against Abortion"

Whereas, the Bible declares that life begins at conception and therefore, that abortion is murder,

Whereas, the present administration of the federal government supports pro abortion positions and practices;

Whereas, the Southern Baptist Convention has on numerous prior occasions taken a stance in opposition to abortion; Therefore Let it Be Resolved that the General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in America exhorts our brothers in the Southern Baptist Convention to continue to take a Scriptural position concerning the value of human life and the atrocity of abortion and the responsibility of all Christian leaders to support human life issues. Adopted by the Presbytery of South Texas on April 24,1993. Attested by: /s/ Irvin M. May, Jr. Stated Clerk

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">CCB advice on Overtures 7 and 19</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
Overtures 7 &amp; 19 - from Calvary and Missouri Presbyteries &quot;Amend BCO 46-5, Re: OPC Wording&quot; Response: It is the advice of the CCB that the proposed amendment to BCO 46-5 is not in conflict with the Constitution of the PCA and that the proposed amendment be referred to the Bills and Overtures Committee. It is noted that several new terms or concepts will be created by these Overtures, namely, a &quot;certificate of standing,&quot; &quot;erasure,&quot; and &quot;erase.&quot; It is further noted that this amendment would extend the inactive period from the current one (1) year to two (2) years.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt · 24th GA minutes, printed p. 162 (PDF p. 163), CCB advice on Overtures 7 and 19](../markdown/ga24_1996.md#ga24-pdf-p163)*

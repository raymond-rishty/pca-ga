# GA52 O23 — Amend BCO 41-3 to Allow Supplemental Judges for a Session Trial

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** the Pacific Northwest Presbytery (to CCB, OC)

*Source: [ga52_2025 PDF p. 1234](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-pdf-p1234)*

---

## Submitted overture

Be it resolved that the 52nd GA approve the amendment to BCO 43-1 shown below (underlining for additions; <del>strikethrough</del> for deletions):

BCO 41-3. In making a reference the lower court may ask for advice
only, or for final disposition of the matter referred; and in particular it
may refer a judicial case with a request <del>for its trial and decision by the</del>
<del>higher court</del>.:

1232

- **a.** That the higher court take original jurisdiction of the case, its trial, judgment, and whatever censure it deems warranted; or

- **b.** That the higher court provide additional teaching and/or ruling elders to serve with the lower court in a judicial case, as may seem prudent to the lower court, which additional elders, during their term of service on the case, shall have all the rights and responsibilities of judges in the lower court, subject to the rights of the parties under BCO 32-16.

# GA12 O2 — Amend BCO 21-1 by Striking the Word "Ordinarily"

**Assembly:** 12th (1984)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Oklahoma Whereas, the word "ordinarily" as used in the Book of Church Or

*Source: [ga12_1984 PDF p. 48](../markdown/ga12_1984.md#ga12-pdf-p48)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the word “ordinarily” a suse dint he Book of Church Order chapter 21, Section I carries great ambiguity, and

Whereas, the Oklahoma Presbytery is in doubt as to its application,

Therefore, be it resolved that the Oklahom a Presbytery respectfully overtures the Twelfth GeneralAssembly to strike the word “ordinarily” from the Book of Church Order chapter 21, Section 1. at tes t: paul E. de long , stated clerk Answered in the negative, 12-53, II, 27, p. 133.

## Action history

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Committee on Judicial Business recommends referral and conditional amendment language</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
16. That Overture 2 from Oklahoma Presbytery regarding BCO 21-1 be found in order and referred to the Committee of Commissioners on Judicial Business. The committee also recommends, if the General Assembly shall determine to answer this overture in the affirmative, that the paragraph be amended by adding, at the end of the second sentence, the following words: &quot;unless explicit Presbytery approval is given.&quot; NOTE: The amended sentence would therefore read: &quot;An ordained minister from another Presbytery or denomination shall not move on to the field until received by the Presbytery unless explicit Presbytery approval is given.&quot;
</blockquote>

# GA1 O7 — Relocate Administrative and Committee Headquarters Outside Atlanta

**Assembly:** 1st (1973)  ·  **Source:** Covenant Presbytery

*Source: [ga01_1973 p. 23](../markdown/ga01_1973.md#ga01-p23)*

---

## Submitted overture

That the administrative and committee headquarters of the Continuing Presbyterian Church be located anywhere within our geographical bounds OTHER THAN Atlanta, Georgia, (emphasis that of presbytery) area. To the Committee on Christian Education and Publications:

# GA11 O36 — Remove West Texas Counties from Texas Presbytery to Allow New Presbytery Formation

**Assembly:** 11th (1983)  ·  **Source:** the Presbytery of Texas

*Source: [ga11_1983 PDF p. 51](../markdown/ga11_1983.md#ga11-pdf-p51)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, by action taken at the regularly Stated Meeting of the Presbytery of Texas on April 29, 1983, we hereby overture the General Assembly to do the following: 'Request Assembly to remove West Texas from Texas Presbytery, along a line west of Wichita Falls, east of Abilene and San Angelo, west of Kerrville, and west of Del Rio. This will allow (1) Texas Presbytery to concentrate its efforts in a geographic area it can reasonably expect to reach, and (2) Allow the development of churches and a presbytery in West Texas before the Texas Presbytery or its successors can reasonably accomplish it, through the Assembly.' Attest: GLEN MILHAM, STATED CLERK Answered in the affirmative §11-61, III, 21, p. 125.

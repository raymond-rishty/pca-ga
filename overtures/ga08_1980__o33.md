# GA8 O33 — Invite the RPCES to join the PCA

**Assembly:** 8th (1980)  ·  **Source:** Mid-Atlantic Presbytery

*Source: [ga08_1980 p. 38](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga08-p38)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, the Reformed Presbyterian Church, Evangelical Synod is a sister of the Presbyterian Church in America, and

Whereas, there is a need today fora united presbyterian voice for the historic christian faith, a viable alternative to theologically liberal Presbyterianism on the North American continent, and

Whereas, the Mid-Atlantic Presbytery is one of three PCA presbyteries most affected by our Lord's mandate to build His church within the same geographical boundaries of those within which the RPCES is at work, sometimes to the confusion of the world and even other parts of Christ's body. Therefore, the Mid-Atlantic Presbytery overtures the Eighth General Assembly to humbly extend an invitation to the Reformed Presbyterian Church, Evangelical Synod to join the Presbyterian Church in America, thereby creating one church, one united witness in the nation and to the world and one fellowship as true brethren in Christ. Furthermore, should the Eighth General Assembly act upon the above overture in the affirmative, that Mid-Atlantic Presbytery be authorized immediately to discuss with Delmarva Presbytery of the RPCES our mutual concerns and possible redrawing of boundaries should our presbyteries be joined. Adopted at the Spring meeting. May 9-10, 1980. Attest: Daniel B. Ice Stated Clerk Answered by letter of invitation. See § 8-43, IV, 5; p. 80ff. To the Committee of Commissioners on Judicial Business

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
5. That the Eighth General Assembly of the PCA reaffirm at this time that portion of its original letter from our First General Assembly to all Churches, which reads: “As this new member of the family of Churches of the Lord Jesus Christ comes into being, we necessarily profess the Biblical doctrine of the unity of all who are in Christ... We greet all believers in an affirmation of the bond of Christian brotherhood. We invite into ecclesiastical fellowship all who maintain our principles of faith and order.”

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
The Assembly resumed regular session and voted to adopt the amendment on the floor, by substituting the shortened form of the “Letter” for the longer form originally recommended by the Sub-committee on Interchurch Relations. The vote was 423 affirmative, 124 negative.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Eighth GA minutes, printed p. 82 (PDF p. 84), 8-43, IV, Recommendation 5 floor proceedings](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga8-p84)*

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">Assembly adds the constitutional-process paragraph and adopts the amended letter, 525–38</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
It is to be understood that this invitation is an action of the Eighth General Assembly, and to receive any denomination which responds to this invitation it should be stated that for the Presbyterian Church in America to consummate any such union it would have to go through the constitutional process of approval by subsequent General Assemblies and three-fourths of our presbyteries. In this constitutional process each denomination must be dealt with separately. The amended substitute for Recommendation No. 5 of the Committee of Commissioners on Interchurch Relations was then adopted by vote of 525 affirmative, 38 negative.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Eighth GA minutes, printed p. 82 (PDF p. 84), 8-43, IV, amended Recommendation 5](../markdown/ga08_1980.md#ga8-p84)*

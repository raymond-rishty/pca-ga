# GA52 O31 — Amend BCO 31-10 to Require Two-Thirds Vote of Entire Court to Suspend Officer

**Assembly:** 52nd (2025)  ·  **Source:** Houston Metro Presbytery (to CCB, OC)

*Source: [ga52_2025 PDF p. 1267](../markdown/ga52_2025.md#ga52-pdf-p1267)*

---

## Submitted overture

Be it resolved that BCO 31-10 is amended as follows (underlining for additions):

31-10. When a member of a church court is under process, all his
official functions may be administratively suspended at the court's
discretion; but this shall never be done in the way of censure, and this
requires a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the entire court, even if the
process is being conducted by a commission.

So that BCO 31-10 as amended would read:

31-10. When a member of a church court is under process, all his
official functions may be administratively suspended at the court's
discretion; but this shall never be done in the way of censure, and this
requires a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the court itself, even if the
process is being conducted by a commission.

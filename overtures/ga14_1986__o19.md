# GA14 O19 — Discontinue the Annual GA Memorial Service for Teaching Elders

**Assembly:** 14th (1986)  ·  **Source:** Westminster Presbytery Whereas, Scripture makes no distinction in regard to authority betw

*Source: [ga14_1986 PDF p. 44](../markdown/ga14_1986.md#ga14-pdf-p44)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, Scripture makes no distinction in regard to authority between Teaching and Ruling Elders; and

Whereas, the BCO 7-2 states that these “Elders jointly have the government and spiritual oversight of the Church, including teaching”; and

Whereas, the General Assembly consists of Teaching and Ruling Elders; and

Whereas, the General Assembly has made recognition of deceased Teaching Elders an annual practice, but has not recognized deceased Ruling Elders; and

Whereas, deceased Ruling Elders and Teaching Elders are recognized by their presbyteries,

Therefore be it resolved that the General Assembly cease its annual memorial service for Teaching Elders. Adopted at the Winter Stated Meeting of Westminster Presbytery, January 18, 1986. Attest: Larry E. Ball, Westminster Presbytery Answered in the affirmative, 14-74, III, 12, p. 171.

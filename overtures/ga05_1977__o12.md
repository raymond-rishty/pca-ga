# GA5 O12 — Appoint Committee to Incorporate Divorce Conditions into the BCO

**Assembly:** 5th (1977)  ·  **Source:** North Georgia Presbytery to the Fifth General Assembly

*Source: [ga05_1977 PDF p. 16](../markdown/ga05_1977.md#ga5-pdf-p16)*

---

## Submitted overture

Whereas, God’s Word enjoins His people to recognize the legitimacy of divorce on the grounds of adultery, or desertion by an unbelieving partner only,

Whereas, our civil authorities have taken it upon themselves to recognize other than Scriptural grounds for divorce,

Therefore, be it resolved that the General Assembly appoint a committee to further investigate the practice of divorce and remarriage, both within and without the PCA, to the end that specific conditions of divorce be incorporated in the Book of Church Order, as may be most conformable to the Word of God.

<h3 class="overture-action-heading">A motion seeks to reopen the divorce action; no vote is recorded</h3>

<blockquote class="overture-action-text">
The Rev. Ronald L. Bossom was permitted by the moderator to present a special resolution calling for reconsideration of the previous night&#x27;s action on divorce and remarriage as follows: That the General Assembly re-open the issue of marriage and divorce as it was decided last night for the single purpose of hearing and acting upon a clarifying statement which would explain the force and effect of the action taken by the Assembly in relation to the Constitution of the Church. The Stated Clerk, in response to this report, set forth the following points regarding the binding character of the different kinds of actions of the Assembly:<br>
<br>
1. Constitutional changes made by the Assembly, in accord with Chapter 27 of the Book of Church Order are binding upon the whole Church.<br>
<br>
2. Judicial cases, when adjudicated by the Assembly, are binding regarding the matter thus settled.<br>
<br>
3. Review and Control of Presbyteries and of Committees are binding upon the particular Presbytery or Committee.<br>
<br>
4. Adoption of reports on particular subjects give the consensus of an Assembly&#x27;s opinion on that subject. Such opinions by our Assembly serve as a guideline or pious advice to our constituency on the subject. As such, they should be reported, studied, and taken seriously. They are not binding on the consciences of sessions or individuals who believe on Biblical grounds that another position is preferable. Discussion was terminated by a call for the order of the day.
</blockquote>

*Source: [Excerpt — Seventh GA minutes, printed pp. 111–112 (PDF pp. 113–114), 7-48, Special Resolution](../markdown/ga07_1979.md#ga7-pdf-p113)*

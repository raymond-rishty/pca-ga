About this edition

PCA General Assembly Minutes

An independent research edition of the PCA’s published Minutes of the General Assembly, prepared to help readers find, read, and cite the recorded source material.

What is here

The corpus includes cleaned, OCR-corrected text of the published minutes, with structured catalogues for judicial cases, overtures, constitutional inquiries, Review of Presbytery Records exceptions, and studies.

How to use it

Use a catalogue as a finding aid, then follow its link to the verbatim minutes. Record type and procedural status matter: an adopted action, a judicial decision, CCB advice, an RPR exception, and a study report do not all say the same kind of thing.

What it does not replace

The corpus is a research tool, not a substitute for the current PCA Constitution or the official published record. Check the printed-page source before relying on a quotation.

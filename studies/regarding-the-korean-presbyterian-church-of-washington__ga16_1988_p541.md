## Full text

> ## REPORT OF THE AD HOC COMMITTEE REGARDING THE KOREAN PRESBYTERIAN CHURCH OF WASHINGTON
>
> The Fifteenth General Assembly, through the Stated Clerk, named a committee to look into the conduct of some business in the Korean Presbyterian Church of Washington, D.C.
>
> The committee met on May 10, 1988, in Randallstown, Maryland. Committee members present included RE Robert Bain, RE Charles Baker, TE Michael Coleman, and TE Daniel Osborne. TE Gary Nantt translated the records and had input to the committee by correspondence but could not be present for this meeting.
>
> After reviewing the records, the committee found the Korean Southeastern Presbytery and the Session of Korean Presbyterian Church to have acted in order. Records indicate that there were reasons given for the discipline handed down in the congregation. They also show that Presbytery met with the dissident church members, but the members were not cooperative.
>
> Therefore, the committee is unanimous in this assessment and does not recommend that General Assembly set up a judicial commission to further investigate.
>
> Respectfully submitted,
>
> Daniel G. Osborne, Chairman
